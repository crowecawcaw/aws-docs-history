# Deleting your AWS DeepRacer Student account

The AWS DeepRacer Student portal stores the following information in your AWS Player account:

- Email address
- Your password
- Your racer name
- Your Student League leaderboard rank
  To learn more about the data collected, see [Data protection in AWS DeepRacer Student](data-protection.md "data-protection.md") in the _AWS DeepRacer Student User Guide_.

If you want to remove this information from AWS's servers, use the following procedure to delete your AWS DeepRacer Student portal account. Deleting your AWS DeepRacer Student account also deletes your AWS Player account and all associated resources.

To learn more about AWS Player accounts, see [What are AWS Player accounts?](setting-up.md "setting-up.md")

###### Note

If you have created AWS DeepRacer multi-user event, you cannot delete your AWS Player account. For more details, see [I can't delete my AWS DeepRacer Student account](troubleshooting.md#sl-troubleshooting-account-deletion "troubleshooting.md#sl-troubleshooting-account-deletion") in the _AWS DeepRacer Student User Guide_.

###### To delete your AWS DeepRacer Student player account

###### Important

Deleting your AWS DeepRacer Student account is an action that cannot be undone. When you delete your
AWS DeepRacer Student you are also deleting your AWS Player account and all associated resources.

When you delete your AWS DeepRacer Student account, the resources in your AWS Player account are removed from our servers within one year.

1. Open the AWS DeepRacer Student landing page: [https://student.deepracer.com/signIn](https://student.deepracer.com/ "https://student.deepracer.com/").
2. If prompted, log in to your AWS DeepRacer Student account.
3. Choose **Your account**.
4. On the **Your account** page, choose **Delete your account**.
5. Under **To confirm deletion, type Delete in the field,** type `Delete`.
6. Choose **Delete**.
   When your account is deleted successfully, the message **Account deleted successfully** appears and you are returned to the AWS DeepRacer Student login page.

If you would also like to delete your AWS account, use the steps outlined in [Closing your AWS account](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/knowledge-center/close-aws-account/ "https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/knowledge-center/close-aws-account/").
