# Contacting the Service Management

Connector specialist team

You can contact the AWS Service Management Connector (SMC)
specialist team directly from the connector using an Support case.

###### To create a support case with the SMC specialist team from Support

console

1. In the console, choose **Technical Support**.
2. Complete the form's required fields:
   - Service - `Service Catalog`
   - Category - `Service Management Connectors`
   - Severity - `General Guidence` or `System
Impaired` (based on your need).
   - Subject - A brief summary of the question or issue; include
     the name of the Connector in use.
   - Description - A detailed account of the question or issue.

3. In **Console Options**, choose
   **Web**.
4. Choose **Submit**. A SMC specialist team member
   will contact you through the support case.
