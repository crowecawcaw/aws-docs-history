# Installing Jira Service

Management Connector add-on

Follow these steps to install the Jira Service Management Connector add-on.

1. Log in to your Jira instance as an admin.
2. From the admin menu, choose **Add-ons**.
3. On the **Manage add-ons** screen, choose
   **Find new apps** or **Find new
   add-ons** from the left side of the page.
4. Find **AWS Service Management Connector
   for JSM**. The search results should include app versions
   compatible with your Jira instance.
5. Choose **Install** to download and install your
   app.
6. Proceed to **Configuring AWS Accounts and
   Regions**.
   Alternatively, download the [AWS Service Management Connector for Jira Service Management](https://servicecatalogconnector.s3.amazonaws.com/jira-servicedesk-connector-2.0.8-20240610-0810.obr "https://servicecatalogconnector.s3.amazonaws.com/jira-servicedesk-connector-2.0.8-20240610-0810.obr") file.

7. Go to **Manage apps**.
8. Select **Upload app** and upload the OBR
   file.
9. Proceed to **Configuring AWS Accounts and
   Regions**.
   You can apply the Connector for Jira Service Management add-on to
   the supported Jira software (Jira Service Management) releases noted
   above.
