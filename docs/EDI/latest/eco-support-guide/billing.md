# Submit EDI billing questions

To submit a billing-related question:

1. Open the [Support Center Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/").
2. Choose **Account & billing**.

![ECO billing graphic.](images/ecoBilling.png) 3. Choose **Create case**.
