# ECO backup monitoring and reporting

ECO generates daily self-service reports and monthly reports on resource coverage and backup job status. Your ESDM shares the monthly reports are
in Monthly Business Reviews (MBRs). To learn more about daily backup reports, see
[Backup report (daily)](../../../managedservices/latest/accelerate-guide/daily-backup-report.md "../../../managedservices/latest/accelerate-guide/daily-backup-report.md")
in the _AMS Accelerate User Guide_.

ECO experts monitor all your backup tasks. If a backup fails, ECO investigates the failure and informs you of the root
cause and remediation options, if available.
