## Brand voices

In addition to the available voices listed in the previous table, you can use Amazon Polly to
build a custom voice for your brand persona. With a brand voice, you can offer unique
and exclusive voices to your customers. To learn more about Amazon Polly brand
voices, see [Brand
Voice](https://aws.amazon.com/polly/features/#Brand_Voice "https://aws.amazon.com/polly/features/#Brand_Voice").
