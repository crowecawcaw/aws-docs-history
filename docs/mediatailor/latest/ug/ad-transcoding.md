# Ad transcoding in AWS Elemental MediaTailor

AWS Elemental MediaTailor transcodes ads, bumpers, and slate to match the format and specifications of
your origin video streams, ensuring seamless playback during ad breaks. MediaTailor supports two
transcoding approaches: dynamic transcoding (the default) and a custom transcode profile.
Understanding the differences between these approaches will help you determine the right
configuration for your workflows.
