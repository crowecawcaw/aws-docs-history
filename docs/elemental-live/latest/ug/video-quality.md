# Controlling video quality

You can tune video quality in each output in an Elemental Live event. You
tune the video quality by setting parameters that are in the output section of
the event: in the relevant output group, choose an
**Output**, choose a **Stream**, choose the
**Video** tab, then open the **Advanced**
section.

The list also specifies the section in this guide that describes the
parameter.
