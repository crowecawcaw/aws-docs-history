# View export history

Select the **Export history** tab to view the files
exported in the last 7 days, including their status, progress, and the task’s start and end
time.

You can change the settings according to your preferences by clicking on the settings
icon located in the right-hand corner of the screen.

To see all the related task messages, click the task ID. To copy the messages, click
**Copy**.
