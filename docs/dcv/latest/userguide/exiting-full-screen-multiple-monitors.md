

# Exiting full screen on multiple monitors
<a name="exiting-full-screen-multiple-monitors"></a>

To exit full screen on multiple monitors, do one of the following depending on your client.

------
#### [ Windows ]

1. After you extend the displays or enter full screen mode, a tab appears at the top-center edge of the screen.

1. Choose the tab and then select **Exit fullscreen**.

------
#### [ macOS ]

1. After you extend the displays or enter full screen mode, the toolbar appears at the top of the primary monitor. The toolbar auto-hides after 5 seconds. Move the mouse to the top edge of the monitor to reveal it.

1. Choose the green zoom button on the toolbar.  
![Toolbar showing the green zoom button to exit full screen.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/dcv/latest/userguide/images/mac-exit-full-screen.png)

------
#### [ Linux ]

1. After you extend the displays or enter full screen mode, a toolbar appears at the top of the screen. Move the mouse to the top edge of the screen to reveal it.

1. Choose the **Leave full screen** button on the toolbar.

------

**Note**  
By default, DCV will save your display settings. If DCV detects a different monitor configuration, the display settings will be reset.
