| [Rewrite of the close account topic](manage-acct-closing.md "manage-acct-closing.md")                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Completely overhauled the entire close account topic including adding steps for how to close member and management accounts.                                                                                                                                                                                              | February 1, 2024   |
