

# Remove a spend limit in AWS Settings
<a name="remove-spend-limit"></a>

**Note**

[Compare sign-up options](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/accounts/latest/reference/sign-up-for-aws.html#compare-sign-up-options).

The project owner can remove a spend limit in AWS Settings at any time. When you remove a spend limit, AWS will no longer monitor your costs or take any early cost controls on your project. For more information about how spend limits work, see [Create a spend limit in AWS Settings](create-spend-limit.md).

## Remove a spend limit in AWS Settings
<a name="remove-spend-limit-procedure"></a>

**To remove a spend limit**

1. Open AWS Settings at [https://settings.aws.com](https://settings.aws.com).

1. In the main navigation pane, choose **Billing**.

1. In **Cost by project**, for **Spend limit**, choose the pencil icon.

1. Choose **Remove limit**.

1. Confirm your choice and choose **Remove**.

Your spend limit will be removed immediately.
