# Managed Dashboards

Managed Dashboards are preconfigured, read-only dashboards authored and maintained by AWS
that appear automatically in your dashboard list. They are populated with your account data and
provide immediate visibility into your cost trends, service costs, and commitment performance
without requiring any setup. These dashboards are built on common customer reporting patterns and
FinOps best practices, combining widget types and configurations that address the most frequently
needed cost analysis scenarios.
