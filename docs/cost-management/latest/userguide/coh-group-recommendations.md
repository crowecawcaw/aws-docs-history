# Grouping related recommendations

Use the following procedure to view related recommendations and their estimated
savings.

1. On the **Savings opportunities** page, choose **Group related
   recommendations**.
2. Choose a row in the table.

This opens a split-view panel with a choice of recommended actions for your chosen
resource type. 3. Under **Recommended actions**, select one of the recommended
actions.

This updates the recommended action details on the left-hand side and the estimated
savings on the right. 4. Based on the recommended action, you can choose to view the recommendation in the
AWS Billing and Cost Management console, or you can open it in AWS Compute Optimizer
or the relevant console.
