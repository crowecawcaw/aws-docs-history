# Troubleshooting

Bluetooth pairing

You may find yourself attempting to pair your iOS mobile device with a gateway
that it has already paired with. This could happen because the gateway has changed
locations, or because the general configuration of your Amazon Monitron site has
been altered.

In that case, tell your iOS device to "forget" its Bluetooth connection with the
gateway.

###### Topics

- [To unpair a gateway from your
  device](#unpair-gateway-ethernet "#unpair-gateway-ethernet")

## To unpair a gateway from your

device

1. On your iOS device, choose **Settings**.
2. On your **Settings** screen, choose
   **Bluetooth**.
3. On the **Bluetooth** screen, choose the information
   icon next to the name of your Amazon Monitron Gateway.
4. On the next screen, choose **Forget This
   Device**.
