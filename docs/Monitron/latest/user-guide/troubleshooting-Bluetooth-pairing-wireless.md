# Troubleshooting

Bluetooth pairing

You may find yourself attempting to pair your iOS mobile device with a gateway
that it has already been paired with. This could happen because the gateway has
changed locations, or because you have altered the general configuration of your
Monitron site.

In that case, tell your iOS device to "forget" its Bluetooth connection with the
gateway.

###### Topics

- [To unpair a
  gateway from your device](#troubleshooting-Bluetooth-pairing-wireless-unpair "#troubleshooting-Bluetooth-pairing-wireless-unpair")

## To unpair a

gateway from your device

1. On your iOS device, choose **Settings**.
2. On your **Settings** screen, choose
   **Bluetooth**.
3. On the **Bluetooth** screen, choose the information
   icon next to the name of your Monitron Gateway.
4. On the next screen, choose **Forget This
   Device**.
