

# Troubleshooting
<a name="troubleshooting"></a>

This chapter collects the recurring issues that surface most often during Agentic Vehicle Experience (AVX) deployment and operation, each drawn from the AVX development history. For every entry the symptom is described as it appears in logs or client behavior, the root cause is identified, and a prescriptive resolution is provided. The AgentCore operational constraints documented in [Amazon Bedrock AgentCore runtime](agentcore-runtime.md) and [Amazon Nova 2 Sonic speech-to-speech voice](nova-sonic-voice.md) are the platform properties that motivate several entries here; they are not bugs to fix but boundaries to design around.
