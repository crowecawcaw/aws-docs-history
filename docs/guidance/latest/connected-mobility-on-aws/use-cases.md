# Use cases

**Fleet Operations Management** - Commercial fleet operators can digitize their operations, track vehicle locations in real-time, monitor driver performance, and optimize routes for improved efficiency and reduced operational costs.

**Predictive Maintenance** - Automotive service providers can analyze vehicle diagnostic data to predict maintenance needs, schedule proactive service appointments, and reduce unexpected breakdowns through early detection of potential issues.

**Safety & Compliance Monitoring** - Transportation companies can monitor driver behavior in real-time, detect safety violations, generate compliance reports, and implement automated safety alerts to reduce accidents and insurance costs.

**Electric Vehicle Fleet Management** - EV fleet operators can monitor battery health, optimize charging schedules, track energy consumption, and manage charging infrastructure to maximize vehicle uptime and minimize operational costs.

**Development and Testing** - Use the integrated fleet simulator to generate realistic telemetry data for testing stream processing applications, validating analytics pipelines, and demonstrating capabilities without physical vehicles.
