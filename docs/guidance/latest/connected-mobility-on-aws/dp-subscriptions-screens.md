

# Subscriptions in the Fleet Intelligence portal
<a name="dp-subscriptions-screens"></a>

The **Data Products** section of the Fleet Intelligence portal shows the data products a fleet consumes. The Subscriptions page totals active subscriptions, enrolled vehicles against capacity, streaming feeds and estimated monthly cost, and lists each subscription with its producer, tier, status, vehicle count, feed health, last data and latest invoice.

![Subscriptions page: totals for active subscriptions](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/guidance/latest/connected-mobility-on-aws/images/fm-subscriptions.png)


A subscription’s detail page shows its producer, tier, status, contract dates, entitled signals and vehicle count, the fleets it serves, and its feed health.

![Subscription detail page for a Meridian Premium subscription: an overview with producer](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/guidance/latest/connected-mobility-on-aws/images/fm-subscription-detail.png)
