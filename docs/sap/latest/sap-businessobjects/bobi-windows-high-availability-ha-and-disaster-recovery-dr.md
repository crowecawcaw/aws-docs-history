# High Availability (HA) and Disaster Recovery (DR)

If you require a highly available BOBI environment, then it critical to design the HA and DR environment that can support the recovery time objective (RTO) and recovery point objective (RPO) that your business teams have established. For more information, see the [SAP BusinessObjects BI Platform on AWS: HA/DR Guide for Windows](sap-bobi-ha-dr-win.md "sap-bobi-ha-dr-win.md").
