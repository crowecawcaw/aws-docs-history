# Sample playbooks

A number of sample playbooks can be found in Appendix B in [Playbook resources](appendix-b-incident-response-resources.md#playbook-resources "appendix-b-incident-response-resources.md#playbook-resources").
The examples here can be used to guide you on what playbooks to create and what to include
in your playbooks. However, it’s important you craft playbooks that incorporate the risks
most relevant to your business. You need to verify that the steps and workflows within
your playbooks include your technologies and processes.
