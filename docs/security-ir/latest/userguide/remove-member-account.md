# Removing members from AWS Security Incident Response

To remove an account from your membership, you can remove a member account from your organization, move accounts out of your selected OUs,
or remove OUs from your membership.

To remove an account from your membership, follow the procedures for
[removing a member account from an organization](../../../organizations/latest/userguide/orgs_manage_accounts_remove.md "../../../organizations/latest/userguide/orgs_manage_accounts_remove.md").

To move accounts out of your OUs, follow the procedures for
[Moving accounts to an organizational unit (OU) or between the root and OUs with AWS Organizations](../../../organizations/latest/userguide/move_account_to_ou.md "../../../organizations/latest/userguide/move_account_to_ou.md").

To remove OU from your membership, follow the procedures for [Managing membership with organizational units (OUs)](managing-membership-with-ou.md "managing-membership-with-ou.md").
