# Identify appropriate log retention

When you use an S3 bucket or CloudWatch log group to store logs, you must establish adequate
lifecycles for each log source to optimize storage and retrieval costs. Customers generally
have between 3 and 12 months of logs readily available for querying, with retention of up
to seven years. The choice of availability and retention should align with your security
requirements and a composite of statutory, regulatory, and business mandates.
