# Starting the training process

The **Review and train** page gives you a chance to change some
of your settings before you start training your model.

- To review model details such as the name, the encryption key, or the AWS
  tags, see [Specifying model details](specifying-model-details.md "specifying-model-details.md").
- To review your input data configuration, which is where you (optionally)
  differentiated your training data from your evaluation data and set your
  sample rate and off time parameters, see Configuring your input data.
- To review data labels, see [Labeling your data](labeling-data.md "labeling-data.md")
  When you're ready to train your model, choose **Train
  model**.
