# Amazon Lookout for Equipment step by step

###### To use Amazon Lookout for Equipment to monitor your equipment, you must do the

following:

1. [Set up your account.](getting-started-brain.md "getting-started-brain.md")
2. [Create your project.](create-project.md "create-project.md")
3. [Format your data.](formatting-data.md "formatting-data.md")
4. [Add your dataset to your project.](ingest-dataset.md "ingest-dataset.md")
5. [Review the dataset ingestion.](understanding-ingestion-validation.md "understanding-ingestion-validation.md")
6. [Train your model.](create-model.md "create-model.md")
7. [Evaluate your model.](view-model.md "view-model.md")
8. [Schedule inference.](inference.md "inference.md")
9. [Review your inference results.](understanding-results.md "understanding-results.md")
   You repeat the preceding steps for each asset that you want to monitor.
