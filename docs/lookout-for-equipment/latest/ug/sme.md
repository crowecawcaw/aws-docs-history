# Consulting subject matter experts

Lookout for Equipment identifies patterns in the dataset that help to detect critical issues, but it's
the responsibility of a technician or subject matter expert (SME) to diagnose the
problem and take corrective action, if needed. To ensure that you are getting the right
output, we highly recommend that you work with a SME. The SME should help you make sure
that you are using the right input data and that your output results are actionable and
relevant.
