

# Edit a monitor in Performance Monitor

You can edit a monitor to add or remove the VPCs that it monitors.

**To edit a monitor**

1. Open the CloudWatch console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Network Monitoring**, choose **Performance monitors**.

1. Choose the monitor that you want to edit.

1. Choose **Edit**.

1. Make your changes, and then choose **Save**.
