

# Delete a monitor in Performance Monitor

**To delete a monitor**

1. Open the CloudWatch console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Network Monitoring**, choose **Performance monitors**.

1. Choose the monitor that you want to delete.

1. Choose **Delete**.

1. Confirm the deletion.

Deleting a monitor doesn't delete your scope or affect other monitors.
