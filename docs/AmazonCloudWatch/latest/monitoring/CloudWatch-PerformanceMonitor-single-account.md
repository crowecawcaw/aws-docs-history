

# Initialize Performance Monitor for a single account

If you're monitoring VPCs in a single AWS account, follow these steps.

**To initialize Performance Monitor for a single account**

1. Open the CloudWatch console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cloudwatch/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Network Monitoring**, choose **Performance monitors**.

1. Choose **Create Monitor**. Alternatively, to define your scope first, choose **Account Configuration**.

1. Review the service-linked role permissions, and then choose **Accept**.

Performance Monitor creates your scope and generates the initial topology. After initialization, you can view performance metrics in monitors for VPCs in your account.
