

# Distribute through AWS Marketplace
<a name="managed-entitlements-marketplace"></a>

When your management account or delegated administrator account accepts a private offer for a third-party model in Amazon Bedrock, you can distribute it from AWS Marketplace. You distribute on the purchase confirmation page, right after you accept the offer.

AWS sets up AWS License Manager, creates the grants, and activates them for every account in your organization. Member accounts don't need to take any action.

**Note**  
You can't distribute private offers that you accept as part of an offer set. An offer set is a package of two to seven private offers that a seller groups together so that you can review and accept them with a single action. Each offer in the set keeps its own pricing and terms, and each product creates its own agreement. For more information, see [Understanding offer sets](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/buyerguide/understanding-offer-sets.html) in the *AWS Marketplace Buyer Guide*.  
To distribute public offers or offer sets, or to distribute to specific accounts or organizational units (OUs), see [Distribute through AWS License Manager](managed-entitlements-license-manager.md).
