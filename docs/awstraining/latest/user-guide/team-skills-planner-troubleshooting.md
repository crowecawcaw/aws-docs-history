

# Troubleshooting

Common issues and resolutions:
+  **I can’t edit my goal** — Goals can’t be edited after a baseline starts.
+  **I can’t change my training plan** — Training plans can’t be edited after learners are assigned.
+  **Progress or insights look out of date** — Data refreshes about every 6 hours. Check the last-updated time. If activity is still missing after the next refresh, contact your AWS Training representative.
+  **Some job tasks show Not measured** — No respondent answered questions on those job tasks. Ask more people in that role to take the baseline before it closes.
+  **The PDF has no baseline results** — The baseline section is omitted when no baseline was run.
+  **A learner can’t access assigned training** — Confirm the learner has the required subscription, seat, or training access for that item.
+  **I don’t see Team Skills Planner** — Confirm your organization has an AWS Skill Builder Team Subscription and that you’re a Learning Administrator, or contact your AWS Training representative.
