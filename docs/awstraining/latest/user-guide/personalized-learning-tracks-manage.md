

# Learning Administrators

If you’re a Learning Administrator, use the following information to give your team access to personalized learning tracks and view their activity.

 **How do I give my team members access to personalized learning tracks?** 

Assign a Team Subscription seat to each learner through your existing AWS Skill Builder Admin tools. Access to personalized learning tracks follows your organization’s standard subscription seat assignment process; no additional step is required beyond assigning a seat.

 **Where can I find team-wide personalized learning track activity?** 

In your Admin Reporting dashboard, choose the Personalized learning tracks tab.

 **What can I see as a Learning Administrator?** 

On the Personalized learning tracks tab of your Admin Reporting dashboard, you can see the following:
+  **Team-wide activity** — including status and progress of key performance indicators (KPIs), and an activity-over-time view.
+  **Individual-learner activity** — including a learner-level table with status, progress, lessons completed, milestones completed, and key dates. The learner-level table supports exports to CSV or Microsoft Excel files, and filters by organization, team, and date.

 **Can I see an individual’s personalized learning track?** 

Yes. From your Admin Reporting dashboard or an individual user detail page, choose the title of the user’s personalized learning track to open a read-only view.
