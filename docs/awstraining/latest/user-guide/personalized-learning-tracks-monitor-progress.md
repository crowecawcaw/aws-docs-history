

# Monitor progress

You can see the progress of your personalized learning tracks in three places:
+  **Learner Dashboard** — Your saved personalized learning track appears as a card.
+  **Training activity page** — The Personalized learning tracks tab shows lessons reviewed, milestones completed, percent complete, and your last active date.
+  **Your personalized learning track’s page** — On the Training activity page, on the Personalized learning tracks tab, choose your learning track to see detailed milestone-by-milestone progress.

 **Will I get notifications about my progress?** 

Yes. You receive an email message when you save a new personalized learning track, and another one when you complete a personalized learning track.

 **Can my Learning Administrator see my progress?** 
+ Yes — If your organization assigned you a Team Subscription seat, your Learning Administrator can see your personalized learning track status and progress, including milestones and lessons completed with dates. They can also open a read-only view to see the lessons in your personalized learning track.
+ No — If you have an individual subscription, there is no Learning Administrator view to see your progress.
