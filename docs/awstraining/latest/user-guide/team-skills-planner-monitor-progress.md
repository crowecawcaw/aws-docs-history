

# Monitor progress
<a name="team-skills-planner-monitor-progress"></a>

Each goal’s page shows the number of learners, created date, status, and its roles, with each role’s progress, cohort end date, and whether any roles are at risk. Choose **Download recommendations** to download the training recommendation PDF. If no baseline has run, the page notes that the plan assumes a Beginner level.

 **Roles at risk** 

Team Skills Planner flags roles that might not finish their assigned training on time. A role is marked at risk when 25 percent or more of its assigned learners are at risk. The role shows how many learners are at risk, for example, **At risk · 4 of 12 learners**.

A learner is considered at risk if any of the following is true:
+  **Not started** — The learner hasn’t started their training more than seven days after it was assigned.
+  **Inactive** — The learner hasn’t been active in more than seven days.
+  **Behind pace** — The learner’s progress is more than five percentage points lower than expected for the time that has passed. Expected progress is based on the role’s start date and target completion date. For example, if 50 percent of the time has passed, a learner with less than 45 percent progress is behind pace. This applies only to roles with a target completion date, and only after the learner completes at least one item.

Only Learning Administrators with permission to view learner details can see which learners are at risk.

 **Progress insights** 

Monitor progress by goal and role, with catalog item completion at the team level. Charts show progress by domain and the trend of course completions per week. Charts and AI insights are team-level. Learning Administrators with permission to view learner details can see which learners are behind on assigned training.

Progress and insights data refresh about every 6 hours. Each view shows when it was last updated, so check that before you compare values.

 **Using the conversational assistant to explore insights** 

Ask the assistant about your goals, baselines, and training, or choose **Discuss insights** on any page to bring its summary into the conversation. For example, the assistant might note that one role is progressing faster than another and flag the risk if both must be ready at the same time.

Example questions to ask the assistant:
+ "Give me an overview of how the Application Modernization goal is progressing."
+ "Which roles are behind, and what should I do first?"
+ "What did the baseline tell us about our Solutions Architects?"
+ "What do you recommend I do to improve engagement for the Solutions Architect role?"
+ "Draft a short note I can send to the Solutions Architect team manager about where they stand."

Review any draft the assistant writes before you send it.

Don’t include personal information in your questions.
