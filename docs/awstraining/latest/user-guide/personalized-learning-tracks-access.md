

# Access

 **Who can use personalized learning tracks?** 

Anyone with an active AWS Skill Builder subscription—​either an individual subscription or a Team Subscription seat assigned by your organization.

 **My organization has a Team Subscription. Why don’t I see personalized learning tracks?** 

Your organization must assign you a Team Subscription seat. Check with your Learning Administrator if you believe you should have one.

 **Do I need to pay extra for it?** 

No. Personalized learning tracks are included with your subscription.

 **Can I use it if I don’t have a paid subscription?** 

No. To see what’s included and how to subscribe, see the [AWS Skill Builder Subscriptions page](https://skillbuilder.aws/subscriptions).
