

# Learner experience

What a learner sees depends on whether the Learning Administrator sends a baseline link or assigns training:
+  **Baseline** — Learners receive a link from their Learning Administrator. They don’t need a Skill Builder account. They enter an alias, choose their role, and answer a short, timed quiz on product knowledge. Learners don’t see individual scores.
+  **Assigned training** — Learners are notified by email and find the assigned training in their AWS Skill Builder account. The applicable subscription or training access still applies. For AWS Classroom Training in a cohort, learners get an email with details on how to join the session.
+  **Team Skills Planner itself** — Learners don’t have access to the planning experience, goals, or team results.
