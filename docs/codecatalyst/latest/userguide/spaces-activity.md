# Monitoring activity for users and resources in a space

To see recently created projects and status updates, you can use the CodeCatalyst console to
view an activity feed that shows updates for space resources.

In the activity feed, you can view metrics such as failed workflow runs and created
projects.

###### To view activity in your space

1. Open the CodeCatalyst console at [https://codecatalyst.aws/](https://codecatalyst.aws/ "https://codecatalyst.aws/").
2. Navigate to your CodeCatalyst space.

###### Tip

If you belong to more than one space, choose a space in the top navigation
bar. 3. Choose **Activity**. 4. View the information in **Activity**. 5. To filter by activity, choose the selector on the upper right. 6. To view all activity in your space, choose **Any activity
type**.
