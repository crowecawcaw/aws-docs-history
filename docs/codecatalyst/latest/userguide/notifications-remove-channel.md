# Removing a channel

You can remove a Slack channel from Amazon CodeCatalyst. By removing a Slack channel, notifications
about a chosen CodeCatalyst project will no longer be sent to the channel.

###### To remove a channel

1. Open the CodeCatalyst console at [https://codecatalyst.aws/](https://codecatalyst.aws/ "https://codecatalyst.aws/").
2. Navigate to your project. In the navigation pane, choose **Project
   settings**.
3. On the **Project settings** page, choose the
   **Notifications** tab.
4. Choose the indicator next to the channel you want to remove and then choose
   **Remove channel**. Choose **Ok** in
   the confirmation window.
