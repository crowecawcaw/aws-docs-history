# Editing notifications for a notification channel

You can change which channels notifications go to, and you can turn off specific
notifications altogether.

###### To edit notifications

1. Open the CodeCatalyst console at [https://codecatalyst.aws/](https://codecatalyst.aws/ "https://codecatalyst.aws/").
2. Navigate to your project.
3. In the navigation pane, choose **Project settings**.
4. Choose the **Notifications** tab.
5. Choose **Edit notifications**.
6. Do one of the following:
   - To send a notification to a specific channel, choose the channel from the
     drop-down list.
   - To turn off a notification globally, choose the toggle next to the
     notification.
   - To stop sending a notification to a specific channel, choose the
     **X** on the channel.

7. Choose **Save**.
