# Removing a project role for a team

In CodeCatalyst, you can view the project roles for your team. You can also view the members in
a team. You can remove the project role for a team.

###### To remove a project role

1. Open the CodeCatalyst console at [https://codecatalyst.aws/](https://codecatalyst.aws/ "https://codecatalyst.aws/").
2. Navigate to your space. Choose **Project settings**, and then
   choose **Teams**.
3. Choose the **Project roles** tab.
4. Choose the role you want to remove.

###### Important

Removing a role from a team removes the associated permissions for all users in the
team. 5. Choose **Save**.
