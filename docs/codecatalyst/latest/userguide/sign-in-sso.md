# Signing in with SSO

Follow these steps to use SSO to sign in to Amazon CodeCatalyst.

To sign in with your AWS Builder ID instead, see [Signing in with an AWS Builder ID](id-how-to-sign-in.md "id-how-to-sign-in.md").

###### To sign in with SSO

1. Open the CodeCatalyst console at [https://codecatalyst.aws/](https://codecatalyst.aws/ "https://codecatalyst.aws/").
2. Under **Choose a sign-in option**, choose **Use Single
   Sign-On (SSO)**.
3. In **AWS Identity Center application name**, enter the
   application name provided by your identity federation administrator.
4. Choose **Continue to IAM Identity Center**.
