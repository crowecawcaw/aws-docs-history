# Launch a new desktop

1. From the menu, choose **My Virtual Desktops**.
2. Choose **Launch New Virtual Desktop**.

![Virtual desktops](/images/res/latest/ug/images/res-virtualdesktops.png) 3. Enter the details for your new desktop. 4. Choose **Submit**.
A new card with your desktop information appears instantly, and your desktop will be
ready to use within 10-15 minutes. Startup time depends on the selected image. RES
detects GPU instances and installs the relevant drivers.
