# Turning subscription auto-renewal on or off in

AWS Data Exchange

Manage your subscription auto-renewals through the AWS Data Exchange console.

###### To turn subscription auto-renewal on or off

1. Open and sign in to the [AWS Data Exchange
   console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/dataexchange "https://console.aws.amazon.com/dataexchange").
2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Subscriptions**, and then choose
   your product.
3. On the product detail page, under **Renewal terms**, turn on
   **Auto-renewal** or turn off **Auto-renewal**.

A success message appears, confirming your updated renewal settings.
