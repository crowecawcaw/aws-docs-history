# Troubleshooting

updates to invoice units

You can use the information on this page to resolve common issues customers
encounter when updating invoice units.

## I want to update the

invoice receiver's email address

### Resolution

You can update the invoice receiver's email address using the following
method:

###### Update your legal information using Account Management

- Update the **alternate contact address** on your
  **Accounts** page. For detailed steps, see
  [Update your AWS account contact information](../../../accounts/latest/reference/manage-acct-update-contact.md#manage-acct-update-contact-alternate-edit.html "../../../accounts/latest/reference/manage-acct-update-contact.md#manage-acct-update-contact-alternate-edit.html") in the
  _AWS Account Management User Guide_.
