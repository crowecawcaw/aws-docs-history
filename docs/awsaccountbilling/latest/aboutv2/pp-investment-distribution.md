

# Understanding investment distribution
<a name="pp-investment-distribution"></a>

Investment distribution assigns a percentage of the total investment amount to each eligible investment account on your Private Pricing contract. You configure distribution percentages to control how much each investment account receives.
+ Each investment account receives a percentage of the total investment amount.
+ All percentages must sum to exactly 100%.
+ By default, the system applies equal distribution across all investment accounts (auto-calculated).
+ You can manually override the percentages on the attestation form.

## When distribution is required
<a name="pp-distribution-required"></a>

The number of investment accounts on your contract determines whether you must specify a distribution.
+ **Multiple investment accounts** — You can, optionally, specify a custom distribution when submitting an attestation.
+ **Single investment account** — Single account receives 100% of the investment.

## Adjusting distribution on the attestation form
<a name="pp-distribution-adjust"></a>

You can adjust the investment distribution percentages when you complete the attestation form for an outcome.

**To adjust investment distribution**

1. On the attestation form, locate the **Investment distribution** section for the outcome.

1. Review the table that displays each eligible investment account with a percentage field and a computed amount.

1. Adjust the percentage for each account as needed.

1. Verify that the percentages sum to 100%.

**Note**  
If the percentages do not sum to 100%, you cannot submit the attestation. The form displays an error message.

## Example
<a name="pp-distribution-example"></a>

The following example shows a contract with three investment accounts and a USD $30,000 investment disbursement:
+ Investment Account A: 50% = USD $15,000
+ Investment Account B: 30% = USD $9,000
+ Investment Account C: 20% = USD $6,000

The three percentages (50% \+ 30% \+ 20%) sum to 100%, so the full USD $30,000 investment is distributed across the accounts.
