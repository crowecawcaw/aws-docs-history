# How much does porting a number to

Amazon Connect cost?

Amazon Connect does not charge fees for porting numbers. Your existing carrier may have
fees associated with the disconnection and early termination of your
service.

After a phone number is ported to Amazon Connect, standard pricing applies for [Amazon Connect service usage and associated
telephony rates](https://aws.amazon.com/connect/pricing/ "https://aws.amazon.com/connect/pricing/").
