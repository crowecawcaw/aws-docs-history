# Set up your contact center in Amazon Connect

To get started, you [create an Amazon Connect
instance](amazon-connect-instances.md "amazon-connect-instances.md"), which is your a virtual contact center.

After you create an Amazon Connect instance, you can:

- [Test](chat-testing.md "chat-testing.md") the voice and chat experiences to learn
  how they work.
- [Set up your channels](set-channels.md "set-channels.md"). How do you want
  customers to reach your contact center?
  - [Claim a phone number](ag-overview-numbers.md "ag-overview-numbers.md") for your
    contact center or [port](about-porting.md "about-porting.md") your own phone
    number from another carrier.
  - [Set up the customer's chat
    experience](enable-chat-in-app.md "enable-chat-in-app.md"). Developer skills are required.
  - [Set up SMS (text)
    messaging](setup-sms-messaging.md "setup-sms-messaging.md").
  - [Set up in-app, web, and video calling](inapp-calling.md "inapp-calling.md")
    capabilities.
  - [Set up tasks](concepts-getting-started-tasks.md "concepts-getting-started-tasks.md").

- [Enable outbound calling](enable-outbound-calls.md "enable-outbound-calls.md"), and set your
  [outbound caller ID](queues-callerid.md "queues-callerid.md").
- [Add](user-management.md "user-management.md") agents and other users.
- Review this [list](security-profile-list.md "security-profile-list.md") of security profile
  permissions, and then [assign](assign-security-profile.md "assign-security-profile.md") security
  profile permissions to users so they can access those parts of Amazon Connect that are
  appropriate to their roles.
- [Set up routing](connect-queues.md "connect-queues.md"). You can create a single
  queue for incoming contacts, or set up multiple queues so that you can route
  contacts to agents with specific skills.
- Use the [default flows](contact-flow-default.md "contact-flow-default.md") that come with
  Amazon Connect or [create](create-contact-flow.md#create-inbound-contact-flow "create-contact-flow.md#create-inbound-contact-flow") your own flows to
  define how your customers experience your contact center.
- Provide your agents with [access](amazon-connect-contact-control-panel.md "amazon-connect-contact-control-panel.md") to the Contact Control Panel (CCP), which they will use to
  interact with contacts.
  The topics in this section explain how to do these steps.
