

# Region requirements for ordering and porting phone numbers in Connect Customer
<a name="phone-number-requirements"></a>

Country or region regulations often require a local office address and specific identification documents to purchase and own a phone number. The address that you provide can be the business or personal address where the phone numbers are used. 

For a list of the telephony capabilities that Connect Customer provides, see the [Connect Customer Telecoms Country Coverage Guide](https://d1v2gagwb6hfe1.cloudfront.net/Amazon_Connect_Telecoms_Coverage.pdf). This guide provides visibility of what regions are supported, and if there are any exceptions to being able to access a country from any commercial region.

Following is a list of ID Requirements by country or region for ordering and porting numbers. **All AWS Regions are supported—except Africa (Cape Town) and AWS GovCloud (US-West)—unless noted otherwise**. When you [ request an international number](number-request.md), we'll work with you to submit your documents. 

**Important**  
Addresses that can be claimed without presence, such as PO Box addresses, are not valid in any country.
After your numbers are ordered or ported, the exact number of requested phone numbers appear on the **Manage Phone numbers** page in the Connect Customer admin website for you to [manage](get-connect-number.md#get-connect-number1). You won't have access to all available numbers in that country.
