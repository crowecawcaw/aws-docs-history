

# Create Voluntary time off slots for agents in your contact center

1. Choose the **Make Request** button in the Published schedule calendar UI and select **VTO**.

1. A supervisor or manager enters the date and time range for voluntary time off.

1. Select the productive activity from the Activity list.

1. Choose **By Staffing group** or **By Staff**.
   + **By Staffing group** – Creates voluntary time off (VTO) slots and notifies all agents in the selected staffing groups that the slots are available. Agents accept or decline the slots in their schedule calendar, and slots are filled on a first come, first served basis. For more information, see [How contact center agents accept or decline a time off request](agent-accept-decline-vto.md).
   + **By Staff** – Applies VTO directly to the published schedules of the agents that you select. Agents don't receive an offer, and there is no accept or decline step.
**Important**  
When you choose **By Staff**, Connect Customer adds the VTO to the selected agents' published schedules as soon as you choose **Request**. Before you submit a **By Staff** request, make sure that the selected agents have agreed to the VTO.

1. Select the number of required voluntary time off slots.

1. Provide the reason for requesting voluntary time off in the **Reason** text box. For **By Staffing group** requests, agents can view the reason before they accept or decline the VTO slot.

1. Choose **Request**

After you submit a **By Staff** request, the VTO appears as accepted in the published schedule calendar. The shift details show an accepted timestamp that matches the time the request was submitted, and no agent name. This is expected behavior: Connect Customer applied the VTO, so it didn't record an agent action.
