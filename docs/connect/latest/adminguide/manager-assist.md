# Manager assist in Connect Customer

As a supervisor or contact center manager, you can
ask about your contact center performance in plain language instead of opening a dashboard
or building a report. Type a question such as `How is my contact center performing
 over the last week?`, and manager assist returns a response that is generated
from the metrics data in your instance.

You can ask about more than 150 standard and custom
Connect Customer metrics, including agent performance, queue health, contact volume, self-service
optimization, and evaluations. For the definition of each metric, see [Metric definitions in Connect Customer](metrics-definitions.md "metrics-definitions.md").

You can do the following:

- Get insights into the metrics and reporting data in your Connect Customer instance without
  switching between dashboards and reports.
- Ask follow-up questions about key operational use cases, so you can move from a
  summary to the specific queue, agent, or hour that you care about.
- Request an investigation when a metric changes unexpectedly, and receive
  prioritized recommendations. For more information, see [Investigations and
  recommendations](manager-assist-investigations.md "manager-assist-investigations.md").
- Generate charts, tables, and narrative summaries from your metrics data.

###### Note

Manager assist preview is available in all supported languages. During the
preview, manager assist operates in read-only mode: it cannot modify any
configuration or resources in your instances currently.

###### Important

Responses might contain inaccuracies. Always validate
insights against your Connect Customer dashboards and reports before you make business
decisions.
