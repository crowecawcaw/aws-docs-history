# DisconnectParticipant

Disconnects the participant from the contact and stops this flow from running.

## Parameter object

No parameters are expected.

## Results and conditions

None. Conditions are not supported.

## Errors

None.

## Restrictions

This action is supported for all channels and in contact flows, transfer flows,
and customer queue flows.
