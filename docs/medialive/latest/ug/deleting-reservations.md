# Deleting an expired

reservation

When a reservation has expired, you can delete the reservation from the list. You can't
cancel a reservation that hasn't expired.

Open the MediaLive console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/medialive/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/medialive/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/medialive/"). In the navigation pane, choose
**Reservations**. Choose the item or items, and then choose
**Delete**.
