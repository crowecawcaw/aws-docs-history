

# Deleting an expired reservation
<a name="deleting-expired-reservation"></a>

When a reservation has expired, you can delete it.

**To delete an expired reservation (console)**

1. Open the MediaLive console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/medialive/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/medialive/).

1. In the navigation pane, choose **Reservations**.

1. Choose the reservation name, and then choose **Delete**.
