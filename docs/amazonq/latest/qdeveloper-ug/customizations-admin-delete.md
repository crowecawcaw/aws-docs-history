# Deleting your customization

This section explains how to delete a customization with Amazon Q.

###### Warning

Deleting a customization will delete all versions associated with the
resource.

To delete your customization, follow this procedure:

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console.
2. Switch to the Amazon Q Developer console.
3. From the navigation pane on the left, choose
   **Customizations**.
4. The customizations page will appear.
5. If the customization that you want to delete is still active, choose
   **Deactivate**.
6. Choose **Delete**.

###### Note

You can also delete a customization from the page that gives the details of that
customization.

To do that, just choose **Delete** from the upper right corner of
the customization detail page.
