# Pausing integration with

Quick Suite

After you enable the plugin, it automatically queries Amazon Quick Suite for every user prompt.
To stop automatically querying Quick Suite, you can pause the plugin. When you pause the
plugin, end users will no longer see insights from Quick Suite answers. You continue to incur
costs for Quick Suite Admin Pro subscription users.

###### To pause the integration

1. Log in to the Amazon Q Business console.
2. Choose your application.
3. In the navigation pane, choose **Amazon Quick Suite**.
4. In **QuickSight details**, choose **Pause Quick Suite
   answers**.
5. Confirm your decision to pause the integration. When you're ready to resume,
   choose **Resume QuickSight Answers** on this page.
