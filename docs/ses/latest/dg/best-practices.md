# Best practices for sending email using Amazon SES

The way you manage email communications with your customers is referred to as your
_email program_. There are several factors that can lead to the
success or failure of your email program; these factors may seem confusing or mysterious at
first. However, by understanding how email is delivered, and by following certain best
practices, you can increase the chances of your email successfully reaching your customers'
inboxes.
