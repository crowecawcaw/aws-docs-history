# View a WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) in AWS End User Messaging Social

You can view the WABA associated with your AWS account.

###### To view the WABA associated with your account

1. Open the AWS End User Messaging Social console at
   [https://console.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/social-messaging/").
2. In **Business accounts**, choose a WABA.
3. On the **Phone numbers** tab, view your phone number, display name, quality
   rating, and the number of business initiated conversations that you have left
   for the day.

On the **Event destinations** tab, view your event destination.
To edit your event destination, follow the directions in [Message and event destinations in AWS End User Messaging Social](managing-event-destinations.md "managing-event-destinations.md").

On the **Templates** tab, choose **Manage message
templates** to edit your WhatsApp templates through Meta. Each WABA
has a 250 template limit.

On the **Tags** tab, you can manage your WABA resource
tags.
