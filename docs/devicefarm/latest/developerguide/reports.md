## Report retention

Device Farm stores your reports for 400 days. These reports include metadata, logs,
screenshots, and performance data.

## Report components

Reports in Device Farm contain pass and fail information, crash reports, test and device
logs, screenshots, and performance data.

Reports include detailed per-device data and high-level results, such as the number of
occurrences of a given problem.

## Logs in reports

Reports include complete logcat captures for Android tests and complete Device Console
logs for iOS tests.
