

# Design principles

 The following design principles serve as fundamental guidelines that shape the recommendations while preparing your AWS environment: 
+  **Establish consistent patterns, processes, and configurations across the organization:** Standardization reduces complexity, improves operational efficiency, and enables effective governance at scale. 
+  **Security by design:** Integrate security controls and compliance requirements into the foundation of your infrastructure. Security should be built-in rather than bolted-on, with automated enforcement of security policies and controls. 
+  **Use automation to maintain consistency:** Prioritize automation in all aspects of infrastructure management. Manual processes should be the exception, not the norm, enabling consistency, reducing human error, and improving operational efficiency. 
+  **Centralize control and distributed execution:** Maintain centralized governance and control while enabling teams to operate independently within established controls. This balance promotes innovation while improving organizational compliance. 
+  **Operational excellence through observability:** Design for comprehensive visibility across all environments. Standardized monitoring, logging, and alerting practices enable effective operations and rapid problem resolution. 
+  **Promote cost-conscious architecture:** Incorporate cost awareness and optimization into architectural decisions from the start. Enable accurate cost attribution and establish mechanisms for ongoing cost optimization. 
+  **Create future-proof, flexible foundations:** Design foundations that can adapt to changing business needs and technological evolution. Avoid rigid structures that limit future options or create technical debt.
