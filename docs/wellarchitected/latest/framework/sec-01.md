# SEC 1. How do you securely operate your workload?

To operate your workload securely, you must apply overarching
best practices to every area of security. Take requirements and
processes that you have defined in operational excellence at an
organizational and workload level, and apply them to all areas.
Staying up to date with AWS and industry recommendations and
threat intelligence helps you evolve your threat model and
control objectives. Automating security processes, testing, and
validation permit you to scale your security operations.
