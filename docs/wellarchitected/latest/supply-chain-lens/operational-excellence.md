#

Operational excellence

The operational excellence pillar provides guidance on running and
monitoring systems to deliver business value and continually
improving supporting processes and procedures.

The operational excellence pillar includes the ability to support
development and run workloads effectively, gain insight into their
operations, and to continuously improve supporting processes and
procedures to deliver business value.

This section provides an overview of design principles, questions,
best practices, and implementation guidance on implementation. For
more information, see the Operational Excellence Pillar whitepaper.
