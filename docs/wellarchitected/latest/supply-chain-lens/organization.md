# Organization

| SCOPS01: How do you identify and track all stakeholders<br>interacting with the supply chain application? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                           |

To assess the impact of outages and develop robust business
continuity plans, it is important to understand who the end users
of the application are and how they use it today.

| SCOPS02: How are key performance indicators established, regularly reviewed, and communicated for each stage of the supply chain? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                                                   |

The ability to measure and understand the health and effectiveness
of your supply chain operations is critical across all stages.

| SCOPS03: How do you empower supply chain<br>teams to make real-time decisions to optimize<br>operations? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                          |

The agility and responsiveness of supply chain teams in modern
supply chains is important for making informed and timely
decisions.

| SCOPS04: How do you determine priorities for integrating AI into<br>your supply chain? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                        |

Determining priorities for AI integration in your supply chain is
a critical strategic decision that impacts operational efficiency,
cost management, and competitive advantage.

| SCOPS05: How do you determine if<br>everyone's part in enabling business success with AI is<br>understood? |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                            |

Understanding everyone's role in enabling AI success is crucial
because it establishes clear accountability, facilitates proper
resource allocation, and creates organizational alignment across
technical and business functions.

| SCOPS06: How do you structure your<br>organization to support AI-driven supply chain<br>outcomes? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                   |

Properly structuring your organization to support AI-driven supply
chain outcomes is critical because it determines how effectively
you can implement, scale, and derive value from AI technologies
across your supply chain operations. 

| SCOPS07: How do you meet the compliance<br>and regulatory needs for your supply chain workload? |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                 |

Meeting compliance and regulatory requirements for your supply
chain workload is essential because non-compliance can result in
significant legal penalties, operational disruptions, and
reputational damage across global markets.

| SCOPS08: How do you assess and manage the<br>operational readiness of new suppliers or logistics<br>partners? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                               |

Assessing and managing the operational readiness of new suppliers
or logistics partners is crucial because these third parties
directly impact your ability to deliver products reliably,
maintain quality standards, and respond to market changes. 

| SCOPS09: How do you facilitate continuous<br>refresh of your demand and supply data? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|                                                                                      |

Facilitating continuous refresh of demand and supply data is
critical because it enables real-time visibility and
decision-making across your supply chain, allowing for faster
responses to market changes and operational disruptions.
