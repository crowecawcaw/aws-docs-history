# Infrastructure protection

| SCSEC06: How do you secure and isolate the different stages of your supply chain? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                   |

Segmenting and isolating different stages of your supply chain is
crucial for limiting the blast radius of potential security
incidents. This approach helps prevent lateral movement of threats
and makes sure that a compromise in one area doesn't cascade
throughout your entire supply chain environment.

| SCSEC07: How do you protect your supply<br>chain systems and workloads from potential vulnerabilities or<br>misconfigurations? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|                                                                                                                                |

Supply chain systems are prime targets for bad actors seeking to
exploit vulnerabilities or misconfigurations. Proactive
identification and remediation of these weaknesses is essential to
maintain the security posture of your supply chain and help
prevent disruptions to business operations.
