# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar provides guidance on managing resources
efficiently and aligning supply chain costs with business
objectives. It focuses on optimizing resource utilization across
materials, transportation, and warehousing while balancing
speed-to-market requirements. By implementing strategic cost
management practices, organizations can maximize the economic
benefits of cloud-based deployments while maintaining competitive
advantage in supply chain operations.
