

# Data protection


|  TELCOSEC04: How do you protect customer proprietary network information (CPNI) and personally identifiable information (PII)?  | 
| --- | 
| [TELCOSEC04-BP01 Enable encryption for CPNI and PII information at rest and in transit and access restriction](telcosec04-bp01.md) | 

 Telecom operators must protect sensitive customer data like CPNI and PII in accordance with strict regulations. This question examines the organization's implementation of encryption, access controls, audit logging, and data handling procedures to guard against unauthorized access or misuse of customer information.
