# Resource availability

| TELCOOPS06: How do you verify resource availability for scale-out scenarios in<br>telecommunication workloads? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                                |

Telecommunication workloads often require rapid scaling to meet sudden increases in
demand or to maintain service continuity. However, cloud environments may not guarantee
immediate availability of resources, especially for large instance types commonly used in
telco deployments. This question addresses the challenges of maintaining consistent resource
availability for scale-out scenarios in dynamic telecommunication environments.
