# Design principles

In addition to the general design principles from the cost optimization pillar of the
Well-Architected Framework whitepaper, the following design principles can assist you to
optimize the costs of your telco network infrastructure.

- **Measure cost at every layer of the network
  infrastructure**: The costs to develop, operate, and maintain the network have
  significant impact on the financial viability of a telco. Therefore, it is important to
  understand and track the infrastructure costs at each layer - core, distribution, access,
  edge - so you can identify areas that may require cost optimization.
