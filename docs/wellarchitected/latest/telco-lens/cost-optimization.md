# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of refinement and improvement
of a system over its entire lifecycle. From the initial design of your first proof of concept to
the ongoing operation of production workloads, adopting the practices in this paper will allow
you to build and operate cost-aware systems that achieve business outcomes and minimize costs,
thus allowing your business to maximize its return on investment.
