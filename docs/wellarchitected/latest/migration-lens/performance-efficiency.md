# Performance efficiency

The performance efficiency pillar focuses on the efficient use of
computing resources to meet requirements and the maintenance of that
efficiency as demand changes and technologies evolve. As migration
progresses through the assess, mobilize, and migrate and modernize
phases, it is important to avoid impacting performance. This can be
done by selecting the right architecture or right infrastructure and
resources for the migrated workload and ensuring you have mechanism
to automatically detect smigub-optimal performance. This section
includes best practice considerations to maintain performance
efficiency while migrating workloads into the AWS Cloud.
