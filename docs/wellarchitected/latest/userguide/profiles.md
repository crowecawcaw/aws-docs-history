# Using profiles in AWS WA Tool

You can create profiles to provide your business context, and identify goals you'd like to
accomplish when performing a Well-Architected review. AWS Well-Architected Tool uses the information
gathered from your profile to help you focus on a prioritized
list of questions that are relevant to your business during the workload review. Attaching a
profile to your workload also helps you see which risks are prioritized for you to address
with your improvement plan.

You can [create a profile](creating-a-profile.md "creating-a-profile.md") from the
**Profiles** page and associate it to a new workload, or you can [add a profile to an existing workload](adding-profiles-to-workloads.md "adding-profiles-to-workloads.md").
