# The AWS Well-Architected Tool Milestones tab

To display the milestones for your workload, choose the
**Milestones** tab.

After you select a milestone, choose **Generate report** to
create the workload report associated with the milestone. The report contains the
responses to the workload questions, your notes, and the number of high and medium
risks in the workload at the time that the milestone was saved.

You can view details about the state of your workload at the time of a specific
milestone by either:

- Choosing the name of the milestone.
- Selecting the milestone and choosing **View
  milestone**.
