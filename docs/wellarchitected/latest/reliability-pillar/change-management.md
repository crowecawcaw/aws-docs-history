# Change management

Changes to your workload or its environment must be anticipated and
accommodated to achieve reliable operation of the workload. Changes
include those imposed on your workload such as spikes in demand, as
well as those from within such as feature deployments and security
patches.

The following sections explain the best practices for change management.
