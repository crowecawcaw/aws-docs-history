## FSICOST18-BP01 Define ambitious modernization strategy to

become truly AWS optimized

In traditional financial institutions databases and core banking solutions are key
cost drivers. Improve your Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) by refactoring your lift and
shift strategies to continue your modernization activities where you can improve
performance while reducing your costs. The Operational Excellence pillar helps you
define which workloads are suitable for refactoring. In the case of core banking systems
provided by a vendor, start a dialog with your vendor to build a roadmap for workload modernization to make them cost-efficient. Also concentrate on modernization of
workloads that interact with databases and core banking systems (for example,
customer-facing web-pages, and apps). Leverage the AWS service WorkSpaces for remote
diagnostics.
