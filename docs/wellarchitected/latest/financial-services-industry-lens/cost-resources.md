## Partner solutions

There are many AWS Partner solutions helping our customers to provide cost insights. These
partners use AWS services and are well-integrated with AWS. Financial services customers
can use these third-party products in addition to AWS services to optimize cost. These
products are listed in the [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/ "https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/"), which
can help with seamless billing and discounts, if applicable.
