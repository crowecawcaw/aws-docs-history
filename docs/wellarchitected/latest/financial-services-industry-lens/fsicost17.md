## FSICOST17-BP01 Assess workload architecture to identify

the most cost-effective resources

There are multiple factors that affect the architecture, for example, new
enhancements related to business requirements, re-architecting your workload to improve
efficiency, new services released by AWS, price changes by AWS, or your team
creating an MVP product with services without considering costs. It is necessary to
assess the architecture and resources used by workload, for example, usage of serverless technologies, managed services to reduce the operational overhead, or AWS Graviton-based instances that meet your needs. Alternatively, you can refactor your monolithic
application to run as microservices. Most of the FSI systems are API-driven, so
splitting them across a number of diverse services helps procurement, and the
right-sizing of related resources.
