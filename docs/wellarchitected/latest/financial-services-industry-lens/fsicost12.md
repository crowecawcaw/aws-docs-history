# FSICOST12: Are you saving costs by adopting a set of modern microservice

architectures?

Financial institutions are moving from monolithic legacy systems such as mainframes into modern microservices architectures, giving them the flexibility of provisioning multiple environments to develop features rapidly, instead of waiting for the single monolith environment to be available, giving them greater agility and faster time-to-market.

## FSICOST12-BP01 Migrate your mainframe and on-premises

infrastructure to adopt a cloud-based microservices approach

Financial institutions are moving from monolithic legacy systems such as mainframes
into modern microservices architectures, giving them the flexibility of provisioning
multiple environments to develop features rapidly, instead of waiting for the single
monolith environment to be available, giving them greater agility and faster
time-to-market. Quantifying this gain is important for stakeholder buy-in.
