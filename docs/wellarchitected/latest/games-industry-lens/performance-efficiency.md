# Performance efficiency

The performance efficiency pillar focuses on the efficient use of
computing resources to meet requirements and maintaining that
efficiency as demand changes and technologies evolve.

Take a data-driven approach to selecting a high-performance
architecture. Gather comprehensive data on the architecture, from
the high-level design to the selection and configuration of
resource types. Consider your architectural choices on a cyclical
basis to take advantage of the continually evolving set of
services and solutions. Metrics assist with understanding
deviation from expected performance so you can take action. A data
driven approach assists with making tradeoffs with your
architecture to improve performance, reduce cost, or improve the
developer experience.
