# Process and culture

| GAMEPERF08: How do you align the performance bar for your<br>game with player and developer expectations? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                           |

Knowing your player and developer is one of the most important
aspects of improving performance efficiency. Delivering a
performant game with low operational overhead is one of the best
ways to show players and developers that you care about their
experience and can differentiate your game and studio.
