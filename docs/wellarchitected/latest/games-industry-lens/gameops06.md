# Optimize over time

| GAMEOPS06: How do you optimize your game over time? |
| --------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                     |

Optimize your game over time by monitoring key metrics and
telemetry data to identify player trends, system performance, and
areas for improvement. Continuously update your game design,
infrastructure, and load testing approach based on these insights,
while adapting to new technologies and frameworks to provide
optimal performance and player experience as your game evolves.
