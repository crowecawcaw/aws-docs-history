# Design principles

In addition to the design principles from the cost optimization
pillar of the Well-Architected Framework, the following design
principles optimize the costs of running your game workload in the
cloud.

- **Measure the infrastructure cost per
  player, system, and game feature:** Understand and
  track the infrastructure costs required for specific player
  experiences and features across game systems. This can
  identify areas of your architecture that may need cost
  optimization.
- **Assess the trade-off of cost
  optimization versus player experience:** Evaluate
  what stage your game is in to determine the right focus -
  player experience or cost optimization. Typically, once a game
  reaches critical mass and player population stabilizes, it is
  time to focus on optimizing operating costs. The key is to
  balance providing a great player experience with running your
  game infrastructure in the most cost-effective manner.
  Implementing these design principles maximize the return on
  your game investments.
