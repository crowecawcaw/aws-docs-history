

# Sustainability
<a name="sustainability"></a>

 The digital sovereignty lens doesn't add new best practices to the sustainability pillar. Best practices already defined under the [sustainability pillar](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/sustainability-pillar/sustainability-pillar.html) provide adequate advice. 

 Sovereignty requirements can also trade-off against sustainability goals. Data residency and jurisdictional constraints narrow Region selection, and duplicating operations across jurisdictions for continuity increases resource use. Weigh these trade-offs where they arise.
