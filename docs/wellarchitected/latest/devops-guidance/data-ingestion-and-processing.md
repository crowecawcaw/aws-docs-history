# Data ingestion and processing

Data ingestion and processing involves the collection, centralization, and analysis of
data from multiple sources. This data, when effectively ingested and processed, helps teams to
understand the availability, security, performance, and reliability of their systems in
real-time. Through streamlining data ingestion and processing, teams can make quicker and more
effective decisions, enhancing overall agility and reliability of systems.
