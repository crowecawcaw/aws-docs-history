# Indicators for automated compliance and guardrails

Integrate risk management and
governance mechanisms into the maintenance of compliance in
dynamic environments. It enables automatic enforcement of
directive, detective, preventive, and responsive measures,
reducing the manual overhead associated with compliance
management.
