# Data lifecycle management

Enforce stringent data controls, residency, privacy, sovereignty, and security
throughout the entire data lifecycle. Scale your data collection, processing, classification,
retention, disposal, and sharing processes to better align with regulatory compliance and
safeguard your software from potential disruptions due to data mismanagement.
