# Team interfaces

Team interfaces are the input and output mechanisms that direct the flow of work
between teams in a DevOps environment. These interfaces work to share information
and resources aacross the organization, facilitate collaboration, and
help teams to align their goals and priorities. Effective team interfaces streamline
processes, reduce bottlenecks, and improve overall productivity within and across teams.
