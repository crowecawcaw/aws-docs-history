# Hardware and services

| MIDASUS05: How do you optimize hardware resources for manufacturing<br>workloads? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                   |

Efficient hardware utilization in manufacturing environments helps reduce energy
consumption while maintaining the performance requirements of critical operations. By
properly selecting and configuring compute resources, organizations can minimize their
environmental impact while improving reliable manufacturing operations across design,
simulation, planning, and execution systems.
