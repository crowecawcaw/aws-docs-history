# Process and culture

| MIDASUS06: How do you integrate sustainability into manufacturing<br>processes and culture? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                             |

Manufacturing organizations can promote sustainability through effective processes and
cultural change by establishing clear sustainability goals and implementing continuous
improvement processes. Building a sustainability-oriented culture requires not just
technological solutions, but also organizational alignment, education, and metrics that make
environmental impact visible and actionable across the enterprise.
