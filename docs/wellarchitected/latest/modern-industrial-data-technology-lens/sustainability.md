# Sustainability

The sustainability pillar provides you with the discipline to address the long-term
environmental, economic, and societal impact of your business activities. You can find
extensive prescriptive guidance on your implementation's sustainability in the [Well-Architected Sustainability Pillar whitepaper](../sustainability-pillar/sustainability-pillar.md "../sustainability-pillar/sustainability-pillar.md").

Manufacturing organizations must focus on sustainability within their design, production
processes and supply chain operations to reduce their impact on the environment and to
encourage sustainable practices. Focusing on these areas helps manufacturers adapt their
operations to industry sustainability best practices, adopt new environmentally friendly
technologies and materials, and plan for the business impacts of potential future regulatory
requirements related to environmental protection and resource conservation.
