# Design principles

The operational excellence pillar in the MIDA Lens is concerned with enabling
manufacturing organizations to achieve reliable, high-performing, and continuously improving
data infrastructure to support their operational needs. By emphasizing proactive monitoring,
automated incident response, continuous optimization, and thorough documentation, MIDA
empowers manufacturing teams to rapidly detect, diagnose, and resolve issues that could
impact production, quality, or maintenance.

This operations-centric approach verifies that MIDA can adapt to changing business
requirements, drive continuous improvement, and provide the responsive, observable data
system that modern factories require to maintain a competitive edge. Through the principles
of the operational excellence pillar, MIDA lays the foundation for manufacturing
organizations to extract maximum value from their data and transform their operations.

The key design principles for operational excellence in MIDA include:

- **Designing for failure modes in industrial
  environments:** MIDA accounts for common failure scenarios in manufacturing,
  such as edge device failures, network outages, and equipment malfunctions. For reliable
  operation despite these disruptions, it incorporates features like redundancy, failover
  mechanisms, and self-healing capabilities.
- **Operational simplicity:** MIDA prioritizes simplicity
  in its operations by using self-healing mechanisms, infrastructure as code (IaC), and
  centralized configuration management. This approach reduces complexity and manual effort
  in maintaining the data infrastructure, allowing the manufacturing team to focus on
  higher-value activities.
- **Proactive monitoring and observability:** Comprehensive
  monitoring and observability, as emphasized in the AWS Well-Architected Framework, are
  crucial for MIDA. Observability enables the manufacturing team to quickly detect,
  diagnose, and resolve issues that could affect production, quality, or maintenance.
- **Automated incident response and remediation:** MIDA
  incorporates automated mechanisms to respond to and remediate incidents, minimizing
  downtime and improving the reliability of manufacturing processes. Tools such as
  automated runbooks, playbooks, and self-healing capabilities help maintain the
  operational health of the data infrastructure.
- **Continuous improvement and optimization:** MIDA is
  designed with a culture of continuous improvement and optimization in mind. This allows
  the manufacturing team to regularly review performance metrics, identify bottlenecks,
  and implement enhancements that improve the overall reliability and efficiency of the
  data infrastructure.
  By incorporating these design principles, the modern industrial data architecture can
  deliver the operational excellence required to support the ever-evolving needs of modern
  manufacturing organizations.
