|  MIDAREL05: How do you plan for disaster recovery in manufacturing environments?  | 
| --- | 
|   | 

 In manufacturing environments, production continuity is critical as downtime directly impacts operational efficiency and revenue. Manufacturing systems often involve specialized equipment, industrial control systems, and complex supply chains that require careful consideration for disaster recovery planning. Implementing a robust disaster recovery (DR) strategy verifies that manufacturing operations can recover quickly from disruptions.
