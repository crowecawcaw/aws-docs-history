# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar provides guidance to help manufacturing customers apply best
practices in the design, delivery, and maintenance of their AWS environments, with the most
effective use of services and resources at a minimal cost. Cost optimization includes the
continual process of refinement and improvement of a system over its entire lifecycle.

Businesses of different sizes in the manufacturing sector can benefit significantly from
the flexibility and scalability of the AWS Cloud when it comes to cost optimization. Whether a
small workshop, a mid-sized factory, or a large enterprise with complex IT environments, each
can use AWS to continuously evolve and optimize their resources, usage, and processes.
The cloud allows companies to efficiently manage their infrastructure and applications,
scaling as needed and paying only for what they use. This approach to cost optimization is
applicable and advantageous across the entire spectrum of manufacturing businesses, enabling
them to stay efficient and competitive regardless of their size or complexity.

However, even small and medium-sized manufacturing companies can maximize their return on
investment (ROI) by migrating their workloads to the AWS cloud and implementing cost
optimization best practices. This includes understanding cost at both the application and
workload level, using AWS services and features that optimize for cost, and continuously
monitoring and adjusting their infrastructure to maintain efficiency.
