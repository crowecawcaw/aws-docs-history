| MIDASEC03: How do you design your manufacturing data environments for<br>governance and compliance? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                     |

Design your manufacturing data environments with built-in governance and compliance
capabilities, including data privacy, classification, and protection policies. This process
involves implementing proper data handling procedures from the start, classifying,
encrypting, and managing sensitive manufacturing data throughout its lifecycle, and
maintaining regulatory compliance across different manufacturing operations.
