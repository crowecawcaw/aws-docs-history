# Conclusion

Cell-based architecture can bring a higher level of isolation,
predictability, and testability to your workload. It is extremely
important to understand the trade-offs of using this architectural
model and that not all your business workloads are going to
require extreme levels of resiliency.

This guidance focused on workload architecture of cell-based
architecture, but one aspect that cannot be overlooked is
operational excellence, with cells you now have dozens if not
hundreds of replicas of your workload to operate and evolve. All
of the operational excellence best practices recommended by the
Well-architected framework should have their attention reinforced
on workloads that use this architectural style.
