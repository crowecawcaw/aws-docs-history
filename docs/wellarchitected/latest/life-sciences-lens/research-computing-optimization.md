# Research computing optimization

| LSSUS01: How do you optimize the scheduling and execution of research computing<br>pipelines? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                               |

Life sciences research involves intensive computational workloads that can lead to
significant energy consumption and resource waste if not properly managed. Organizations need
to balance the demands of complex research computations with sustainable resource usage,
providing maximum scientific output while minimizing environmental impact.
