# Alignment to demand

| ADVSUS02: How do you align SLAs with<br>sustainability goals? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                               |

Verify that the service-level agreements (SLAs) for your
advertising workloads incorporate sustainability metrics,
incentives, and monitoring processes that align with and actively
support your organization's environmental goals.
