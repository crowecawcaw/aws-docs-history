# Programmatic advertising workloads

The petabyte-scale, millisecond latency world of programmatic advertising brings
significant challenges for performance, throughput, latency, and costs to advertising
platforms. The key platforms in programmatic advertising are ad servers, ad exchanges and
SSPs, and DSPs.
