# Moderation and brand safety

| ADVSUS10: How do you verify sustainability in content<br>moderation and brand safety systems? |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                               |

Content moderation and brand safety systems require continuous
processing of ad content and placement context across multiple
channels and formats. Implementing sustainable practices while
maintaining effective moderation requires balancing real-time
performance requirements with energy efficiency and resource
optimization.
