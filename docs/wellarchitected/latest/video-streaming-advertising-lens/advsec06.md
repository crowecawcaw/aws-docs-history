# Regulatory adherence

| ADVSEC06: How do you verify that you adhere to<br>regulatory requirements for your advertising solution? |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|                                                                                                          |

Compliance is essential for organizations as it supports
organizational growth and enables organizations to operate
legally across different jurisdictions. Also, compliance
standards enhance data protection and security, by addressing
requirements such as General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
or NIST frameworks you verify proper data storage, handling, and
deletion protocols.
