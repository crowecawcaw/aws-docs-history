# Prioritize and plan improvements

Prioritize your identified improvements based on the greatest
anticipated impact with the lowest costs and acceptable risk.

Decide which improvements to focus on initially, and include them
in your resource planning and development roadmap.

Applying this step to the [Example scenario](improvement-process.md#example-scenario "improvement-process.md#example-scenario"), you prioritize the target improvements as follows:

| **Priority** | **Improvement**                                 | **Potential** | **Cost** | **Risk** |
| ------------ | ----------------------------------------------- | ------------- | -------- | -------- |
| 1            | Implement more effective compression mechanisms | High          | Low      | Low      |
| 2            | Implement predictive scaling                    | Medium        | Low      | Low      |

The high potential, low cost, and risk of updating file
compression make it a high-value target for your company and a
priority over implementing predictive scaling. You determine that
implementing predictive scaling with its medium potential impact,
low cost, and low risk should be the priority improvement after
file compression is complete.

You assign a team member to implement improved file compression
and add predictive scaling to your backlog.
