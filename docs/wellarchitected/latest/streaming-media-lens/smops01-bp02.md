

# SMOPS01-BP02 Create RACI matrices for streaming video operations
<a name="smops01-bp02"></a>

Define clear roles and responsibilities for each stakeholder involved in streaming video operations. By implementing RACI (responsible, accountable, consulted, and informed) matrices, organizations can establish a clear framework for accountability and decision-making across the entire streaming workflow.

**Desired outcome:**
+ Clear accountability and efficient decision-making processes that avoid operational gaps and keep streaming video delivery running smoothly.

**Benefits of establishing this best practice:**
+ Reduced response time during incidents
+ Elimination of confusion during critical operations
+ Improved cross-team collaboration
+ Enhanced operational efficiency

**Level of risk exposed if this best practice is not established:** Medium

## Implementation guidance
<a name="implementation-guidance"></a>

Comprehensive RACI matrices establish accountability, decision-making authority, and communication requirements for each step of the streaming video workflow. This clarity avoids confusion, gaps, or overlaps in responsibilities so all stakeholders understand their roles.

For streaming video workloads, RACI matrices should address content ingestion and processing, live event operations, quality monitoring and alerting, incident response and escalation, content delivery optimization, and monetization operations for ad-supported content. The complexity of streaming workflows, which often span multiple teams and third-party providers, makes explicit role definition particularly important during high-pressure situations like live events.

### Implementation steps
<a name="implementation-steps"></a>

1. **Identify key streaming workflow processes:** Map all critical processes in your streaming video workflow, including:
+ Content ingestion
+ Processing
+ Delivery
+ Monitoring

1. **List all roles involved:** Enumerate all roles involved in these processes, including internal teams and external partners.

1. **Define RACI responsibilities:** Assign responsibilities using the RACI model:
+ Responsible (who performs the work)
+ Accountable (who approves the work)
+ Consulted (who provides input)
+ Informed (who receives updates)

1. **Validate the matrix with stakeholders:** Review and validate the matrix with all stakeholders to confirm accuracy and buy-in.

1. **Distribute and implement:** Distribute the RACI matrix and integrate it into operational procedures and onboarding processes.

1. **Review and update regularly:** Regularly review and update the matrix as processes evolve and team structures change.
