

# SMOPS03-BP04 Use specialized media support solutions for business-critical streaming events
<a name="smops03-bp04"></a>

For business-critical streaming workloads and high-profile live events, use AWS Unified Operations for Media Support Solution (MSS) to augment your operational capabilities with proactive monitoring, specialized media expertise, and accelerated incident response with defined service-level objectives (SLOs).

**Desired outcome:**
+ Business-critical streaming events are supported by specialized media operations teams with proactive monitoring, pre-event validation, and rapid incident response thereby reducing operational risk and improving viewer experience during high-stakes streaming periods.

**Benefits of establishing this best practice:**
+ Proactive monitoring of streaming workloads by specialized media operations teams
+ 5-minute response SLO for business-critical streaming incidents
+ Pre-event planning and infrastructure validation with media-specialized engineers
+ Reduced operational burden on internal teams during high-profile events
+ Access to deep media domain expertise for incident diagnosis and resolution

**Level of risk exposed if this best practice is not established:** High

## Implementation guidance
<a name="implementation-guidance"></a>

Evaluating your streaming workload portfolio helps identify candidates for specialized media support. Live events with high business impact (sports, concerts, and premieres) where failure has direct revenue and reputational consequences, always-on linear streaming channels requiring ongoing operational monitoring, and ad-supported streaming platforms where downtime directly affects monetization are strong candidates.

Engaging with AWS Unified Operations for MSS establishes pre-event infrastructure reviews and readiness assessments, proactive monitoring configurations tailored to your streaming architecture, defined escalation paths and response SLOs for streaming-specific incidents, and post-event analysis and ongoing improvement recommendations.

Integrating MSS support with internal operational procedures aligns MSS monitoring with your existing observability stack, incorporates MSS escalation paths into your war room procedures, and uses MSS pre-event reviews as input to your capacity planning process.

### Implementation steps
<a name="implementation-steps"></a>

1. **Identify business-critical streaming workloads:** Identify business-critical streaming workloads and events that would benefit from specialized support based on business impact and operational complexity.

1. **Engage with AWS account team:** Engage with your AWS account team to evaluate Unified Operations for MSS and determine fit for your workload portfolio.

1. **Define scope of coverage including:** Define scope of coverage including specific workloads, event windows, and services to be supported.

1. **Establish pre-event review cadence:** Establish a pre-event review cadence with the MSS team to validate infrastructure readiness ahead of major events.

1. **Integrate MSS with internal procedures:** Integrate MSS monitoring and escalation paths with internal procedures to create a unified operational response framework.

1. **Conduct joint readiness exercises ahead:** Conduct joint readiness exercises ahead of major events to validate coordination between internal teams and MSS.

1. **Review post-event reports:** Review post-event reports and incorporate lessons into future event preparations and operational improvements.
