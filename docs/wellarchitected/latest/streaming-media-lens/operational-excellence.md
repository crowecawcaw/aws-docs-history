

# Operational excellence
<a name="operational-excellence"></a>

The operational excellence pillar covers running and monitoring streaming video systems to deliver business value and continually improve supporting processes. Streaming media workflows require high automation, deep observability, and thorough testing to maintain quality across encoding, packaging, and delivery at scale.

## Design principles
<a name="operational-excellence-design-principles"></a>
+ **Automate operational processes:** Reduce manual intervention in encoding, packaging, and delivery to lower the risk of errors that cause quality issues or outages.
+ **Optimize for observability:** Collect and analyze infrastructure metrics, client telemetry, and logs in real time to detect and resolve issues quickly.
+ **Implement thorough testing:** Validate encoding, packaging, and delivery across devices and network conditions before and during live operation.
