

# Design principles
<a name="design-principles"></a>

In the cloud, there are several principles that can help you create and run streaming media workloads. Keep these in mind as we discuss best practices:
+ **Keep a glass-to-glass perspective:** From camera to viewer device, consider how changes impact the critical path of media delivery. Identify key health and diagnostic metrics for video, audio, captions, and metadata that affect Quality of Experience (QoE).
+ **Scale delivery:** Media streaming can generate high traffic volumes with stringent requirements for Quality of Service (QoS). To handle demand effectively, use content in adaptive bitrates (ABR) with content delivery networks (CDNs) for appropriate caching and auto scaling. Verify that static content is cached with appropriate time-to-live (TTL) values and load test dynamic content well above expected audience capacity.
+ **Understand content value:** Establish a shared understanding among stakeholders regarding content value and legal obligations, weighing these against costs, performance, and latency considerations. Content protection may require implementation of Digital Rights Management (DRM) and media-specific tokenization.
+ **Design for failure:** Define complete reliability objectives across workflows based on risks to viewer experience. Architect systems to gracefully handle one or more component failure or degradation through primary, backup, and disaster recovery modes. Test failure scenarios extensively with operational playbooks and verify that operations teams have complete workflow visibility.
+ **Enable monetization:** Use Server-Side Ad Insertion (SSAI) or Server-Guided Ad Insertion (SGAI) for personalized video advertising to maximize revenue while maintaining uninterrupted playback.
+ **Know your audience:** Build deep audience understanding to inform business and technical decisions. Use analytics to identify network conditions, devices, and displays your audience uses. Optimize for their consumption habits and prioritize playback testing. Analyze interaction data to engage audiences through recommendations, personalization, and notifications. Use viewership data and usage forecasts to validate workflow component performance at peak scale.
