assets to establish traceability, reproducibility, and continuous
improvement in your generative AI workflows? This includes the
practices and tools used for maintaining a structured approach to
prompt engineering, model versioning, and performance evaluation,
including methods for testing variants, capturing baselines, and
optimizing based on defined metrics and ground truth data.
