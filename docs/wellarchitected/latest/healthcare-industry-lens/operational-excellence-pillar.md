# Operational excellence pillar

The operational excellence pillar provides guidance on running and monitoring systems to deliver
business value and continually improving supporting processes and
procedures.

The operational excellence pillar provides an overview of design
principles, best practices, and questions. You can find
prescriptive guidance on implementation in the
[Operational
Excellence Pillar](../operational-excellence-pillar.md "../operational-excellence-pillar.md") whitepaper.
