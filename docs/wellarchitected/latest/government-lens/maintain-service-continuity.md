# Maintain service continuity

Particular government services must not have unexpected downtime due to the risk of
immediate dangers to the quality of life for citizens, for example, emergency services, social
welfare payments, policing systems, and healthcare services. Resilient architectures are key
to support this design principle, and the people responsible for service delivery must take a
risk-based approach to architectural decisions to support critical government services. Make
deliberate use of the [AWS Well-Architected
Framework Reliability Pillar](../reliability-pillar/welcome.md "../reliability-pillar/welcome.md") to inform your decision making.

**Questions to ask:** 

- How will your system or service handle an attack or disruption? What backup options
  exist for users?
- How will a disruption to service be detected and escalated proportionately to the
  risk and impact of the service?
