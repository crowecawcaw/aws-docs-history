# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of refinement and improvement
of a system over its entire lifecycle. This optimization should occur from the initial design
of your first proof of concept to the ongoing operation of production workloads. Choose the
right solution and pricing model. Build cost-aware systems that allow you to achieve
business outcomes and minimize costs. To realize cost optimization over time, you need to
identify data, infrastructure resources, or analytics jobs that can be deleted or downsized.
