# 11 – Detect and react to failures

**How do you detect and react to failures impacting your SAP
workload?** Design how software or operating procedures can help ensure the health
and resilience of your SAP workload. Monitor for potential and actual failures, focusing on
prevention where possible. Consider whether a component is distributed or is a single point of
failure and design a resiliency solution that minimizes the impact to your workload. In
addition to testing periodically to understand your risk profile, examine how automation could
improve your resilience.
