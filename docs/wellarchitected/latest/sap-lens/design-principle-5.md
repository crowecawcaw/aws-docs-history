# 5 – Understand security standards and how they apply to

your SAP workload

**How do you define the security standards and controls to align with the
criticality of your SAP workload?** Standards are published documents that define
the policies and procedures required to secure your systems following best practices for a
product, organization, industry, or jurisdiction. They provide a framework against which your
SAP workload can be evaluated. Some standards are mandatory to ensure compliance with
regulatory requirements, while others are optional but help with establishing roles and
responsibilities.
