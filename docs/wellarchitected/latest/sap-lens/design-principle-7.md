# 7 – Control access to your SAP workload through

identity and permissions

**How do you control access to your SAP workload?** Use
mechanisms provided by AWS, SAP, and other third parties to ensure that end users and
interfacing systems are properly identified and authenticated. How are permissions
controlled to ensure least privilege? How is access audited and reported on? Start by
identifying your user categories and then systematically work through the controls and your
identity management approach to limit access to your SAP workload.
