# Cost optimization pillar - Best practices

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of
refinement and improvement of a system over its entire lifecycle.
From the initial design of your very first proof of concept to the
ongoing operation of production workloads, adopting the practices
in this document can enable you to build and operate cost-aware
systems that achieve business outcomes and minimize costs, thus
allowing your business to maximize its return on investment. This
section includes best practices to consider while processing data.
