# Identify requirements and approvals

| RAIUC05: How will you identify applicable organizational requirements and approvals? |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
|                                                                                      |

AI systems may involve data processing, contributors, and users
that span multiple geographic locations and legal jurisdictions.
This may result in the AI system needing to meet multiple
differing, overlapping, and organizational obligations. Consult
organizational experts to identify and clarify requirements.
