# Assessment

Comprehensive assessment of your Microsoft workload environment is a
critical first step in optimizing costs on AWS. By utilizing
discovery and assessment tools like Migration Evaluator and AWS OLA,
organizations can gain complete visibility into their workloads,
understand resource utilization patterns, and identify opportunities
for optimization. This evaluation process creates the foundation for
effective cost management and resource rightsizing of Microsoft
workloads.

It is important to have the complete picture of your workload.
Taking this first step ensures your applications are using
right-sized resources. Gathering licensing information may also be
aided by this.
