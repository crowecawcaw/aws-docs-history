There are five best practice areas for cost optimization:

- [Cost-effective resources](cost-effective-resources.md "cost-effective-resources.md")
- [Expenditure and usage awareness](expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md "expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md")
- [Matching supply and demand](matching-supply-and-demand.md "matching-supply-and-demand.md")
- [Cloud Financial Management](cloud-financial-management.md "cloud-financial-management.md")
- [Optimize over time](optimize-over-time.md "optimize-over-time.md")
  Design decisions are sometimes guided by haste as opposed to empirical data, as the
  temptation always exists to overcompensate _just in case_ rather than spend
  time benchmarking for the most cost-optimal deployment.

This often leads to over-provisioned and poorly optimized deployments. The following
sections provide techniques and strategic guidance for the initial and ongoing cost
optimization of your deployment.
