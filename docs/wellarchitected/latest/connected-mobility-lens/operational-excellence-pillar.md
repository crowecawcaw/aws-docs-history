# Operational excellence pillar

The operational excellence pillar includes operational practices and procedures used to
manage connected mobility workloads. Operational excellence comprises how planned changes are
performed, as well as responses to unexpected operational events. Change execution and responses
should be automated. All processes and procedures of operational excellence must be documented,
tested, and regularly reviewed. By adopting the practices in this paper, you can build
architectures that provide insight to the connected mobility workload status, perform effective
and efficient operation and event response, and can continue to improve and support your
business goals.
