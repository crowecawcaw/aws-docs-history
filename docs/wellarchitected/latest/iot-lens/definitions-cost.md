# Definitions

There are four focus areas for cost optimization:

- [Practice
  Cloud Financial Management](practice-cloud-financial-management.md "practice-cloud-financial-management.md")
- [Expenditure
  and usage awareness](expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md "expenditure-and-usage-awareness.md")
- [Cost-effective
  resources](cost-effective-resources.md "cost-effective-resources.md")
- [Managing
  demand and supplying resources](managing-demand-and-supplying-resources.md "managing-demand-and-supplying-resources.md")

There are tradeoffs to consider. For example, do you want to
optimize for speed to market or cost? In some cases, it's best to
optimize for speed (going to market quickly, shipping new
features, or meeting a deadline) rather than investing in upfront
cost optimization. Design decisions benefit from empirical data
and thoughtful benchmarking rather than being rushed because of
deadline, enabling teams to identify cost-efficient solutions that
deliver optimal performance while maximizing return on investment.
Rushed decisions could lead to over-provisioned and
under-optimized deployments. The following sections provide
techniques and strategic guidance for your deployment's initial
and ongoing cost optimization.
