# Use Over-The-Air device management

Over-the-air device management refers to operations to update,
secure and configure your IoT devices from the cloud.  Devices
that go offline unexpectedly or have critical security
vulnerabilities often require expensive site visits or shipment
of a device to a refurbishment center. To reduce the carbon
footprint and operational costs across your device fleet, your
device must support one or more capabilities for over-the-air
device management.
