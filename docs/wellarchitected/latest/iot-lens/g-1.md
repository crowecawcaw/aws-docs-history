# 3G

In most cases, carriers have sunset or have plans to sunset 3G
networks, and 2G networks that exist will likely outlive their
3G counterparts. It is not recommended to design new 3G
products or to expect 3G fallback for LTE devices as the
phase-out of 3G will impact the longevity of the device and
ultimately the overall sustainability of the solution due to
replacements.
