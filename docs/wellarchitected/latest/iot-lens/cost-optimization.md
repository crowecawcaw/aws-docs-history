# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of
refinement and improvement of a system over its entire lifecycle.
From the initial design of your first proof of concept to the
ongoing operation of production workloads, adopting the practices in
this paper helps you build and operate cost-aware systems that
achieve business outcomes and minimize costs, allowing your business
to maximize its return on investment.
