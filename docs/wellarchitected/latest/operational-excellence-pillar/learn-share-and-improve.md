# Learn, share, and improve

It’s essential that you regularly provide time for analysis of
operations activities, analysis of failures, experimentation, and
making improvements. When things fail, you will want to ensure
that your team, as well as your larger engineering community,
learns from those failures. You should analyze failures to
identify lessons learned and plan improvements. You will want to
regularly review your lessons learned with other teams to validate
your insights.
