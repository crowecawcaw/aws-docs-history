# 12 – Build financial accountability models for data and workload usage

**How do you measure and attribute the
analytics workload financial accountability?** As your
business continues to evolve, so will your analytics workload.
Data analytics systems and the data generated from them will
grow over time into a mix of both shared and isolated-team
resources. Your organization should establish a financial
attribution model for these resources. Teams will understand
how their use of data analytics inﬂuences costs to the
business and this promotes a culture of accountability and
frugality. Creating a financial accountability model will
allow departments to cross-charge departments for shared
resources.
