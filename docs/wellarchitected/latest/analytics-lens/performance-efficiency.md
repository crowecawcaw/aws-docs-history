# Performance efficiency

The performance efficiency pillar focuses on the efficient use of
resources to meet requirements as demand changes and
technologies evolve. Performance optimization is not a one-time
activity. It is an incremental and continual process of
confirming business requirements, measuring the workload
performance, identifying under-performing components, and tuning
the components to meet your business needs.

Performance optimization should start with your organization’s
requirements, such as the business users of the analytics
workload. Let the business stakeholders define the performance
requirements and SLAs that must be met, then determine the
computing requirements meeting their performance needs.
