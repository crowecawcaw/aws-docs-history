# Cost optimization

The cost optimization pillar includes the continual process of
refinement and improvement of a system over its entire lifecycle
to optimize cost. Cost optimization is a key effort, from the
initial design of your first proof of concept, to the ongoing
operation of production workloads. It’s a years-long, continual
process. Choose the right solution and pricing model. Build
cost-aware systems that allow you to achieve business outcomes
and minimize costs. To perform cost optimization over time, you
should identify data, infrastructure resources, and analytics
jobs that can be removed or downsized.

Determine the analytics workﬂow costs at each individual data
processing step or individual pipeline branch. The benefit of
understanding analytics workﬂow costs at this granular level
will help you decide where to focus engineering resources for
development, and to perform a return on investment (ROI)
estimation for the analytics portfolio as a whole.
