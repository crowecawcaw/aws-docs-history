# Workload context checklist

To understand your business’s context better, you must gather the
following information.

| **ID**  | **Priority** | **Workload Context**                                                                                                             |
| ------- | ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| ☐<br>C1 | Required     | Name of the workload                                                                                                             |
| ☐<br>C2 | Required     | Description that contains the business purposes, key<br>performance indicators (KPIs), and the intended users of the<br>workload |
| ☐<br>C3 | Required     | Review owner who leads the lens review                                                                                           |
| ☐<br>C4 | Required     | Workload owner who is responsible for maintaining the<br>workload                                                                |
| ☐<br>C5 | Required     | Business stakeholders who sponsor the workload                                                                                   |
| ☐<br>C6 | Required     | Business partners who have a stake in the workload, such as<br>information security, finance, and legal                          |
| ☐<br>C7 | Recommended  | Architecture design document that describes the workload                                                                         |
| ☐<br>C8 | Recommended  | AWS account IDs associated with the workload                                                                                     |
| ☐<br>C9 | Recommended  | Regulatory compliance requirements relevant to the workload<br>(if any)                                                          |
