# Performance efficiency

The performance efficiency pillar focuses on the efficient use of computing resources to
meet requirements and maintain efficiency as demand changes and technologies evolve.

Use a data-driven approach to select a high-performance architecture. Gather data on all
aspects of the architecture, from the high-level design to the selection and configuration of
resource types. Review your choices on a cyclical basis to ensure that you are taking advantage
of the continually evolving AWS platform. Monitor your workload to ensure that you are aware
of any deviance from expected performance. Understand where you can make architecture tradeoffs
to improve performance, such using VPN over internet vs dedicated circuits via AWS Direct Connect for
your hybrid connectivity or terminating your hybrid connectivity on Virtual Private gateway
instead of Transit Gateway.
