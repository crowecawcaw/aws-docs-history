# Sustainability pillar

The sustainability pillar focuses on the long-term environmental
impacts of SaaS products. This pillar helps organizations design
and operate their SaaS products in a way that minimizes their
environmental footprint: Reducing energy consumption and
increasing efficiency across all components of a workload by
maximizing the benefits from the provisioned resources and
minimizing the total resources required. It helps organizations
understand the trade-offs involved in various design and
operational decisions and make informed choices that align with
their values and long-term business goals. By considering
sustainability from the beginning, organizations can create SaaS
products that not only meet the needs of their customers but also
contribute to a more sustainable future.
