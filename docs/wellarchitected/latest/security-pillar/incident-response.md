# Incident response

Even with mature preventive and detective controls, your organization should implement
mechanisms to respond to and mitigate the potential impact of security incidents. Your
preparation strongly affects the ability of your teams to operate effectively during an
incident, to isolate, contain and perform forensics on issues, and to restore operations to a
known good state. Putting in place the tools and access ahead of a security incident, then
routinely practicing incident response through game days, helps ensure that you can recover
while minimizing business disruption.
