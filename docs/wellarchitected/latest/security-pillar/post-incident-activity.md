# Post-incident activity

The threat landscape is constantly changing and it is important to
be equally dynamic in your organization’s ability to effectively
protect your environments. Key to continuous improvement is
iterating on the outcomes of your incidents and simulations in order
to improve your capabilities to effectively detect, respond to, and
investigate possible security incidents, reducing your possible
vulnerabilities, time to response, and return to safe operations.
The following mechanisms can help you verify that your organization
remains prepared with the latest capabilities and knowledge to
effectively respond, no matter the situation.
