# Unblock a Worker

If you mistakenly blocked a Worker, you can unblock them.

###### To unblock a Worker

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/ "https://requester.mturk.com/"),
   choose the **Manage** tab and then choose
   **Workers**.
2. On the **Manage Workers** page, choose the Worker ID of
   the Worker you want to unblock.
3. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, choose
   **Unblock Worker**.
4. In the **Unblock Worker** dialog box, enter a reason for
   unblocking the Worker and choose **Unblock**.
   The Worker receives a message with the reason you are unblocking them.
