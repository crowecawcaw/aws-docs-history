# Revoke a Worker's qualification

###### To revoke a Worker's qualification

In the case where a qualified Worker submits inferior work, you might choose to
revoke their qualification to do work on your batch.

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/ "https://requester.mturk.com/"),
   choose the **Manage** tab and then choose
   **Workers**.
2. On the **Manage Workers** page, choose the Worker ID whose
   qualifications you want to revoke.
3. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, choose the
   `X` next to the qualification type you want to revoke.
