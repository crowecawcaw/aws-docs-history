# View Worker statistics

Mechanical Turk enables you to view a Worker's statistics, which characterize what the
Worker is good at.

###### To view a Worker's statistics

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/ "https://requester.mturk.com/"),
   choose the **Manage** tab and then choose
   **Workers**.
2. On the **Manage Workers** page, the **Block Status** column can have the following values:
   - **Never Blocked** – Worker has never been
     blocked you.
   - **Blocked** – Worker is not allowed to
     work for you.
   - **Unblocked** – Worker was blocked by you
     at one time, but is no longer blocked.

3. To take a specific action on an individual Worker, choose a Worker
   ID.
4. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, you can view the Worker's approval
   rating, in addition to the number of assignments you approved and rejected in
   the last 7 days, 30 days, or for all time
   (**Lifetime**).
