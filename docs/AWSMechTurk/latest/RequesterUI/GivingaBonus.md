# Award a bonus

You can award bonuses to Workers to encourage them to keep working for you.

###### To give a Worker a bonus

1. On the Mechanical Turk Requester website at [https://requester.mturk.com/](https://requester.mturk.com/ "https://requester.mturk.com/"),
   choose the **Manage** tab and then choose
   **Workers**.
2. On the **Manage Workers** page, choose the Worker ID of
   the Worker you want to give a bonus to.
3. On the **Manage Individual Worker** page, choose
   **Bonus Worker**.
4. On the **Bonus Worker** page, enter the
   **Amount** of the bonus, the **Assignment
   ID** of the assignment that the Worker worked on, and the
   **Reason** for the bonus.
5. Choose **Pay Bonus Now**.
