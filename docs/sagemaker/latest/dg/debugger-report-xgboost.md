# SageMaker Debugger interactive report for XGBoost

Receive training reports autogenerated by Debugger. The Debugger reports provide insights into
your training jobs and suggest recommendations to improve your model performance.
For SageMaker AI XGBoost training jobs, use the Debugger [CreateXgboostReport](debugger-built-in-rules.md#create-xgboost-report "debugger-built-in-rules.md#create-xgboost-report") rule to receive a comprehensive training
report of the training progress and results. Following this guide, specify the [CreateXgboostReport](debugger-built-in-rules.md#create-xgboost-report "debugger-built-in-rules.md#create-xgboost-report") rule while
constructing an XGBoost estimator, download the report using the [Amazon SageMaker Python SDK](https://sagemaker.readthedocs.io/en/stable "https://sagemaker.readthedocs.io/en/stable") or
the Amazon S3 console, and gain insights into the training results.

###### Note

You can download a Debugger reports while your training job is running or after the job
has finished. During training, Debugger concurrently updates the report reflecting the
current rules' evaluation status. You can download a complete Debugger report only after
the training job has completed.

###### Important

In the report, plots and recommendations are provided for informational purposes
and are not definitive. You are responsible for making your own independent assessment
of the information.
