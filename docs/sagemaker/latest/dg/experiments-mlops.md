# SageMaker Experiments

ML model building requires many iterations of training as you tune the algorithm, model
architecture, and parameters to achieve high prediction accuracy. You can track the inputs
and outputs across these training iterations to improve repeatability of trials and
collaboration within your team using Amazon SageMaker Experiments. You can also track parameters,
metrics, datasets, and other artifacts related to your model training jobs. SageMaker Experiments
offers a single interface where you can visualize your in-progress training jobs, share
experiments within your team, and deploy models directly from an experiment.

To learn about SageMaker Experiments, see [Amazon SageMaker Experiments in Studio Classic](experiments.md "experiments.md").
