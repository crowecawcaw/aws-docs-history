# List training plans

To view your training plans:

1. Navigate to the SageMaker AI console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/").
2. Choose **Training Plans** in the left pane menu. This displays a
   list of all your training plans, including their names, status, target resource type,
   and other key details.

After purchasing a plan, you are directed to this list. Newly created plans appear
with a `Pending` status until payment is completed. The status is typically
updated within a few minutes of payment processing.
