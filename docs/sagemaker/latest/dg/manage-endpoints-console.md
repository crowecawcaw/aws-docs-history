# View endpoint details in the SageMaker AI console

To view your endpoints in the SageMaker AI console, do the following:

1. Go to the SageMaker AI console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/sagemaker/").
2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Inference**.
3. From the dropdown list, choose **Endpoints**.
4. On the **Endpoints** page, choose your endpoint.
   The endpoint details page should open, showing you a summary of your endpoint and
   metrics that have been collected for your endpoint.
