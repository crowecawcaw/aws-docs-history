

# Compute capacity reservations in Amazon SageMaker AI

SageMaker AI provides two ways to reserve compute capacity for your ML workloads. A training plan reserves GPU capacity for a time window that you choose. A SageMaker Capacity Reservation is an On-Demand Capacity Reservation that SageMaker AI reserves for your AWS account.
