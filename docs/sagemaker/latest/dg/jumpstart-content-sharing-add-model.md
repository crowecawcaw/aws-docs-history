# Add a model

To add a model, choose **Shared by my organization**, and then select
**Add model** from the **Add** dropdown list. Enter the
basic information for your model, and add any training or inference information you want to
share with collaborators to train or deploy your model. After you enter all the necessary
information, choose **Add model** in the lower right corner of the
screen.
