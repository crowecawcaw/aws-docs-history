# RCS country launch compliance guide

This guide helps you prepare a compliant RCS country launch registration that
passes carrier review on the first submission. It covers the specific requirements
that carriers evaluate during the review process, with examples of compliant and
non-compliant submissions.

For general information about submitting a country launch registration, see
[Launching RCS in countries](rcs-country-launch.md "rcs-country-launch.md"). For the
testing registration workflow, see
[Getting started with RCS](rcs-getting-started.md "rcs-getting-started.md").
