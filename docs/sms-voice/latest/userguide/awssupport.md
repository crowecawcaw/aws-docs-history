# Requesting support for SMS, MMS, and voice messaging through Support

Certain SMS options in AWS End User Messaging SMS can only be configured by creating a case in the
[AWS Support Center](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/"). Open a case to request any of the following:

- ###### An increase to your monthly SMS, MMS, or Voice spending threshold

By default, the monthly spending threshold is $1.00 (USD). Your spending
threshold determines the volume of messages that you can send with AWS End User Messaging SMS.
Request a spending threshold that meets the expected monthly message volume for
your SMS, MMS, or voice use case. To change your spending threshold, see [Change your spending threshold](awssupport-spend-threshold.md#awssupport-spend-threshold-open "awssupport-spend-threshold.md#awssupport-spend-threshold-open").

- ###### Moving from the sandbox to production

New AWS End User Messaging SMS accounts are placed into an SMS or voice sandbox. The sandbox
protects both AWS customers and recipients from fraud and abuse. The sandbox
also creates a safe environment for test, development, and QA accounts. To move
your account out of the sandbox and into production, see [The AWS End User Messaging SMS SMS/MMS sandbox](sandbox.md#sandbox-sms "sandbox.md#sandbox-sms") and [AWS End User Messaging SMS Voice sandbox](sandbox.md#sandbox-voice "sandbox.md#sandbox-voice").
When you create your case in the AWS Support Center, include all the information that's
required for the type of request you're submitting. If you don't, Support will contact you to
obtain this information before proceeding. By submitting a detailed case, you help to make
sure that your request is fulfilled quickly. For the details that are required for specific
types of SMS requests, see the topics in this section.
