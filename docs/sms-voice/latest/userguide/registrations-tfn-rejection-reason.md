# Toll-free number

registration rejection reasons

If your Toll-free number registration was rejected, use the following table to determine why it was
rejected and what you can do to fix your Toll-free number registration. After you
determine why the registration was rejected, you can modify the existing registration to address
that issue and resubmit. For more information, see [Edit a registration in AWS End User Messaging SMS](registrations-edit.md "registrations-edit.md").

| Reason for rejection             | AWS End User Messaging SMS rejection short description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | AWS End User Messaging SMS rejection long description |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Compliant Opt In Missing         | The opt-in process or screenshot is missing. A compliant opt-in<br>process or screenshot will clearly specify how your recipient is<br>able to provide their explicit consent to receive SMS messages. Some<br>common rejection reasons: missing explicit language around SMS<br>opt-in consent, mismatch between provided company name and opt-in<br>screenshots, receiving a text message cannot be required to sign up<br>for service, or SMS opt-in consent cannot be included in the Terms<br>of Service. For more information, see [Obtain permission](best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission "best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission").                                                                                                                                                  |
| Invalid Business Connection      | The contact information and company/application information does not<br>have a clear connection. SMS Messages can't be sent on behalf of a 3rd<br>party. In order to be verified please resubmit explaining the connection<br>between your contact and company/application information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Invalid Company Info             | The company information you provided is unable to be verified. In<br>order to be verified please confirm your company website is valid and<br>aligns with your company name and address.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Invalid Multi Numbers            | A single Toll Free number can only be associated with a single<br>business. Please either resubmit a new registration request for each<br>company with its own phone number or explain the connections between the<br>multiple businesses called out.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Invalid Overall                  | The information provided has been considered invalid. Please confirm<br>your company website, use case, opt-in, and message samples are all<br>valid inputs and align with other inputs in your registration.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Invalid URL                      | The company URL you provided is unable to be accessed. In order to be verified please confirm your provided company website is valid and active.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Non Compliant Opt In             | The opt-in process or screenshot you have provided is either<br>insufficient or non compliant. A compliant opt-in process or screenshot<br>will clearly specify how your recipient is able to provide their<br>explicit consent to receive SMS messages. Some common rejection reasons:<br>missing explicit language around SMS opt-in consent, mismatch between<br>provided company name and opt-in screenshots, receiving a text message<br>cannot be required to sign up for service, or SMS opt-in consent cannot<br>be included in the Terms of Service. For more information, see<br>[Obtain permission](best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission "best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission").                                                                                                |
| Non Compliant Opt In Consent     | The opt-in process or screenshot you have provided does not show<br>explicit consent. Explicit consent is the deliberate action of a user<br>having the option to request a specific message. A compliant opt-in<br>process or screenshot will clearly specify how your recipient is able to<br>provide their explicit consent to receive SMS messages. Some common<br>rejection reasons: missing explicit language around SMS opt-in consent,<br>mismatch between provided company name and opt-in screenshots, receiving<br>a text message cannot be required to sign up for service, or SMS opt-in<br>consent cannot be included in the Terms of Service. For more information, see<br>[Obtain permission](best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission "best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission"). |
| Non Compliant Opt In Third Party | The opt-in process or screenshot you have provided is either<br>insufficient or non compliant due to opt-in information being shared<br>with 3rd parties. A compliant opt-in process or screenshot will clearly<br>specify how your recipient is able to provide their explicit consent to<br>receive SMS messages and is not shared with 3rd parties. Please resubmit<br>after you remove any language around opt-in information sharing or<br>include language specifically stating opt-in information is not shared<br>with 3rd parties. For more information, see<br>[Obtain permission](best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission "best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission").                                                                                                                  |
| Non Compliant Use Case           | The use case and/or message samples provided are considered<br>restricted content under US Telecom regulations. Please refer to the<br>documentation below for a full list of items considered restricted<br>content. If you believe your content is falsely considered restricted<br>you can attempt to update your sample messages and use case and<br>re-submit the registration. For more information, see<br>[Obtain permission](best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission "best-practices.md#best-practices-sms-obtain-permission").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
