# Review and launch the campaign

At this point, you're almost ready to send the campaign to your audience segment. Before
you launch the campaign, you should review your settings and make changes if needed.

###### Prerequisite

Before you begin, complete [Schedule the campaign](campaigns-schedule.md "campaigns-schedule.md").

###### To review and launch a campaign

1. On the **Review and launch** page, review the settings for the
   campaign. If you need to make changes, use the navigation section on the left side of
   the window to go directly to the page that contains the content that you want to
   edit.
2. If all of the settings are correct, choose **Launch
   campaign**.
