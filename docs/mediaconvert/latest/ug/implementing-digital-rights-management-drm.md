# Content encryption and DRM in AWS Elemental MediaConvert

Protect your content from unauthorized use through encryption. Digital rights management (DRM)
systems provide keys to AWS Elemental MediaConvert for content encryption, and licenses to supported players and other consumers for
decryption.

The following sections provide guidance on how to choose and implement content encryption
using SPEKE for MediaConvert.
