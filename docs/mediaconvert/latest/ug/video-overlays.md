# Using video overlays

With the **Video overlays** feature, you can layer video content from
multiple videos simultaneously. You can also dynamically move or resize video content over
time.

Video overlays allow for many creative possibilities. Some examples of common workflows
might include the following:

- Provide alternate footage for a scene.
- Blank out content, such as content captured during a live broadcast or content you
  don't have the rights to.
- Shrink your primary content while an ad plays.
