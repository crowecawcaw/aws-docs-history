# Downloading the MediaConvert usage report

You can download a usage report as an XML or a comma-separated values (CSV) file.

###### To download the usage report

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console and open the MediaConvert console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconvert/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconvert/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconvert/").
2. In the title bar, choose your username or account ID, and then choose
   **Billing and Cost Management**.
3. In the navigation pane, choose **Cost explorer**.
4. Under **Report parameters**, **Group by**, **Dimension**, choose **Usage type**.
5. Under **Filters**, **Service**, choose **Elemental
   MediaConvert**.
6. Under **Cost and usage breakdown**, choose
   **Download as CSV**.
