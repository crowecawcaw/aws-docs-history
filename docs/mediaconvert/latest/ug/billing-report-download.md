

# Downloading the MediaConvert billing report
<a name="billing-report-download"></a>

You can download a billing report as a comma-separated values (CSV) file. In addition to costs, the report also shows the total number of Normalized Transcoding Minutes for your jobs.

**To download the billing report**

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console and open the MediaConvert console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconvert/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconvert/).

1. In the title bar, choose your username or account ID, and then choose **Billing and Cost Management**. 

1. In the navigation pane, choose **Bills**.

1. Under **Amazon Web Services, Inc. charges by services**, **Filter by service name or region name**, enter `Service name = Elemental MediaConvert`

1. At the top of the page, choose **Download all to CSV**.
