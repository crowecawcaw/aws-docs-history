# Sending email on behalf of someone else

You can send email on another user's behalf if they've made you their delegate.

###### To send email on behalf of someone else

1. In the Amazon WorkMail web application, on the shortcut bar, choose the mail
   icon.
2. On the menu bar, choose **+ New item**, **New
   email**.

###### Tip

You can also choose the plus sign (**+**) on the tab bar. 3. Under the **To** field, choose
**From**. 4. For **From**, enter the name of the person for whom you are
sending email. 5. In the contents pane, type your message and choose
**Send**.
The mailbox owner appears in the **From:** header, and you appear in
the **Sender:** header.

###### Note

If you have not been granted delegation permissions for the person you specified
in the **From** field, you receive an email message in your own
inbox indicating that you don't have the required permissions.
