# Enabling journaling

You enable journaling to record your email communication. When using journaling,
you typically use integrated third-party archiving and eDiscovery tools. Journaling
helps ensure that you meet compliance regulations for data storage, privacy
protection, and information protection.

For more information, including how-to steps, see [Using email journaling with Amazon WorkMail](journaling_overview.md "journaling_overview.md") in the
_Getting started_ section of this guide.
