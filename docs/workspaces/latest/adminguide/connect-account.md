# Update the AD Connector account (AD Connector) for WorkSpaces Personal

You can update the AD Connector account that is used to read users and groups
and join WorkSpaces machine accounts to your AD Connector directory.

###### To update the AD Connector account

1. Open the WorkSpaces console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/workspaces/v2/home](https://console.aws.amazon.com/workspaces/v2/home "https://console.aws.amazon.com/workspaces/v2/home").
2. In the navigation pane, choose **Directories**.
3. Select your directory and then choose **View details**.
4. Under AD connector account, choose **Edit**.
5. Enter the sign-in credentials for the new account.
6. Choose **Save**.
