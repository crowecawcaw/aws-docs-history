# Transcribing numbers

Amazon Transcribe Medical transcribes digits as numbers instead of words. For example, the spoken number
"one thousand two hundred forty-two" is transcribed as
`1242`.

Numbers are transcribed according to the following rules.

| Rule                                                                                                                                               | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Convert cardinal numbers greater than 10 to numbers.                                                                                               | <br>• "Fifty five" > 55 <br>• "a hundred" > 100 <br>• "One thousand and thirty one" > 1031 <br>• "One hundred twenty-three million four hundred fifty six thousand seven hundred eight nine" > 123,456,789 |
| Convert cardinal numbers followed by "million" or "billion" to numbers followed by a word when "million" or "billion" is not followed by a number. | <br>• "one hundred million" > 100 million <br>• "one billion" > 1 billion <br>• "two point three million" > 2.3 million                                                                                    |
| Convert ordinal numbers greater than 10 to numbers.                                                                                                | <br>• "Forty third" > 43rd <br>• "twenty sixth avenue" > 26th avenue                                                                                                                                       |
| Convert fractions to their numeric format.                                                                                                         | <br>• "a quarter" > 1/4 <br>• "three sixteenths" > 3/16 <br>• "a half" > 1/2 <br>• "a hundredth" > 1/100                                                                                                   |
| Convert numbers less than 10 to digits if there are more than one in a row.                                                                        | <br>• "three four five" > 345 <br>• "My phone number is four two five five five five one two one two" > 4255551212                                                                                         |
| Decimals are indicated by "dot" or "point."                                                                                                        | <br>• "three hundred and three dot five" > 303.5 <br>• "three point twenty three" > 3.23 <br>• "zero point four" > 0.4 <br>• "point three" > 0.3                                                           |
| Convert the word "percent" after a number to a percent sign.                                                                                       | <br>• "twenty three percent" > 23% <br>• "twenty three point four five percent" > 23.45%                                                                                                                   |
| Convert the words "dollar," "US dollar," "Australian dollar, "AUD," or "USD" after a number to a dollar symbol before the number.                  | <br>• "one dollar and fifteen cents" > $1.15 <br>• "twenty three USD" > $23 <br>• "twenty three Australian dollars" > $23                                                                                  |
| Convert the words "pounds," or "milligrams" to "lbs" or "mg".                                                                                      | <br>• "twenty three pounds" > 23 lbs <br>• "forty-five milligrams" > 45 mg                                                                                                                                 |
| Convert the words "rupees," "Indian rupees," or "INR" after a number to rupee sign (₹) before the number.                                          | <br>• "twenty three rupees" > ₹23 <br>• "fifty rupees thirty paise" > ₹50.30                                                                                                                               |
| Convert times to numbers.                                                                                                                          | <br>• "seven a m eastern standard time" > 7 a.m. eastern standard time <br>• "twelve thirty p m" > 12:30 p.m.                                                                                              |
| Combine years expressed as two digits into four. Only valid for the 20th, 21st, and 22nd centuries.                                                | <br>• "nineteen sixty two" > 1962 <br>• "the year is twenty twelve" > the year is 2012 <br>• "twenty nineteen" > 2019 <br>• "twenty one thirty" > 2130                                                     |
| Convert dates to numbers.                                                                                                                          | <br>• "May fifth twenty twelve" > May 5th 2012 <br>• "May five twenty twelve" > May 5 2012 <br>• "five May twenty twelve" > 5 May 2012                                                                     |
| Separate spans of numbers by the word "to."                                                                                                        | <br>• "twenty three to thirty seven" > 23 to 37                                                                                                                                                            |
