# Archiving a Detective Investigations report

When you complete your investigation in Amazon Detective, you can
**Archive** the investigation report. An archived investigation
indicates you have completed reviewing the investigation.

You can archive or unarchive an investigation only if you are a Detective Administrator. Detective will store your archived investigations for 90 days.

###### To archive an investigations report from the Reports table.

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console. Then open the Detective console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/").
2. In the navigation pane, choose **Investigations**.
3. Select an investigation, from the **Reports** table, and
   choose **Archive**.

###### To archive an investigations report from the summary page.

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console. Then open the Detective console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/detective/").
2. In the navigation pane, choose **Investigations**.
3. Select an investigation, from the **Reports** table.
4. In the investigations summary page, choose
   **Archive**.
