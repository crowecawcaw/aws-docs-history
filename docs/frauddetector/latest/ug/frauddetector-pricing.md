# Pricing

With Amazon Fraud Detector, you pay only for what you
use. There are no minimum fees or upfront commitments. You're charged based on the compute hours
that are used to train and host your models, the amount of storage you use, and
the quantity of fraud predictions that you make. For more information, see [Amazon Fraud Detector pricing](https://aws.amazon.com/fraud-detector/pricing/ "https://aws.amazon.com/fraud-detector/pricing/").
