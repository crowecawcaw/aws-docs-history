# Viewing the models deployed to a station

You can use the Defect Detection Station App to view the models that you have deployed to a station. The name of a model
is the name you gave the model in [Training your model](dda-train-model.md "dda-train-model.md").

###### To view the models on a station (Station App)

1. Open the Station App on your edge device by opening a browser and navigating to
   `x.x.x.x`:3000. Change `x.x.x.x` to the IP address of
   your edge device.
2. On the left of the application, choose **management** and then **Deployed models**.
3. In the **Deployed models** section, view the models that you have deployed to the device.
