# Request a quote for AWS Elemental appliances and software

To request a quote for AWS Elemental appliances and software, contact your account manager, or
use your active AWS account to contact Sales.

###### To request a quote

- If you have an account manager, contact your account manager to discuss your
  needs. After your discussion, your account manager will email you one or more
  quotes. The email message contains copies of the quotes you requested and a link to
  view those quotes in the AWS Management Console.

If you don’t have an account manager and are interested in receiving quotes, visit
[https://console.aws.amazon.com/elemental-appliances-software/home](https://console.aws.amazon.com/elemental-appliances-software/home "https://console.aws.amazon.com/elemental-appliances-software/home"), and
then select **Contact Sales**.

## Next steps

After you receive your quote from your account manager, review the quote to confirm it
or to request changes. For details, see [Review your
quote for AWS Elemental appliances and software](access-and-confirm-your-quote.md "access-and-confirm-your-quote.md").
