

# Manage workloads in Incident Detection and Response
<a name="manage-workloads-idr"></a>

A key part of effective incident management is having the right processes and procedures in place to onboard, test, and maintain your monitored workloads. This section covers the essential steps, including developing comprehensive runbooks and response plans to guide your teams through incidents, thoroughly testing and validating new workloads, requesting changes to update workload monitoring, and properly offboarding workloads when required.
