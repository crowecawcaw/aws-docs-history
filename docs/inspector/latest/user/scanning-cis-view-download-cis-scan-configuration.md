

# Downloading a CIS scan results
<a name="scanning-cis-view-download-cis-scan-configuration"></a>

 You can download a PDF or CSV of a CIS scan using the Amazon Inspector console or API. 

**Note**  
 You can only download a CSV file of your CIS scan results for CIS scans collected after 05/03/2024. 

 This topic describes how to download a CIS scan using the Amazon Inspector console. 

**To download CIS scan results from the console**

1.  Sign in using your credentials, and then open the Amazon Inspector console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/inspector/v2/home](https://console.aws.amazon.com/inspector/v2/home). 

1.  Use the AWS Region dropdown to select the AWS Region where you created your CIS scan configuration. 

1.  From the navigation pane, choose **On-demand scans**, and then choose **CIS scans**. 

1.  Choose the **Scan results** tab. 

1.  Under the **Scheduled By** column, choose the scan schedule ID that you want to view. Or select the row with the scan schedule ID that you want to view, and then choose **View details**. 

1.  Choose **Download**, and then choose **PDF** or **CSV**. If your account is the delegated administrator account, you can choose **Select account** to download results for a specific member account.
