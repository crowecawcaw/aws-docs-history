# Appearance

Super administrators can manage and customize the appearance of Wickr for their
network.

**To customize the appearance of Wickr**

1. In the navigation pane of the Wickr Super Administrator Console, choose
   **Appearance**.
2. On the **Appearance** page, choose **Edit** in the top
   right corner of the **Customize login screen** section.
3. Under **Add a custom logo**, choose **Upload Image** to
   upload a logo image.
4. In the **Add a custom text** dialog box, enter a description for your
   admin console login screen.
5. Choose **Save**.
