# Delete a room in the Wickr client
<a name="room-delete"></a>

You can delete a room or group in the Wickr client.

Complete the following steps to delete a room.

**Note**  
Groups cannot be deleted.

1. Sign in to the Wickr client. For more information, see [Sign in to the Wickr client](getting-started.md#sign-in-step2).

1. In the navigation pane, locate and choose the name of the room you want to delete.

1. Choose ![Information icon](http://docs.aws.amazon.com/wickr/latest/userguide/images/icon-wickr-room-information.png) in the room window to view room details.

1. In the **Room Details** pane that appears, scroll down and choose **Delete Room**.

1. In the confirmation prompt that appears, choose **Delete** to confirm that you want to delete the room or choose **Cancel** to not delete it.
