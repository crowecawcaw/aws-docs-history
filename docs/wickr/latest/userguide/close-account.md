

This guide provides documentation for AWS Wickr. For Wickr Enterprise, which is the on-premises version of Wickr, see [Enterprise Administration Guide](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wickr/latest/enterpriseadminguide/what-is-wickr.html).

# Close a guest user account in the Wickr client
<a name="close-account"></a>

You can close a guest user account on AWS Wickr.

Complete the following procedure to close a guest user account.

1. Sign in to the Wickr client. For more information, see [Sign in to the Wickr client](getting-started.md#sign-in-step2).

1. In the navigation pane, choose the menu icon (![Menu icon](http://docs.aws.amazon.com/wickr/latest/userguide/images/icon-wickr-settings-hamburger.png)), and then choose **Support**. 

1. Choose **Close Account**.

1. In the **Close Your Account** pane that appears, choose one of the following options (optional):
   + It's missing important features
   + I need help to better use Wickr
   + I'm not satisfied with the product quality
   + I have privacy/security concerns
   + Other

1. Choose **Continue**.

1. In the **Close Your Account** pane that appears, choose **Close Account**.

1. In the next **Close Your Account** pane that appears, enter your password in the text field.

1. Choose **Sign in**.

1. In the pop up window, choose **Close Account**.
