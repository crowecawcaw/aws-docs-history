Administration Guide](../adminguide-classic/what-is-wickr.md "../adminguide-classic/what-is-wickr.md").

# Manage users in AWS Wickr

In the **User management** section of the AWS Management Console for Wickr you can view
current Wickr users and bots, and modify their details.
