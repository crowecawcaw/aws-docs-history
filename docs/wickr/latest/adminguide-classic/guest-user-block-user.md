# Block a guest user in AWS Wickr network

You can block and unblock a guest user in your Wickr network. Blocked users can't communicate with anyone in your network.

**To block a guest user**

1. Open the AWS Management Console for Wickr at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/").
2. On the **Networks** page, choose the
   **Admin** link, to navigate to the Wickr Admin Console for
   that network.
3. In the navigation pane of the Wickr Admin Console, choose
   **User**, and then choose **Guest
   Users**.
4. On the **Guest Users** page, choose the **Guest
   Users** section.
5. The **Guest Users** section shows the guest users that have
   communicated in your Wickr network.
6. In the **Guest Users** section, find the email of the guest
   user you want to block.
7. On the right-hand side of the guest user's name, select the three dots, and
   choose **Block**.
8. Choose **Block** on the pop-up window.
9. To view the list of blocked users in your Wickr network, choose the
   **Blocked Users** section.
   **To unblock a guest user**

10. Open the AWS Management Console for Wickr at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/").
11. On the **Networks** page, choose the
    **Admin** link, to navigate to the Wickr Admin Console for
    that network.
12. In the navigation pane of the Wickr Admin Console, choose
    **User**, and then choose **Guest
    Users**.
13. On the **Guest Users** page, choose the **Blocked
    Users** section.
14. The **Blocked Users** section shows the guest users that are
    blocked in your Wickr network.
15. In the **Blocked Users** section, find the email of the guest
    user you want to unblock.
16. On the right-hand side of the guest user's name, select the three dots, and
    choose **Unblock**.
17. Choose **Unblock** in the pop-up window.
