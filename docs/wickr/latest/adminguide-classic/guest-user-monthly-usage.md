# View monthly usage in AWS Wickr network
<a name="guest-user-monthly-usage"></a>

You can view the number of guest users your network has communicated with during a billing period.

Complete the following procedure to view your monthly usage for your Wickr network.

1. Open the AWS Management Console for Wickr at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/wickr/).

1. On the **Networks** page, choose the **Admin** link, to navigate to the Wickr Admin Console for that network.

1. In the navigation pane of the Wickr Admin Console, choose **User**, and then choose **Guest Users**.

1. On the **Guest Users** page, choose the **Monthly Usage** section.
**Note**  
Guest billing data is updated every 24 hours.
