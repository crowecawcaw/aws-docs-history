# Update a portal in API Gateway

You can update the theme, logo, or the portal products in your portal. When you update your portal, the changes
don't take effect until you republish your portal.

## Update a portal

The following procedure shows how to update the design of a portal.

###### To update a portal design

1. Sign in to the API Gateway console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway](https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway "https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway").
2. In the main navigation pane, choose
   **Portals**.
3. Choose a portal.
4. In the **Portal design** tab, choose **Edit**.
5. To update the portal logo, choose **Update portal logo**. You can either remove the
   existing logo or use a new logo.
6. Make any theme or background updates to your portal design.
7. Choose **Save changes**.
8. (Optional) Choose **Generate portal preview** to see your changes.

After you've confirmed your changes, publish your portal. 9. Choose **Publish portal**. 10. Enter a description for your portal update and choose **Publish**.
