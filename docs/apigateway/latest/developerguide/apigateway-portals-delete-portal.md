# Delete a portal in API Gateway

When you delete a portal, it cannot be recovered and you must disable it first. If you want to remove your
portal from the web, you can disable it. This lets you modify your portal and republish it later.

## Delete a portal

The following procedure shows how to delete a portal.

###### To delete a portal

1. Sign in to the API Gateway console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway](https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway "https://console.aws.amazon.com/apigateway").
2. In the main navigation pane, choose
   **Portals**.
3. Choose a portal.
4. If your portal is not disabled, disable it by choosing **Actions**,
   **Disable portal**.

It takes a few minutes for your portal to be disabled. You can monitor the **Publish
status** to see when your portal publish status is disabled. 5. After you have unpublished your portal, you can delete it. To delete your portal, choose **Actions**,
**Delete portal**. Confirm your choice and choose **Delete**.
