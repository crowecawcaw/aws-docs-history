# Viewing content quality analysis settings and alerts

When you enable content quality analysis, MediaConnect starts posting warnings and
alerts for the enabled metrics in your AWS account. You can confirm your content
quality analysis settings, and view any warnings and alerts for the flows and router
inputs in your account.
