

# Updating an endpoint in MediaConnect
<a name="updating-endpoint"></a>

You can update the settings of a registered endpoint as your workflow changes.

## Prerequisites
<a name="updating-endpoint-prerequisites"></a>

The following procedures assume that you have at least one endpoint in your AWS account.

## Procedure
<a name="updating-endpoint-procedure"></a>

### To update a source endpoint
<a name="update-source-endpoint-section"></a><a name="update-source-endpoint-procedure"></a>

1. Open the [MediaConnect console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconnect/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Endpoints**, choose **Source endpoints**.

1. Select the source endpoint that you want to update.

1. Choose **Edit**.

1. Change the settings as needed.

1. Choose **Save changes**.

### To update a destination endpoint
<a name="update-destination-endpoint-section"></a><a name="update-destination-endpoint-procedure"></a>

1. Open the [MediaConnect console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconnect/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Endpoints**, choose **Destination endpoints**.

1. Select the destination endpoint that you want to update.

1. Choose **Edit**.

1. Change the settings as needed.

1. Choose **Save changes**.
