

# Viewing endpoints in MediaConnect
<a name="viewing-endpoints"></a>

You can view your source and destination endpoints in the MediaConnect console. For each endpoint, you can see its configuration details and its registration and connection states.

## Prerequisites
<a name="viewing-endpoints-prerequisites"></a>

The following procedures assume that you have at least one endpoint in your AWS account.

## Procedure
<a name="viewing-endpoints-procedure"></a>

### To view your source endpoints
<a name="view-source-endpoints-section"></a><a name="view-source-endpoints-procedure"></a>

1. Open the [MediaConnect console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconnect/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Endpoints**, choose **Source endpoints**.

1. Review the list of your source endpoints.

1. Select a source endpoint to see its details.

### To view your destination endpoints
<a name="view-destination-endpoints-section"></a><a name="view-destination-endpoints-procedure"></a>

1. Open the [MediaConnect console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/mediaconnect/).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Endpoints**, choose **Destination endpoints**.

1. Review the list of your destination endpoints.

1. Select a destination endpoint to see its details.
