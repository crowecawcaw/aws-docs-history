# Monitoring flow and source

health

On the AWS Elemental MediaConnect console, you can monitor the health of your flows and their
sources.

_Flow_ health indicates if your flow is not connected
due to an issue with an entitlement or encryption.

_Source_ health indicates if your source is
connected. If it is, the console shows Amazon CloudWatch metrics that provide the status of the
source over a period of time.
