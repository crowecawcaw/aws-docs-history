# Amazon MSK Standard brokers

Standard brokers for MSK Provisioned offer the most flexibility to configure your
cluster's performance. You can choose from a wide range cluster configurations to
achieve the availability, durability, throughput, and latency characteristics required
for your applications. You can also provision storage capacity and increase it as
needed. Amazon MSK handles the hardware maintenance of Standard brokers and attached storage
resources, automatically repairing hardware issues that may arise. You can find more
details in this document about various topics related to Standard brokers, including
topics on [storage management](msk-storage-management.md "msk-storage-management.md"), [configurations](msk-configuration-standard.md "msk-configuration-standard.md"), and [maintenance](patching-impact.md#patching-standard-brokers "patching-impact.md#patching-standard-brokers").
