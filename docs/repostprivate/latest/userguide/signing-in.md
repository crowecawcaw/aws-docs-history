# Sign in to your private re:Post

To sign in to your private re:Post for the first time, open the email from your re:Post Private console administrator. Then, choose the link that's provided in the email to sign in to the private re:Post. You must use your AWS IAM Identity Center or AWS Active Directory credentials to sign in to re:Post Private.

You might need to take additional steps to allow your browser to support single sign-on. For more information, see [Single sign-on for IE and Chrome](../../../directoryservice/latest/admin-guide/ms_ad_single_sign_on.md#ie_sso "../../../directoryservice/latest/admin-guide/ms_ad_single_sign_on.md#ie_sso") and [Single sign-on for Firefox](../../../directoryservice/latest/admin-guide/ms_ad_single_sign_on.md#firefox_sso "../../../directoryservice/latest/admin-guide/ms_ad_single_sign_on.md#firefox_sso") in the _AWS Directory Service Administration Guide_.
