# Publish an article in your private re:Post
<a name="publish-article"></a>

Follow these steps to publish an article in your private re:Post:

1. From any page in your private re:Post application, choose **Post content**. Then, choose **Publish article**.

   -or-

   From any page in your private re:Post application, choose **Publish article**.

1. For **Article title**, enter a title.

1. For **Context and purpose**, enter a short description.

1. For **Article body**, enter your content.

1. For **Channel**, select the channel where you want to publish the article. If you don't want to select a specific channel, select **Default**.

   You don't see this option if you aren't a user in any channel within your private re:Post.

1. For **Your organization-specific tags**, select custom tags that apply to the article.

1. For **AWS Tags**, select all tags that apply to the article.

1. For **Content level**, select the content level that applies to your article.

1. Choose **Publish article**.

The article that you published is now displayed under the **Articles** tab. The users in your private re:Post can view, upvote, and downvote your article. They can also add comments to your article that are displayed in the **Comments on this article** section.
