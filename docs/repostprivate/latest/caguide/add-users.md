# Add users to your private re:Post
<a name="add-users"></a>

If you're an administrator, you can add users to your private re:Post.

1. Open the re:Post Private console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/repost-private/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/repost-private/).

1. In the navigation pane, choose **All my private re:Posts**.

1. Choose the private re:Post that you want to manage.

1. Choose the **Users** tab.

1. Under **Users**, choose **Add users and groups**.

1. From the list, select the users that you want to add to your private re:Post. Then, choose **Assign**.

The selected users are added to your private re:Post and listed under the **Users** tab.

The users that you've added will receive an onboarding email from your private re:Post. Your private re:Post reviews the list of users and groups once every day to make sure that an onboarding email is sent to those that didn't already receive one. The onboarding email contains information on how to sign in to your private re:Post.
