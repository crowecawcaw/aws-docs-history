# Remove users from your private re:Post
<a name="remove-users-groups"></a>

If you're an administrator, then you can remove users from your private re:Post.

1. Open the re:Post Private console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/repost-private/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/repost-private/).

1. In the navigation pane, choose **All my private re:Posts**.

1. Choose the private re:Post that you want to manage.

1. Under **Users**, from the list, select the users that you want to remove from your private re:Post. Then, choose **Remove**.

The selected users are removed from your private re:Post. Information about the removed users no longer appears under the **Users** tab.
