# Delete blocked terminology from your

private re:Post

As an AWS re:Post Private administrator, you can delete blocked terminologies from your
private re:Post.

Follow these steps to delete blocked terms from your private re:Post:

1. In your re:Post Private web application, choose the user profile, and then choose **Administration**.
2. Choose **Content classification**.
3. Choose **Blocked terminology**.
4. From the list, select the terms that you want to delete from the private re:Post.
5. Choose **Remove blocked terminology**.
   The blocked terms that you removed are no longer displayed in the list under the **Blocked terminology** section.
