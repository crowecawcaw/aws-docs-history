# Update custom topics in your private re:Post

As an AWS re:Post Private administrator, you can update the custom topics in your
private re:Post.

Follow these steps to update the topics in your private re:Post:

1. In your re:Post Private web application, choose your profile, and then choose **Administration**.
2. Choose **Content classification**.
3. Choose **Topics**.
4. From the list, select the topic that you want to update.
5. Choose **Edit topic**.
6. In the **Edit topic** dialog box, update the topic details as needed. Then, choose **Save changes**.

###### Note

    * You can't start the topics with `AWS` or
     `Amazon`.
    * You can't enter duplicate topics.

The updated topics are displayed under the **Topics** section.
