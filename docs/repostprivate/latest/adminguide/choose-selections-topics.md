# Choose your topics of interest for selections

in your private re:Post

To choose your topics of interest for selections, follow these steps:

1. In your private re:Post application, choose the user profile, and then choose **Administration**.
2. Choose **Topics of interest**.
3. From the list, select your topics of interest for selections that you want to display on the home page.

You can also use the dropdown list to select topics of your interest. 4. Choose **Update**.
All selections with the selected topics are added to the home page for your private re:Post.
