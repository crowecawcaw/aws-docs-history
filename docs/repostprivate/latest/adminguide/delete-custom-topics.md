# Delete custom topics from your

private re:Post

As an AWS re:Post Private administrator, you can delete existing custom topics from your
private re:Post.

Follow these steps to delete custom topics from your private re:Post:

1. In your re:Post Private web application, choose your profile, and then choose **Administration**.
2. Choose **Content classification**.
3. Choose **Topics**.
4. From the list, select the topics that you want to delete from the private re:Post.
5. Choose **Delete topic**.
   The topics that you deleted are no longer displayed under the **Topics** section.
