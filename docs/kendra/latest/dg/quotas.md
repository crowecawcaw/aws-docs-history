# Quotas for Amazon Kendra

## Supported regions

For a list of AWS regions where Amazon Kendra is available, see
[Amazon Kendra
regions and endpoints](../../../general/latest/gr/kendra.md "../../../general/latest/gr/kendra.md") in the _Amazon Web Services General
Reference_.

###### Important

Amazon Kendra GenAI Enterprise Edition indices are only available in the
US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon),
Europe (Ireland), Asia Pacific (Sydney), regions.

## Quotas

Service quotas, also referred to as limits, are the maximum number of service
resources for your AWS account. For more information, see [Amazon Kendra service
quotas](../../../general/latest/gr/kendra.md "../../../general/latest/gr/kendra.md") in the _AWS General
Reference_.

###### Note

Quotas for Amazon Kendra GenAI Enterprise Edition indices aren't currently available in
the Service Quotas console.
