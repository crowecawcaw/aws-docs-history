# Encryption in transit

Amazon Kendra uses the HTTPS protocol to communicate with your client application.
It uses HTTPS and AWS signatures to communicate with other services on your
application's behalf. If you use a VPC, you can use AWS PrivateLink to establish a private
connection between your VPC and Amazon Kendra.
