

# Demand Center

You use Demand Center to run co-marketing campaigns with AWS from the AWS Partner Central console. A campaign bundles a set of assets, such as email messages, landing pages, social posts, and documents, with the audience, timing, and budget that they run with. You can use campaigns for webinars, nurture sequences, product launches, and executive events.

You can start a campaign from an AWS Partner Ready template or have AI draft its assets. A template is the faster path for a standard campaign. AI is the better fit when no template matches your message.

Demand Center opens from the AWS Partner Central console. The overview page lists your campaigns and marketing activities, and its **Settings** tab holds the social media connections that campaigns post from.

Demand Center uses the following concepts:
+ **Campaign** – A set of assets, with the audience, timing, and budget that they run with. A campaign is **Draft** while no asset is live, **Live** once at least one asset is live, and **Completed** after its end date passes in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC). A completed campaign keeps its live assets live, but you can't change its details.
+ **Asset** – A touchpoint that reaches your audience, such as an email message, a landing page, a social post, or a document. You configure each asset, and Demand Center publishes it when you launch the campaign.
+ **Marketing activity** – A unit of work in your business plan, such as a campaign, an event sponsorship, or a webinar. When you record your work as activities, you and your Partner Development Manager (PDM) can measure progress against your plan.
+ **Business plan** – The plan that you build with your PDM in Partner Business Planning. Its marketing initiatives group related activities.
+ **Marketing Development Funds (MDF)** – AWS funds that can co-fund your marketing activities. You request MDF in AWS Partner Central.
+ **Agency Connect** – A directory of marketing agencies that AWS approved to run campaigns funded with MDF.
+ **Social connections** – The LinkedIn, X, and Facebook accounts that your campaigns publish posts from. A connection belongs to your AWS Partner Central account, so every campaign can use it.

Demand Center provides help panels in the console. To open the help panel for a page, choose **Info** next to a heading.
