

# Setting campaign details

The **Campaign details** step sets who the campaign targets, when it runs, what it costs, and the company information that appears in the footer of your assets.

1. Set the audience. The industries and company size ranges decide who the campaign reaches. For **Target regions**, select whole regions or individual countries, up to 100 countries.

1. Set the timing. The end date decides when the campaign completes. After it passes in Coordinated Universal Time (UTC), you can't change the campaign. The start date is optional.

1. Set the budget. For **Estimated MDF budget (USD)**, enter the amount of AWS Marketing Development Funds (MDF) that you expect to use. For **Total estimated budget (USD)**, enter your own spend and any contingency. The total must be at least the MDF estimate. Both are planning estimates and don't submit an MDF request.

1. Enter your company information. This information appears in the footer of the campaign's email messages, landing pages, and documents.

   1. For **Privacy policy URL**, enter the page of your privacy policy, not your home page. You need this field to publish the assets.

   1. For **Postal address**, enter an address of up to 200 characters on one line. You need this field to publish the assets.

   1. (Optional) For **Partner badge**, upload a PNG or JPEG file of up to 2 MB. Until you upload a badge, the campaign shows the AWS Partner badge.

1. Choose **Save and next**.
