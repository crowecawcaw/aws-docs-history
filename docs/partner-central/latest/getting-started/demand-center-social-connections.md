

# Connecting social media accounts to Demand Center

With social connections, you can publish your campaign posts on LinkedIn, X, or Facebook from Demand Center. A connection belongs to your AWS Partner Central account, so every campaign can use it. Each campaign then chooses which connected account it posts from. You manage connections on the **Settings** tab of the Demand Center overview.

You approve each connection on the platform's own site. The platform opens in a new tab, where you sign in and approve access. For Facebook, you approve only the one Page that posts publish to.
