# Becoming an AWS Partner

The AWS Partner Network (APN) is a global community of partners that offers programs, expertise, and resources to
build, market, and sell partner offerings. Whether you are just beginning to build or looking to
expand your business, you can join the APN at no cost and use AWS trainings, enablement resources,
Well-Architected tools, and more.

For instructions to become an AWS Partner, refer to [Join the AWS Partner Network](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/").
