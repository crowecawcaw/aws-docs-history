

# Reviewing and launching a campaign

On the **Review and submit** step, **Launch campaign** publishes the campaign's pages and documents, and sends or schedules its email messages and social posts. Email messages go to every valid address in your lead list, from the sender that you set.

**Warning**  
An email message or post can't be recalled after it goes out, so check the sender, the recipients, and each send time before you launch. You can unschedule a scheduled email message or post until 1 hour before its send time.

If you chose to distribute the campaign yourself, the step shows **Download assets** instead of **Launch campaign**, and Demand Center sends nothing.

1. On the **Review and submit** step, review the campaign's details, assets, and send times.

1. Choose **Launch campaign**.

After you launch, the campaign page shows each asset's publication status. For more information about managing campaigns, see [Managing campaigns in Demand Center](demand-center-manage-campaigns.md).
