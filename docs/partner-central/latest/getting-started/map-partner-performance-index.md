

# Performance Indicators
<a name="map-partner-performance-index"></a>

Performance Indicators is a performance framework that shares with you the key criteria and attributes that AWS believes drive customer success with partner migration delivery. With Performance Indicators, you can see how you are delivering against the things that matter most for mutual customer success: win conversion, delivery speed, and migration execution quality. You get a clear view of where you stand, which enables a two-way dialogue about how you and AWS can work together to drive great outcomes for customers.

**Note**  
While Performance Indicators currently includes performance for MAP 2.0 engagements only, AWS plans to evolve Performance Indicators to include additional programs in early 2027. Performance Indicators was previously called *Partner Performance Index*, or *PPI*. AWS updated the name to Performance Indicators in September 2026.

## Why Performance Indicators matters for your business
<a name="ppi-business-value"></a>

Performance Indicators helps you build a stronger migration practice by providing transparent, data-driven insights that benefit both your organization and your customers.

Performance Indicators provides the following benefits for your organization:
+ Clear visibility into your migration practice performance
+ Identification of specific areas for improvement
+ Recognition and rewards for top performers (details announced December 2026)
+ Targeted support where you need it most
+ Competitive advantage in the marketplace

Performance Indicators provides the following benefits for your customers:
+ Access to proven, high-performing migration partners
+ Higher quality project execution and delivery
+ Greater confidence in project outcomes

## How Performance Indicators supports your growth
<a name="ppi-growth-support"></a>

Performance Indicators is a growth tool, not just a measurement system. With Performance Indicators, you get the following:
+ Real-time visibility into your metrics and performance trends that you can use to identify improvement opportunities and improve your migration practice
+ Targeted support from AWS in the specific areas identified by your opportunities
+ Greater customer trust and, through it, more business
+ More recommendations from AWS
+ Reduced administrative overhead for proven excellence
