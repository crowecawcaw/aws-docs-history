

# Retrying, unscheduling, and downloading assets

From the campaign's assets table, you can act on an asset by its status:
+ **Retry publishing** – Publishes a failed asset again, which can fix a temporary problem.
+ **Unschedule** – Withdraws a live email message or social post before its send time, so it doesn't go out. It goes out again only after you edit it and launch the campaign. An asset that already went out, or goes out in less than 1 hour, can't be unscheduled.
+ **Download assets** – Downloads the campaign's finished assets so that you can distribute them yourself.

1. On the Demand Center overview, open the campaign.

1. In the assets table, select the asset.

1. Choose **Retry publishing**, **Unschedule**, or **Download assets**.
