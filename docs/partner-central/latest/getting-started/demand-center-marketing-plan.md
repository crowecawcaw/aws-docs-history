

# Tracking marketing activities in the Marketing Plan

The Marketing Plan shows the marketing activities in your business plan and the campaign behind each one. Your business plan is the plan that you build with your Partner Development Manager (PDM) in Partner Business Planning, and its marketing initiatives group the related activities.

Use the Marketing Plan to track your activities, add activities and campaigns to your plan, and request Marketing Development Funds (MDF) to run them. To add work to your plan, use the **Create** menu and choose **Create campaign** or **Create marketing activity**. New activities and campaigns start with the plan and initiative that you're viewing.
