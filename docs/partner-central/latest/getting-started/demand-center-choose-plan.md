

# Choosing a business plan and initiative

The business plan and initiative that you choose decide which activities appear. **All initiatives** shows the whole plan. Only activities at the market stage of your business plan appear.

The plan's status decides whether it accepts new activities:
+ **Draft** and **In review** – You and your PDM are preparing the plan.
+ **Approved** – The plan is active and accepts new activities.
+ **Canceled** and **Completed** – The plan is closed and doesn't accept new activities.

You create and change plans in Partner Business Planning. To open it, choose **Manage business plan**.

In the activities table, the **Campaign** column links to the campaign built for an activity. An activity without a campaign is work that you run outside Demand Center, such as an event sponsorship. To request funds for an activity, select it and choose **Request MDF**, which opens AWS Partner Central in a new tab. Request funds before the activity starts, so the request can be reviewed in time.
