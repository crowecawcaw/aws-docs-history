

# What is Performance Indicators?
<a name="ppi-overview"></a>

Performance Indicators is a performance framework that shares with partners the key criteria and attributes that AWS believes drive customer success with partner migration delivery. It provides visibility into how partners are delivering against the things that matter most for mutual customer success: win conversion, delivery speed, and migration execution quality. Performance Indicators gives every partner a clear view of where they stand, enabling a two-way dialogue about how AWS and partners can work together to drive great outcomes for customers. While Performance Indicators currently includes performance for MAP 2.0 engagements only, we will continue to evolve Performance Indicators to include additional programs in early 2027. Performance Indicators was previously called Partner Performance Index, or PPI; we updated the name to Performance Indicators in September 2026.

## Why Performance Indicators matters for your business
<a name="ppi-business-value"></a>

Performance Indicators helps you build a stronger migration practice by providing transparent, data-driven insights that benefit both your organization and your customers:

**For your organization:**
+ Clear visibility into your migration practice performance
+ Identification of specific areas for improvement
+ Recognition and rewards for top performers (details announced December 2026)
+ Targeted support where you need it most
+ Competitive advantage in the marketplace

**For your customers:**
+ Access to proven, high-performing migration partners
+ Higher quality project execution and delivery
+ Greater confidence in project outcomes

## How Performance Indicators supports your growth
<a name="ppi-growth-support"></a>

Performance Indicators is designed as a growth tool, not just a measurement system. The mechanism provides you:
+ Real-time visibility in your metrics and performance trends which you can use to identify improvement opportunities and improve migration practice
+ Based on your opportunities, get targeted support on the specific areas from AWS
+ Gain customers' trust and through that more business
+ An opportunity to have more recommendations from AWS
+ Reduce administrative overhead for proven excellence
