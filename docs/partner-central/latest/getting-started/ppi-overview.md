# What is the MAP Partner Performance Index?

The MAP Partner Performance Index (PPI) is a comprehensive measurement system that evaluates partner effectiveness in delivering successful AWS migration projects. PPI provides you with transparent, data-driven insights into your migration practice performance and helps you identify opportunities for improvement.

## Why PPI matters for your business

The PPI helps you build a stronger migration practice by providing transparent, data-driven insights that benefit both your organization and your customers.

**For your organization:**

- Clear visibility into your migration practice performance
- Identification of specific areas for improvement
- Recognition and rewards for excellence
- Accelerated approval processes for top performers
- Targeted support where you need it most
- Competitive advantage in the marketplace

**For your customers:**

- Access to proven, high-performing migration partners
- Faster migration completion and value realization
- Higher quality project execution and delivery
- Greater confidence in project outcomes
- Better overall migration experience

## How PPI supports your growth

PPI is designed as a growth tool, not just a measurement system. Based on your performance tier, you'll receive:

- **Performance insights** – Real-time visibility into your metrics and trends
- **Best practice guidance** – Learn from top performers in the program
- **Targeted support** – Get help in the specific areas where you need it
- **Recognition** – Showcase your expertise to customers and AWS teams
- **Streamlined processes** – Reduced administrative overhead for proven performers
