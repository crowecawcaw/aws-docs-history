

# Creating a campaign in Demand Center

You create a campaign with the create campaign wizard. The wizard names the campaign, sets how it's built, and steps through its assets, audience, timing, and budget. The steps that you see depend on the build method and the assets that you choose.

The wizard includes the following steps, in order:
+ **Create campaign** – Name the campaign and choose how it's built.
+ **Choose assets** (template) or **Select asset type and channels** (AI) – Choose which assets the campaign includes.
+ **Campaign details** – Set the audience, timing, budget, and company information.
+ A step for each asset, and a source step for each AI asset.
+ **Set up delivery** – Choose who distributes the campaign and who receives it.
+ **Create marketing activity** (optional) – Add the campaign to your business plan.
+ **Review and submit** – Review the campaign and launch it.

To move to the next step and save your work, choose **Save and next**.
