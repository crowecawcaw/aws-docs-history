

# Getting started with the BVR motion
<a name="bvr-getting-started"></a>

To participate in the Business Value Realization (BVR) motion, submit an application through AWS Partner Central. Your eligibility is evaluated automatically when you apply.

## Apply for the BVR motion
<a name="bvr-apply"></a>

1. Sign in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Funding benefits**, choose **Business Value Realization**.

1. Choose **Get started with BVR motion**.

1. Review the terms and conditions.

1. Select the **I accept BVR motion terms and conditions** check box.

1. Choose **Submit**.

After you submit, your application is evaluated. If your organization meets the eligibility criteria, you are enrolled and can begin nominating customers immediately. If criteria are not met, you receive guidance on what to address.

## Application outcomes
<a name="bvr-application-outcomes"></a>


| Status | Description | 
| --- | --- | 
| Approved | You can begin nominating customers. | 
| Not approved | Eligibility criteria not met. Review the feedback provided. |
