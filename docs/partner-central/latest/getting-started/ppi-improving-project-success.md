

# Improving your Project Success
<a name="ppi-improving-project-success"></a>

**Consistency across projects:**
+ Develop and follow standard migration methodologies
+ Use project templates and playbooks
+ Conduct project retrospectives to identify lessons learned
+ Replicate success patterns from your best projects
+ Share knowledge across your delivery teams

**Early intervention:**
+ Monitor project health metrics weekly
+ Identify at-risk projects early (within first 90 days)
+ Implement corrective actions immediately
+ Escalate to customer executives when needed
+ Don't let struggling projects drift—address them proactively

**Resource management:**
+ Ensure adequate staffing for each project
+ Match skilled resources to project complexity
+ Provide ongoing training for delivery teams
+ Have backup resources available for critical projects
+ Don't overcommit your team across too many simultaneous projects
