

# Drafting a VRP with the AWS Partner Central chat agent

The AWS Partner Central chat agent is a built-in AI assistant in AWS Partner Central. It helps you execute Business Value Realization (BVR) actions directly from chat, such as drafting a nomination and completing engagement activities, including drafting your Value Realization Plan (VRP) from scratch.

Your VRP is the plan you agree on with your customer at the start of the motion. It records the customer's business goals, the adoption plan, the change management and governance plan, and the baseline key performance indicators (KPIs) used to measure value at each pit stop. The chat agent gives you a structured first draft, so you don't start from a blank page. You then review and refine it with your customer before you submit.

## What the chat agent can help you with
<a name="bvr-vrp-what-it-helps"></a>
+ **Drafting your VRP** — Write a first version of your customer's business goals, adoption plan, and success criteria.
+ **Defining KPIs** — Find KPIs and baseline measurements that fit the AWS service and your customer's use case.
+ **Answering BVR questions** — Get answers about eligibility, nominations, pit stops, required activities, KPI milestones, and funding.
+ **Planning next steps** — Find out what to complete next to reach your next pit stop milestone.
+ **Executing BVR actions** — Draft nominations and complete engagement activities directly from chat.

## Accessing the AWS Partner Central chat agent
<a name="bvr-vrp-accessing"></a>

You can open the chat agent in either of two ways.

### From any page in AWS Partner Central
<a name="bvr-vrp-accessing-any-page"></a>

1. Sign in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com).

1. In the top navigation bar, next to the AWS logo, choose the AWS Partner Central chat agent icon.

### From the Business Value Realization page
<a name="bvr-vrp-accessing-bvr-page"></a>

1. Sign in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Funding benefits**, choose **Business value realization**.

1. In the top-right corner of the page, choose **Ask about BVR**.

**Note**  
If your organization isn't enrolled in the BVR motion yet, choose **Get started** on the Business Value Realization page to apply. For more information, see [Getting started with the BVR motion](bvr-getting-started.md).

## Drafting your VRP
<a name="bvr-vrp-drafting"></a>

1. Open the AWS Partner Central chat agent.

1. Ask the agent to help you draft a VRP. For example: "Help me draft a Value Realization Plan for my customer."

1. Give the agent details about your engagement when it asks, such as the customer, the AWS service being deployed, the customer's business goals, and how they measure success today.

1. Upload supporting documents to give the agent more context, such as the statement of work (SOW), meeting notes, pitch decks, and business case documents.

1. Review the draft the agent creates. Ask it to change sections, suggest other KPIs, or make the language clearer.

1. Confirm the final plan with your customer before you submit it.

## Before you submit your VRP
<a name="bvr-vrp-before-submit"></a>

The chat agent gives you a starting point, not a finished plan. Before you submit:
+ Check that the goals and KPIs reflect what your customer agreed to.
+ Make sure each baseline KPI can be measured and is supported by your customer's usage data.
+ Expect your Partner Success Specialist (PSS) to review your submission and possibly give you feedback.
