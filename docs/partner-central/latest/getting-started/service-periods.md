

# Service periods
<a name="service-periods"></a>

Service periods create mutual agreements that prevent unilateral changes to billing transfers by requiring advance notice before either party can modify or terminate the relationship. Partners establish minimum notice periods of 14, 30, 60, or 90 days, providing flexibility to align with existing service contracts and business needs. Service periods are associated with channel relationships, and provide additional governance options to channel relationships.
