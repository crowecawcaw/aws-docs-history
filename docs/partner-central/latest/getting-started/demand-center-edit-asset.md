

# Editing an asset

You can change the content of one asset. Your changes apply to this campaign only. When you choose **Update** for an asset, the campaign's create campaign wizard opens on that asset's step, which groups fields into header, body, and footer sections. Landing pages group them by page: the form page and the confirmation page.

Campaign-wide fields, such as the sender, postal address, privacy policy URL, and partner badge, are read-only when you edit an asset, because every asset in the campaign shares them.

Saving has different effects, depending on the asset:
+ A published landing page, document, or banner ad gets your changes the next time it's published.
+ A scheduled email message or social post loses its schedule. It's scheduled again when you launch the campaign.
+ An asset that is publishing, already sent, or due in less than 1 hour can't be changed.

Email messages and social posts send at least 1 hour after you schedule them.

1. On the Demand Center overview, open the campaign.

1. In the assets table, select the asset, and then choose **Update**.

1. Change the asset's fields, and then save your changes.
