

# Reconnecting an expired account

An account shows **Connection expired** when the platform no longer accepts the connection. An expired account can't publish, so reconnect it before your next launch. For Facebook, select the same Page again. Selecting a different account or Page adds a second connection, and posts can't publish while two accounts are connected.

1. On the Demand Center overview, choose the **Settings** tab.

1. In **Social connections**, select the expired account, and then choose **Reconnect**.

1. In the new tab, sign in with the same account and approve access.
