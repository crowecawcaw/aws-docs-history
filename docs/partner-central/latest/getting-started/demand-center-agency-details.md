

# Reviewing agency details and contacting an agency

The agency details page shows the following information about one agency, so you can decide whether it fits your campaign before you contact it:
+ **Capabilities** – The services the agency offers. Check that they match the campaign that you plan to run.
+ **Region** and **Languages** – Where the agency works and which languages it runs campaigns in. Choose an agency that serves your target countries.
+ **Cost** – The agency's lowest quoted price for one lead.

When you contact the agency, describe your goals, audience, timing, and budget so that it can scope your first engagement.

1. On the agency details page, choose **Contact agency**.

1. (Optional) If the agency hasn't shared an email address, use its website instead.
