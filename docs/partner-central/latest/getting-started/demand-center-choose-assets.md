

# Choosing assets for a template campaign

For a template campaign, the **Choose assets** step selects the touchpoints that reach your audience. The template groups them by channel, such as email, social media, and banner ads.

If the template includes email messages, you need at least one. If it includes landing pages, you need at least one. You need at least one document only when you choose a landing page. The email messages drive readers to the landing page's sign-up form, and the landing page offers a document for visitors to download. Add the other assets that fit your plan. You configure each asset in its own step, and you can refine it after you create the campaign.

The **Downloadable kit** holds files, such as guides and copy blocks, for your own channels. Kit files aren't campaign assets, so Demand Center doesn't edit or launch them.

**Warning**  
Removing a saved asset permanently deletes its content. You can't undo this action. Published assets stay in the campaign.

1. On the **Choose assets** step, select the assets to include in each channel.

1. Make sure that you select at least one email message and one landing page if the template includes them, and at least one document if you select a landing page.

1. Choose **Save and next**.
