

# Duplicating a campaign

A duplicate keeps the original campaign's details, tags, and assets. It starts without a lead list, send schedules, or published assets, so you add a lead list and send times to the copy before you launch it.

1. In the Demand Center overview, in the **Campaigns** table, select the campaign.

1. Choose **Duplicate**.

1. Open the copy, add a lead list and send times, and then launch it.
