

# Creating a marketing activity

A marketing activity records marketing work that you do with AWS that isn't a campaign built in Demand Center, such as an event sponsorship or a webinar. When you record it in your business plan, your PDM can see it, and you can request MDF for it. The activity goes under the business plan and initiative that it supports. Only plans that accept new activities are available.

Creating the activity publishes nothing. The activity appears on the Marketing Plan page.

1. In Demand Center, choose **Create**, and then choose **Create marketing activity**.

1. For **Choose business plan**, choose a plan that accepts new activities, and then choose the marketing initiative that the activity supports.

1. For **Activity name**, enter a name of 1 to 255 characters. Use the pattern [Activity name] [Quarter] [Geography], for example, AWS Summit San Francisco Q3 NAMER.

1. For **Description**, enter up to 1,000 characters. Some plans require a description.

1. (Optional) For **Activity owner**, enter up to 255 characters. For **Notes and next steps**, enter up to 1,000 characters.

1. Enter the activity's dates. The dates must fall in the plan year, and the end date can't be before the start date.

1. Create the activity.
