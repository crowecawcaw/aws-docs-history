## Login and Registration

### Registration

#### How do I register and log in to

AWS Partner Central?

To create an AWS Partner Central profile, complete the following steps:

1. Fill out the [AWS Partner Central registration form](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNSelfRegister "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNSelfRegister").
2. If you're registering your company's business domain for the first time, you'll create a company account and an alliance lead user profile simultaneously. Enter your company name, business email address, and company information.
3. After you register, verify your email with the security code sent from `apn-no-reply@amazon.com`. Enter this code in the registration form to complete your account verification.

###### Important

You must register with an email address that contains your company's domain (for example, `@companyname.com`). You can't register with a personal email address (for example, `@gmail.com`, `@hotmail.com`, or `@edu`).

###### Note

When multiple users register with the same company email domain, they create individual AWS Partner Central user profiles within your company's AWS Partner Network account.

###### Note

If multiple companies (such as parent and child companies) share the same business domain and register in the AWS Partner Network, they share one AWS Partner Network account. Additional users who register with their company email address that contains the same domain will be linked to the same AWS Partner Network account.

#### Troubleshooting verification code

issues

If you don't receive the verification code after registering in AWS Partner Central, check the following:

- **Generic domain** – AWS Partner Central is for exclusive use of AWS Partners, and all accounts registered in AWS Partner Central must use email addresses with unique business domains. If you registered with a personal email address (for example, `@gmail.com`, `@hotmail.com`, or `@edu`), you need to register again with your company's unique domain.
- **Existing AWS Partner** – If your company is already registered with AWS Partner Network, use the same registration form to create an individual user profile. To create an individual login under your company's account, register with your business email address.
- **Spam filter** – If your company is registered in AWS Partner Network and you didn't receive the security code via email, check your spam folder.
- **Blocked emails** – If you still don't receive these emails and they're not in your spam folder, your company might be blocking these emails. Check with your company's network administrator to ensure that security code emails aren't blocked.

#### Password requirements

Your password must meet the following requirements:

- 8 or more characters
- At least one uppercase and one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one symbol (! @ # $ % ^ & \* ( ) \_ + - = { } [ ] \ | ; : ' , . ? / ` ~ > < ")

For additional assistance with login and registration, contact [AWS Partner Support](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/").

#### How do I use the security code?

You need to enter a security code when prompted to register in AWS Partner Central. This code is sent to the email address you enter in the AWS Partner Central registration form.

If you don't receive the security code, try these troubleshooting steps:

1. Check your inbox for an email from APN No-Reply (`apn-no-reply@amazon.com`), with the subject "AWS Partner Network Email Verification".
2. If the email isn't in your inbox, check your spam or junk folder. Sometimes the security code email is filtered based on your email settings.
3. If you still don't receive the code, your company network might be blocking these emails. Check with your email network administrator to investigate potential issues that may occur inside your organization network with Managed Service Providers (MSPs) or Internet Service Providers (ISPs).
4. Add the sender address `apn-no-reply@amazon.com` to your allowed senders list.
5. Check if any content or text limits are set for incoming emails that may be quarantining incoming AWS Partner Network emails.

###### Important

After receiving the code, enter it into your registration form before the form expires. Don't refresh the verification page as this may cause an error.

#### How do I move to a new AWS Partner

account?

If you no longer work with your current company in AWS Partner Central and have moved to a new company that's already registered in AWS Partner Network, follow these steps to register a new AWS Partner Central profile and transfer your AWS Trainings and Certifications:

1. Register your new company email address with your new AWS Partner Network company [here](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNSelfRegister "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNSelfRegister").

###### Note

AWS Partner Network doesn't support updating an existing AWS Partner Central account's email address. You must register a new AWS Partner Central profile using the registration form. 2. Transfer Accreditations by signing into AWS Skillbuilder using the AWS Partner option and follow the prompts to create a new Skill Builder profile using your new AWS Partner Central email address. 3. Contact the [AWS Training team](https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/ "https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/") to merge your former AWS Partner Skill Builder profile with your new AWS Partner Skill Builder Profile.

###### Important

This step is required to maintain your accreditations. 4. Transfer AWS Certifications by contacting the [AWS Training team](https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/ "https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/") to merge your former Training and Certification profile with your new AWS Partner Central profile or a personal AWS Certification account. 5. Include your previous Training and/or Certification account email and your current AWS Partner Central Profile email in the contact form.

###### Important

This information is required to complete the transfer process.

Your Certifications and Accreditations will sync to the Partner Scorecard within 5 business days of your Training and Certification account update. If you can't access your AWS Training and Certification profile, or are unsure of your AWS Training and Certification profile login email, contact the [AWS Training team](https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/ "https://aws.amazon.com/contact-us/aws-training/").

If you need additional assistance registering with a new company in AWS Partner Network, contact us [here](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/").

### Login

#### What do I do if I'm locked out of AWS Partner Central?

If you're locked out of AWS Partner Central, you can get support by doing the following:

1. Navigate to the [AWS Partner Team contact page](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/") of the AWS Partner Network Knowledge Base.
2. Complete the contact form and choose **Submit**.

#### How can I reset my AWS Partner Central account password?

To reset your AWS Partner Central profile password, follow these steps:

1. Visit the [Forgot Your Password](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNLogin "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/APNLogin") page.
2. Enter your registered AWS Partner Central email address in the **Business Email** field.
3. Choose **Next**.
4. You'll receive an email with instructions on how to reset your password.

###### Note

You must be registered in AWS Partner Central to reset your password.

If you don't receive the password reset email, try these troubleshooting steps:

- Check your inbox for an email from APN No-Reply (`apn-no-reply@amazon.com`). The subject of the email will be "AWS Partner Network Email Verification".
- If the email isn't in your inbox, check your spam or junk folder. Sometimes the email is filtered based on your email settings.
- If you still don't receive this email, your company network might be blocking these emails. Check with your email network administrator to investigate potential issues of AWS Partner Network/Amazon email filtering/blocking that may occur inside your organization network with Managed Service Providers (MSPs) or Internet Service Providers (ISPs).
- Add `apn-no-reply@amazon.com` to your email client's allowed senders list to ensure you receive AWS Partner Network communications.
- Check if any content or text limits are set for incoming emails that may be quarantining incoming AWS Partner Network emails.

Remember that your password must meet the following requirements:

- 8 or more characters
- At least one uppercase and one lowercase letter
- At least one number
- At least one symbol (! @ # $ % ^ & \* ( ) \_ + - = { } [ ] \ | ; : ' , . ? / ` ~ > < ")

For additional assistance with login and registration, contact [AWS Partner Support](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/").

## AWS Partner Central account

### Can I log into my AWS account through AWS Partner Central?

The AWS Management Console isn't accessible through AWS Partner Central. The AWS Management Console is where AWS customers manage AWS services, billing, and more. To access your console, sign in or create your AWS account.

AWS Partner Central is the partner-only portal that offers AWS Partners marketing, business, and technical tools to help build their AWS practice. To access AWS Partner Central, register your company to join the AWS Partner Network. If your company is already registered, you can use the same registration form to create an individual user profile.

### How do I pay the AWS Partner Network (APN) program fee?

For information about paying the AWS Partner Network (APN) program fee, see the [AWS Partner Network Program Fee](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/program-fee/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/program-fee/") page.

### How do I apply for an APN Differentiation program?

In addition to joining the AWS Partner Network (APN), AWS Partners can apply for AWS Partner Differentiation Programs based on the nature of their work on AWS and overall business model. These AWS Partner Programs include: AWS Competency Program, AWS Solution Provider Program, AWS Managed Service Program, AWS Public Sector Program, AWS Service Delivery Program or Service Ready Program, and more.

To learn more about AWS Partner Programs, benefits, and how to get started, visit the [AWS Partner Programs](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/programs/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/programs/") page.

AWS Partners who meet specific APN Designation eligibility requirements can submit an Application for a Designation by following these steps:

1. ###### **Sign in to AWS Partner Central**

Log in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/"). 2. ###### **Navigate to Programs**

Navigate to the Programs tab and choose **Program Applications**. 3. ###### **Create an application**

Choose **Create**. 4. ###### **Select a designation**

In the Application form, choose **Select Designation**. 5. ###### **Find your designation**

Search for the Designation or choose the **Select Program** dropdown menu in the upper right corner to select the Designation type. Applications will only be visible if your company is eligible. 6. ###### **Complete and submit**

Select the desired Designation and choose **Select**. Complete the Application details and choose **Submit**.

Review the designation guides and any validation checklists for the full details and expectations of the designation before submitting your designation application. After you submit, a member of our team will review your application and will send email updates to your Alliance Lead.

### How do I add a domain?

To add a new domain to your AWS Partner Central account, file a case to APN Support from the Alliance Lead or an Alliance Team User profile. APN Support will verify the domain addition request and can add this to your domains list if approved.

The Alliance Lead or Alliance Team User can review their company domain list by following these steps:

1. ###### **Sign in to AWS Partner Central**

Log in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/"). 2. ###### **Access company profile**

Navigate to the My Company menu and choose **Company Profile**. 3. ###### **View domains**

Choose the My Domains tab.

### How do I access my Partner Scorecard?

The AWS Partner Scorecard is available to your company's Alliance Lead and Alliance Team members. Any users who are registered as standard partner users won't be able to access the AWS Partner Scorecard.

If you're the Alliance Lead or an Alliance Team user, you can access your company's Partner Scorecard by following these steps:

1. ###### **Sign in to AWS Partner Central**

Log in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/"). 2. ###### **Find the scorecard**

In the homepage, scroll down to the AWS Partner Paths section. Here, you can select the scorecard for the individual Partner path(s), or you can choose **View Scorecard**.

### Can I change my account's company name?

AWS Partner Alliance Leads, Alliance Team users, and Marketing Staff users can update their account's company name through the Company Profile section of their AWS Partner Central account.

Follow these steps to submit a company name update:

1. ###### **Sign in to AWS Partner Central**

Log in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/"). 2. ###### **Access company profile**

Navigate to the My Company menu and choose **Company Profile**. 3. ###### **Edit company name**

In the Company Name & Description section, choose **Edit**. Enter the new company name in the **Company Name** field. 4. ###### **Save changes**

Choose **Save**.

Your updates will be reviewed within two business days of submission date. The company name update verification process might take additional time if the company name change is related to an AWS Partner account merge or acquisition or requires additional approval from your partner management team.

### How do I merge AWS Partner Central accounts?

An AWS Partner account merge will combine two or more APN accounts, which will transfer all information from the merged account into the acquiring account. The acquiring APN account will remain in AWS Partner Central under a single, unified brand while the merged account will no longer operate as its own entity in APN.

An AWS Partner account merge won't merge individual AWS Partner Central user profiles together. An APN account merge will only combine multiple AWS Partner company accounts registered in the AWS Partner Network.

#### When should I request an APN account merge?

You should request an APN account merge if:

- The company is registered in AWS Partner Central and has been fully acquired by another AWS Partner
- Two companies wish to unify under a single brand and/or are consolidating resources to meet APN path or tier requirements
- The company is a duplicate or accidental registration of another AWS Partner
- You're trying to add a company domain that is already registered on another APN account

You should not request an APN account merge if:

- Your company is still operating as a separate entity, even if acquired by another company

#### How do I request an APN account merge?

To request an APN account merge, first review the AWS Partner Mergers and Acquisitions Policy and FAQ guide. This will provide additional details on AWS Partner Path and Program policy and different types of merge scenarios.

Next, consult your Partner Development Manager to confirm that an APN account merge is the correct action. If you have an assigned PDM, contact them to submit an APN account merge request on your behalf.

If you wish to consolidate two duplicate accounts or if your account doesn't yet have an assigned Partner Development Manager, submit a case to the APN Support team. If available, attach merge confirmation from both accounts' Alliance Teams.

Only the accounts' Alliance Lead or Alliance Team user may request an APN account merge.

#### APN account merge FAQs

Why don't I have Alliance Lead, Alliance Team, or ACE Manager access after the merge?

The Alliance Lead, Alliance Team users, and ACE Managers from the merged account will have their AWS Partner Central access removed during the merge process in order to retain the acquiring account's administrative points of contact. The Alliance Lead or Alliance team can grant administrative access to additional users once the merge is completed, if necessary.

How do I check that my company domains were transferred during a merge?

The Alliance Lead or Alliance Team users on the acquiring account can check their company's domains list by following these steps:

1. ###### **Sign in to AWS Partner Central**

Log in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/"). 2. ###### **Access company profile**

Navigate to the My Company tab and choose **Company profile**. 3. ###### **View domains**

Choose the **My Domains** tab and review the list of your company's domains.

Why isn't my scorecard updated after the merge?

The scorecard will take up to 5 business days to update once a merge is completed. If it has been 5 business days and the scorecard isn't updating as expected, submit a case to the APN Support team.

Can I transfer my Partner Path or tier to the acquiring account?

No, partner paths and tier don't transfer to the acquiring account due to APN policy. Instead, the acquiring account can leverage their combined assets to join a new partner path or apply for a higher path stage or tier.

Is AWS Partner Funding transferred from the merged account to the acquiring account during the merge?

Since funds are allocated based on eligibility for APN Funding Programs, MDF Wallet and active fund requests aren't transferred during a merge. MDF wallet will automatically calculate and update on the acquiring account based on eligibility and achieved designations and programs. The APN Team encourages AWS Partners who are merging into another APN account to complete or cancel their active fund requests through APFP before the merge begins so no funds are lost. Contact your PDM with any questions on open fund requests.

### How is my Partner Development team assigned?

A PDM, or Partner Development Manager, helps manage the relationship between AWS and AWS Partners by working with the partners' alliance leads and various other partner contacts to establish go-to-market programs and business development programs.

Your PDM or partner development team is automatically assigned based on multiple factors, including your company's territory. You might be assigned an individual PDM or partner development team based on the account assignment structure for the specific territory's PDM coverage model.

Note that you might not be able to request a PDM update, since your PDM contact is automatically assigned.

## AWS training and certification

### AWS Partner Accreditations

**How do I register for AWS Partner Accreditations?**

To enroll in AWS Skill Builder and access AWS Partner Accreditations:

1. Sign in to AWS Partner Central.
2. Choose **Learn**, **AWS Partner Training**.
3. Choose **Explore free digital training**.
4. Choose **Sign in to AWS Skill Builder**.

### AWS Partner Certifications

**How do I ensure that my Partner Scorecard registers my organization's AWS Certifications?**

To ensure your AWS Certifications appear on your organization's Partner Scorecard, verify that your AWS Partner Central account is properly linked to your certification account. Contact AWS Partner Support if you need assistance with this process.

**How do I transfer my AWS certifications to a new AWS Partner Central account?**

If you need to transfer your AWS certifications to a new AWS Partner Central account, sign in to AWS Partner Central and update your certification account information in your profile settings. For detailed instructions, contact AWS Partner Support.

## Getting Support

### How to open an AWS Support ticket

To submit a new support case, view your case history, or correspond with AWS Partner Support, follow these steps:

1. Sign in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/ "https://partnercentral.awspartner.com/").
2. Choose **Contact Support**.

### Getting support when locked out of AWS Partner Central

If you can't sign in to AWS Partner Central, you can still get support by doing the following:

1. Navigate to the [AWS Partner Team contact page](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/ "https://aws.amazon.com/partners/support/") of the AWS Partner Network Knowledge Base.
2. Complete the contact form with your issue details.
3. Choose **Submit**.
