

# Understanding your Performance Indicators score
<a name="ppi-scoring"></a>

Your Performance Indicators score is a single percentage that reflects your overall effectiveness across four key performance areas. The score ranges from 33% to 100%, with higher scores indicating stronger performance.

## How scoring works
<a name="ppi-scoring-mechanism"></a>

Performance Indicators evaluates your performance using four key metrics:
+ **Migration Win Rate** – Your ability to convert funded assessments into migration projects
+ **Speed to Value** – How quickly you help customers realize value after the migration starts
+ **Portfolio Success** – Overall revenue achievement across all completed projects
+ **Project Success** – Individual project performance and consistency

For each metric, you receive a score of 1, 2, or 3 points based on your performance:


| Score | Performance Track | Description | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| 3 points | High Performance Track | Exceeds expectations; demonstrates excellence | 
| 2 points | Core Program Track | Meets expectations; solid execution | 
| 1 point | Turnaround Track | Below expectations; improvement needed | 

**Your overall Performance Indicators score calculation:**

Performance Indicators Score = The average of all four KPI scores, with a maximum of 3. A partner must have a score for all four KPIs to receive a Performance Indicators score.

Performance Indicators Score %: The Performance Indicators average score divided by 3 (maximum possible score), expressed as a percentage.

**Example:** If you score 2 points on two KPIs and 3 points on two KPIs:

Performance Indicators Score: (2 \+ 2 \+ 3 \+ 3) / 4 = 2.5

Performance Indicators Score %: 2.5 / 3 = 83%

**Note**  
Partners must have a score for each metric in order to receive a Performance Indicators Score; partners without 3 completed migrations or 3 assess claims will receive a Trending Performance Indicators score, until they meet the minimum activity required.

## Performance tracks
<a name="ppi-performance-tiers"></a>

Based on your Performance Indicators score, you'll be classified into one of three performance levels:


| Level | Score Range | Description | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| High Performance Track | 83% - 100% (10-12 total points) | Partners delivering the strongest results across metrics | 
| Core Program Track | 50% - 82% (6-9 total points) | Partners within core expectations with growth potential | 
| Turnaround Track | 0% - 49% (5 or fewer total points) | Partners who need support to meet performance standards |
