

# Disbursements
<a name="bvr-disbursements"></a>

The BVR motion provides funding that is disbursed automatically as you complete pit stop milestones. The total funding available for your initiative is determined by the size of the opportunity.

## How disbursements work
<a name="bvr-how-disbursements-work"></a>

1. You complete the required activities for a pit stop that has a funding milestone.

1. Your PSS approves the activities.

1. A purchase order (PO) number is automatically generated.

1. The disbursement status updates to **In progress** with the PO number displayed on your initiative.

1. You submit an invoice through [Amazon Payee Central](https://payeecentral.amazon.com) using the PO number.

1. Payment is processed after invoice submission.

## Disbursement statuses
<a name="bvr-disbursement-statuses"></a>


| Status | Description | Your action | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| Locked | Pit stop not yet complete. | Complete the required activities. | 
| In progress | PO generated. | Submit your invoice through Payee Central. | 
| Paid | Payment processed. | None. | 

You can view the status of each disbursement on the initiative detail page under the **Disbursement summary** section.
