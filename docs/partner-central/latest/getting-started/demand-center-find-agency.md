

# Finding and comparing agencies

Use the **Region** and **Campaign type** filters to narrow the list to agencies that work where you sell and run the campaigns that you need. Campaign types include content syndication, digital advertising, event marketing, lead generation, and account-based marketing (ABM). The **Cost** column is the agency's lowest quoted price for one lead. Compare it with your campaign budget.

1. In the AWS Partner Central console, open Agency Connect.

1. Use the **Region** and **Campaign type** filters to narrow the list.

1. Compare the agencies by their **Cost**.

1. Select an agency.

1. Choose **View details**.
