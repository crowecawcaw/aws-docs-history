

# Nominating a customer
<a name="bvr-nominating-customer"></a>

After you are enrolled in the BVR motion, you can nominate customers. A nomination links a customer engagement to the motion and establishes the KPIs that will be tracked throughout the pit stops.

## Before you begin
<a name="bvr-nomination-before-you-begin"></a>

Confirm that:
+ You are enrolled in the BVR motion.
+ The customer is deploying an eligible AWS service.
+ You have an active opportunity linked to the customer in AWS Partner Central.
+ A statement of work (SOW) is signed or expected soon.
+ The customer has executive sponsorship for the engagement.

## Submit a nomination
<a name="bvr-submit-nomination"></a>

1. Sign in to [AWS Partner Central](https://partnercentral.awspartner.com).

1. In the navigation pane, under **Funding benefits**, choose **Business Value Realization**.

1. Choose **Nominate customer**.

1. Follow the wizard steps to provide engagement details, confirm eligibility, and define customer KPIs.

1. Review your entries and choose **Submit nomination**.

## After submission
<a name="bvr-after-nomination"></a>
+ Your nomination enters **In review** status.
+ Your Partner Success Specialist (PSS) reviews the nomination.
+ If approved, the initiative moves to its first pit stop and activities become available.
+ If changes are needed, you receive specific feedback and can edit and resubmit.
