

# Configuring assets

Each asset has its own step, which sets the editable fields of that asset. Fixed AWS copy is part of the layout, so it doesn't appear as a field. The step groups fields into header, body, and footer sections. Landing pages group them by page: the form page that visitors see first, and the confirmation page that they see after they submit.

Footer fields, such as the sender, postal address, and privacy policy URL, apply to the whole campaign, so you set them once on the **Campaign details** step.

Scheduling applies to email messages and social posts. For **Send when published**, the asset goes out as soon as you launch the campaign. For **Schedule for later**, choose a date, time, and time zone at least 1 hour from now.

1. On each asset step, enter the asset's content in its header, body, and footer sections.

1. For an email message or social post, choose **Send when published** or **Schedule for later**.

1. Choose **Save and next**.

For instructions on setting up delivery and launching the campaign, see [Setting up delivery and launching a campaign](demand-center-launch-campaign.md).
