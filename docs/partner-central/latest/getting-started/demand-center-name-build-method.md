

# Naming a campaign and choosing a build method

The first step names the campaign and sets how it's built. You can't change the build method after you create the campaign.

1. In the AWS Partner Central console, open Demand Center.

1. Choose **Create campaign**.

1. For **Campaign name**, enter a name of 1-100 characters that identifies the campaign in reports, such as its topic and city.

1. Choose a build method:

   1. **Use a Partner Ready template** starts from a pre-built AWS kit of email messages, landing pages, social posts, and sales assets. It's the faster path for a standard campaign.

   1. **Draft with AI** has AI draft the assets that you choose in the next step. The campaign language that you choose is fixed after you create the campaign.

1. (Optional) Add **Tags**. Tags are key-value pairs that you use to filter campaigns and report on them.

1. Choose **Save and next**.

If you chose **Draft with AI**, the next step is **Select asset type and channels**. For more information about drafting campaign content with AI, see [Drafting campaign content with AI](demand-center-draft-with-ai.md).
