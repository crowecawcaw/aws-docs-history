

# Selecting an asset type and channels

The **Select asset type and channels** step decides which assets AI drafts and how many steps follow.

The asset type is the content piece that the campaign is built around: a white paper, a case study, or a solution brief. A campaign has one asset type.

1. For **Select an asset type**, choose the content asset that you want to create.

1. For **Channels for selected assets**, choose the channels that distribute the asset type, such as email messages that promote your case study.

1. Select the social media platforms to post on. Each platform gets one post for the whole campaign.

1. Choose **Save and next**.
