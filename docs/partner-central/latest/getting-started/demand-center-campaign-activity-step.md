

# Adding the campaign to a business plan

The **Create marketing activity** step is optional. It adds the campaign to a business plan as a new marketing activity, so you and your Partner Development Manager (PDM) can track it, and you can request Marketing Development Funds (MDF) for it.

The activity goes under the business plan and marketing initiative that the campaign supports. Only plans that accept new activities are available. If you skip this step, the campaign is still created, and it appears on the Demand Center overview.

For more information about activity fields, see [Tracking marketing activities in the Marketing Plan](demand-center-marketing-plan.md).
