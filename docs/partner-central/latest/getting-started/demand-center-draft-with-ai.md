

# Drafting campaign content with AI

When you choose **Draft with AI** as the build method, AI drafts the assets that you choose. Each asset uses a source that you provide, such as a description, a webpage, a file, or an AWS Partner Network (APN) Customer Engagements (ACE) opportunity. AI drafting is the better fit when no template matches your message.

**Important**  
AI can make mistakes. Review every drafted field before you launch the campaign.
