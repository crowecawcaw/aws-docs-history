

# Pit stop progression
<a name="bvr-pit-stop-progression"></a>

After your nomination is approved, the initiative progresses through multiple pit stops. Each pit stop drives adoption forward — from initial activation through scaled usage to long-term value retention.

## How progression works
<a name="bvr-how-progression-works"></a>

To advance through the motion:

1. Complete the required activities for your current pit stop.

1. Meet the KPI achievement threshold defined in your Value Realization Plan (VRP).

1. Once all required activities are approved by your PSS and KPI thresholds are met, the initiative advances to the next pit stop automatically.

Optional activities support adoption but don't block progression.

## What happens as activities are completed
<a name="bvr-activity-completion"></a>

As you submit activities within a pit stop:
+ **All required activities** move to **Under review** while your PSS reviews them.
+ If your PSS approves the submission, the activity moves to **Complete**.
+ If your PSS requests changes, the activity moves to **Action required** and you receive a notification with specific feedback.

Once all required activities are approved and your KPI progress meets the threshold, the initiative advances to the next pit stop.

## Funding at pit stop completion
<a name="bvr-funding-at-completion"></a>

Funding is disbursed automatically when you complete a pit stop that has an associated funding milestone. The amount is determined by the size of your opportunity. You don't need to request payment — meeting the requirements triggers the disbursement process. For more information, see [Disbursements](bvr-disbursements.md).

## Locked activities
<a name="bvr-locked-activities"></a>

Activities that belong to future pit stops appear in a locked state. You can see what's ahead, but you can't submit them until the initiative reaches that pit stop.

## Viewing history
<a name="bvr-viewing-history"></a>

On the initiative detail page, you can expand any completed pit stop to view the activities that were submitted and their completion history.
