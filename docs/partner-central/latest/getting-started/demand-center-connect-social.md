

# Connecting an account

1. On the Demand Center overview, choose the **Settings** tab.

1. In **Social connections**, choose **Connect** for the platform, or choose **Connect social account**.

1. In the new tab, sign in to the platform and approve access. For Facebook, select only the one Page that you want posts to publish to.

1. Return to the Demand Center tab. The account appears as soon as the connection is confirmed.
