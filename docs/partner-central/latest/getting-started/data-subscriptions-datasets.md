

# Available datasets

The following table lists the partner datasets that you can subscribe to and the folder that AWS Partner Central uses for each dataset in your bucket. Datasets with the ACE prefix come from the APN Customer Engagement (ACE) program. To see the columns in a dataset, choose its name on the **Data sharing** page.


| Dataset | Folder in your bucket | Contents | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| ACE Opportunity Pipeline | opportunity\_pipeline | Opportunity lifecycle data: stages, monthly and annual recurring revenue, win and loss, ownership, and geographic, segment, and industry dimensions. | 
| ACE Leads | leads | Lead lifecycle data from the ACE Lead Tool: AWS-shared and partner-imported leads, acceptance, qualification stages, ownership, and conversion to opportunities. | 
| Partner Marketing Campaigns | marketing\_campaigns | Marketing campaign performance: lead flow, opportunity attribution, and pipeline metrics for each campaign and KPI. | 
| Partner Discount Business Report | discount\_business\_report | The Detailed Billing Report for channel and resell partners: one row for each discounted invoice line, with the billed amount and the discount of every partner-program component, for the current month and the six months before it. | 
| Partner Account Relationships | account\_relationships | End customer AWS accounts in an active billing-transfer relationship with a distribution seller, with the relationship type, effective start date, and sector. | 

AWS Partner Central adds datasets over time. The **Data sharing** page lists every dataset that you can subscribe to.
