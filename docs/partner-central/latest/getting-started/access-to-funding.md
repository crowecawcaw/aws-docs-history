

# Accessing funding

To create, view, edit or manage fund requests, a user must be assigned the appropriate permissions in AWS IAM. For more information, see [AWS managed policies for AWS Partner Central users](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/partner-central/latest/getting-started/managed-policies.html). Please note that while a user may have access to the AWS Partner Funding Portal, they may not have anything to review or act on until their first Fund Request.

To get access to funding tools, contact your IAM administrator. You can also contact the AWS Partner Central support team on the [AWS Partner Central and Marketplace Support page](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/support?region=us-east-1) in the AWS Partner Central console.

To access funding tools:

1. Log into [AWS Partner Central in the AWS Console](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral)

1. Click on **Launch AWS Partner Central** in the middle of the home screen.

1. Select **Funding dashboard** under **Funding Benefits** on the lefthand navigation pane

1. Users can begin submitting Fund Requests from this page in Partner Central

   1. Click the **Create fund request** button and a dropdown selection of eligible programs will appear

   1. The subsequent templates will route the user to create a Fund Request for the core programs offered

1. Users can also navigate to the **AWS Partner Funding Benefits** tab to review [eligible core programs](https://aws.amazon.com/partners/funding/)
