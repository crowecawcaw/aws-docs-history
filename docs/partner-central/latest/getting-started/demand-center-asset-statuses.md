

# Understanding asset publication statuses

Each asset on the campaign page has one of the following publication statuses:
+ **Draft** – The asset isn't set up yet. It needs its content and send time.
+ **Ready to launch** – The asset is set up. Demand Center publishes it when you launch the campaign.
+ **Publishing** – Demand Center is handing the asset to the delivery platform.
+ **Published** – The asset is published. A scheduled email message or post goes out at its send time.
+ **Published, last update failed** – The earlier version is still published. The latest update failed to publish.
+ **Failed** – The asset isn't published. To try again, select it and choose **Retry publishing**.
+ **Stopped** – The asset was published, then stopped.

The **Assets** table also shows each asset's **Delivery status** and **Public URL**. Delivery status is what the platform did with an email message or social post: Pending, Delivered, or Failed. Public URL opens a published landing page, document, or banner ad at its public address in a new tab.
