

# Improving your Migration Win Rate
<a name="ppi-improving-win-rate"></a>

**Before requesting Assess funding:**
+ Conduct thorough customer discovery
+ Validate executive sponsorship and commitment
+ Ensure there's a clear business driver for migration
+ Confirm budget and timeline alignment
+ Partner with AWS account teams for joint qualification

**During the Assess phase:**
+ Deliver comprehensive, actionable assessments
+ Maintain weekly customer communication
+ Build a compelling business case with clear ROI
+ Address concerns and objections proactively
+ Create urgency around migration benefits

**Moving to commitment:**
+ Present clear migration roadmaps and timelines
+ Provide accurate cost estimates and resource requirements
+ Secure executive sign-off before requesting Mobilize funding
+ Ensure customer readiness for the next phase
