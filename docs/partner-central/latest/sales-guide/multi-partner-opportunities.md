

# Managing multi-partner opportunities

You can collaborate on opportunities with partners that you have an active connection with. To connect with a partner, see [Discovering and managing partner connections](partner-connections.md).

## Create an opportunity with a connected partner
<a name="multi-partner-create"></a>

1. Choose **Partner Connections**, and then choose a partner with an active connection to open its details page.

1. Choose **Collaborate on opportunities**. AWS Partner Central opens the **Create opportunity** page.

1. Enter the opportunity information as you do for other opportunities, and create the opportunity.

1. To confirm that the opportunity is shared, open the opportunity details, and choose the **Shared partner details** tab.

## Invite a partner to an existing opportunity
<a name="multi-partner-invite"></a>

1. Open the opportunity details, and choose **Invite partner**.

1. Review the window that opens. It shows any partners you invited before, the AWS sender details for AWS-originated opportunities, and the partner details.

1. Complete the details, and choose **Send invitation**. A success banner confirms that AWS Partner Central sent the invitation.

**Note**  
National security opportunities and Limited Visibility opportunities can't be shared with partners.

## View opportunity invitations
<a name="multi-partner-view-invitations"></a>

To see inbound and outbound invitations, open the **Opportunity invitations** tab on the Opportunities page. Then choose an **Invitation ID**, or select an invitation and choose **View invitation**.

## Accept an opportunity invitation
<a name="multi-partner-accept"></a>

1. On the Opportunities page, open the **Opportunity invitations** tab.

1. Accept the invitation in one of the following ways:
   + Select one or more invitations, and choose **Accept invitation**.
   + Choose the **Invitation ID** of an inbound invitation. On the details page, choose **Accept opportunity invitation**.

1. In the confirmation dialog, confirm that you want to accept. A success banner confirms the acceptance.

## Track partners on a shared opportunity
<a name="multi-partner-track"></a>

To track pipeline progress, review the AWS Partners engaged on the opportunity. Open the opportunity, and choose the **Shared partner details** tab.

To see another partner's progress, select the partner, and choose **View partner opportunity details**. The details show the partner's stage, target close date, primary need from AWS, and next steps. They also show the partner's products and solutions.

The other partners see your updates in the same way.
