

# Limited Visibility opportunities

Global System Integrator (GSI) partners can submit opportunities with Limited Visibility. AWS doesn't share a Limited Visibility opportunity with AWS Sales. These opportunities follow the same validation process and criteria as standard opportunities.

## Submit a Limited Visibility opportunity
<a name="limited-visibility-submit"></a>

**Note**  
Only GSI partners can submit Limited Visibility opportunities.

1. Complete all core fields in the customer details. You can do this for a single opportunity or in a bulk create file.

1. For **Partner primary need from AWS**, choose **Do Not Need Support from AWS Sales Rep**.

1. The required **Limited Visibility** field appears. Choose **Yes** to make the opportunity Limited Visibility. The console states that the opportunity won't be visible to AWS Sales.

1. After you complete all core fields, choose **Save and Submit**. The status changes to **Submitted**. You can't edit the opportunity until the ACE Validation Team reviews it.

## Review process
<a name="limited-visibility-review"></a>

After you submit a Limited Visibility opportunity, it appears like a standard opportunity, with a Limited Visibility indicator. The review has the following outcomes:
+ **Approved.** After ACE approves the opportunity, its status changes to **Approved**.
+ **Rejected.** If the opportunity isn't validated, it follows the standard disqualification process. Its status changes to **Rejected**, and you receive a Not approved notification. You can't edit a rejected opportunity.
+ **In Review.** If the ACE Validation Team needs more information, the status changes to **In Review**, and you receive an email notification.

While the opportunity is **In Review**, you can update the Limited Visibility indicator in the opportunity details. You can also ask a validator to update it for you. You can edit the description, the estimated AWS Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), and the close date.

After you submit your updates, the status changes to **Submitted**. You can't edit the opportunity until the ACE Validation Team reviews it.

## Update a Limited Visibility opportunity
<a name="limited-visibility-update"></a>

After validation, update the opportunity every two weeks, the same as a standard opportunity.

You can remove Limited Visibility at any time after validation and before the opportunity reaches the Launched or Closed Lost stage.

**Note**  
To get AWS sales support or funding for an opportunity, turn off Limited Visibility.

To remove Limited Visibility, ask your GSI Partner Development Manager (PDM), or complete the following steps.

1. Open the details of the opportunity.

1. Choose **Edit**.

1. For **Limited Visibility**, choose **No**.
