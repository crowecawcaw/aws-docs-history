# AMAZON.HelpIntent

Responds to words or phrases that indicate the user needs help
while interacting with your bot. When this intent is invoked,
you can configure your Lambda function or application to provide
information about the your bot's capabilities, ask follow up
questions about areas of help, or hand the interaction over to a
human agent.

Common utterances:

- help
- help me
- can you help me
