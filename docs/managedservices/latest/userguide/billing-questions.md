# Billing questions
<a name="billing-questions"></a>

To submit a billing-related question, complete the following steps:

1. Open the [AWS Support Center](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/) at https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home\#/.

1. Choose **Account & billing**.  
![The Support console showing the Account & billing option.](http://docs.aws.amazon.com/managedservices/latest/userguide/images/billingqueries1.png)

1. Choose **Create case**.  
![The Create case button.](http://docs.aws.amazon.com/managedservices/latest/userguide/images/billingqueries2.png)

1. Choose **Account and billing**, and then follow the prompts to submit your case.  
![The Support console showing the Account and billing option.](http://docs.aws.amazon.com/managedservices/latest/userguide/images/billingqueries2.png)
