# Choosing single MALZ or multiple MALZs

The following table provides some high level considerations on deciding between a single multi-account landing zone (MALZ)
vs multiple multi-account landing zones (for example, two multi-account landing zones - Prod and non-Prod). In general, the choice depends
upon individual needs, legal requirements, and operating practices.

| Single multi-account landing zone vs. multiple multi-account landing zones | Entity                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Single AMS landing zone                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Multiple (two or more) landing zones |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Base cost                                                                  | Lower, optimized at approximately $3,000 per month.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Higher, an additional cost of approximately $3,000 per environment.                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Billing                                                                    | Single bill, due to single Billing/ Management account.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Separate bill for each multi-account landing zone. Currently AWS Org does not support multi-Management<br>accounts with a single bill.                                                                                                        |
| Portability of existing reserved instances (RIs)                           | Low. AWS RIs are currently not convertible across multiple billing accounts.<br>You would repurpose existing RIs for multi-account landing zone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Lower. You would repurpose and distribute RIs across all multi-account landing zone.                                                                                                                                                          |
| Product tiering discounts                                                  | High. See<br>[Volume discounts.](../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/useconsolidatedbilling-discounts.md "../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/useconsolidatedbilling-discounts.md")                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Low. See<br>[Volume discounts.](../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/useconsolidatedbilling-discounts.md "../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/useconsolidatedbilling-discounts.md")                                                |
| Initial setup overhead (on project/migration timelines)                    | Low. Active Directory, networking and single sign-on (SSO) integrations once<br>only.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | High. You would perform Active Directory, network integration, and SSO integrations for<br>every landing zone. This could cause potential delays to any migration project.                                                                    |
| Common services configurability                                            | Low efforts. You configure common/shared services like DNS, backup, monitoring,<br>logging etc.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | High efforts. Additional planning is required to address where the common<br>infrastructure or services will be sitting. Traffic traversing across<br>multiple transit gateways (TGWs) in each landing zone, could lead to extra cost.        |
| Scalability                                                                | Medium. AMS has a current practical limit of 150 accounts per multi-account landing zone.<br>Multiple teams or vendors running applications in same account could have access to<br>stacks owned by different teams. This limitation can be mitigated by controlling<br>access to application-specific stacks at the ServiceNow layer (by integrating the<br>AMS ServiceNow Connector application and making use of tags). Ask AMS technical<br>delivery managers (TDMs) or cloud architects (CAs) how to implement this. | High. Ability to leverage multiple multi-account landing zone to distribute the accounts while<br>achieving an account or application level of segregation. Managing large numbers of accounts could<br>lead to operational or cost overhead. |
| Operational Risk                                                           | (Depends) Low. Operational integration and readiness once only. Less chance of<br>process drifts.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | (Depends) Low. Multiple integration and operational activities. Drift in multiple<br>landing zones over the period could lead to operational risks.                                                                                           |
| Multi AWS Region                                                           | Single AWS Region. AMS multi-account landing zone is restricted to a single AWS Region. To span multiple<br>AWS Regions, use multiple multi-account landing zone.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Multi AWS Region. With multiple multi-account landing zones, you can have each MALZ deployed in one region and interconnect them using<br>transit gateway (TGW) peering.                                                                      |
| Account migration or portability                                           | Yes. Moving accounts from one OU to another within the same AWS Organization is<br>possible.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | No. AMS doesn't support migration of an account across landing zones; that is, across<br>AWS Organizations. Workloads can reach across landing zones with transit gateway (TGW) or VPC<br>peering.                                            |
| Change management                                                          | Medium. Making destructive changes to common components like TGW, Active Directory (AD), or<br>outbound (egress) can impact all workloads in a multi-account landing zone. However, changes to AMS-managed<br>components are tested internally and are pushed in rolling updates.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Low. Making destructive changes to common components like TGW, AD, or outbound (egress)<br>can impact only the workloads in that specific multi-account landing zone.                                                                         |
| Data and access controls                                                   | (Depends) Low control if you’d like to connect to different on-premise ADs and networks<br>for Prod vs Non-Prod workloads. SAML federation, TGW domains, and security groups (SGs) can<br>help implement required controls too.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | (Depends) High control if you’d like to connect to different on-premise ADs and<br>networks for Prod vs Non-Prod workloads. Use separate landing zones for strict compliance<br>requirements.                                                 |
| Compliance and Security                                                    | (Depends) Low if there are strict compliance needs to completely segregate<br>material vs non-material workloads. AMS standard preventative and detective controls in place.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | (Depends) High as multiple multi-account landing zone could help achieve strict compliance requirements<br>by completely segregating material vs non-material workloads. AMS standard preventative<br>and detective controls in place.        |

**Recommendation**: Without strict Compliance or multi-Region need, starting
with single AMS multi-account landing zone would strike a good balance among cost, security, operational excellence, and
migration complexity. You can always setup additional landing zone, if any account or business constraints
are encountered.
