# Incident management service commitments

| Incident management service commitments                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Event or action                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Service commitment measurement |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------ |
| Case 1: An event with known impact is generated. AMS opens an incident and informs you.<br>Case 2: AMS contacts you to confirm the impact of the event. You confirm the event is an<br>incident.<br>Case 3: You notice an issue and submit an incident report.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Clock for incident response and incident resolution starts when:<br>Case 1: AMS creates an incident.<br>Case 2: You confirm the alert is an incident.<br>Case 3: You submit an incident.<br>Service commitments depend on the priority of the incident created.                                                                                                                         |
| If you submit the incident, AMS sends a response to acknowledge it.<br>If AMS creates the incident on your behalf, a separate incident response is not sent.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Clock for incident resolution continues ticking.<br>Clock for incident response time stops when AMS sends the incident acknowledgement.<br>Note Time spent waiting for inputs from you is excluded from incident resolution time<br>calculations. For incidents that AMS creates, the initial response time is the time of the<br>creation of the initial incident notification to you. |
| For the resources / services in question, AMS checks the health to verify if:<br>• AMS detected event or customer submitted incident qualifies as an incident, and<br>• the incident is correctly prioritized, and<br>If an incident you submit is not correctly prioritized, AMS re-prioritizes it. If AMS<br>changes an<br>incident priority, a notification is sent to you along with reasoning behind the priority change.<br>In certain cases, an issue you submitted may not qualify as an incident, depending on the cause.<br>In those cases, AMS closes the incident and<br>sends you a notification explaining the reason why. Irrespective of the event categorization,<br>AMS works with you to assist as needed.<br>To understand the rules for incident categorization, see<br>[Incident priority](what-is-incident-mgmt.md#incident-priority "what-is-incident-mgmt.md#incident-priority"). | In case incident priority changes, the service commitment for the new priority is applicable; clock continues ticking.<br>In cases when an incident is closed because it does not meet the definition of an incident, service commitments are not applicable; clock stops.                                                                                                              |
| AMS works on the incident to resolve it within service commitment.<br>In certain cases, if AMS determines that unavailable stack(s) or resource(s) cannot be resolved in<br>a timely manner, AMS will offer<br>Infrastructure Restore as an option for resolution. Infrastructure Restore involves re-deploying<br>existing stack(s), based on<br>the templates of the impacted stack(s), and initiating a data restore based on the last known restore<br>point (EBS/RDS snapshot), unless otherwise<br>specified by you. Ephemeral data on individual EC2 instances will be lost. If you do not authorize an<br>Infrastructure Restore as recommended<br>by AWS, you will not be eligible for a service credit for the associated Incident Resolution Time<br>Service Commitment.                                                                                                                        | Clock stops when:<br>• AMS has restored all Unavailable services or resources pertaining to that<br>Incident to an available state, or<br>• an infrastructure restore is started.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Occasionally, AMS needs clarification from, or activity by, you to keep incident<br>resolution efforts moving forward,<br>unless you have a pre-defined, approved action. As a result, there is communication between AMS and<br>you in order to resolve incidents                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Clock stops when: AMS is waiting for a response or action from you.<br>Clock restarts when: AMS receives the response from you or the action AMS requires of<br>you is completed.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
