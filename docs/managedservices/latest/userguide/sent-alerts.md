# Alert notifications from AMS

As a part of the alert processing, based on the impact analysis, AWS Managed Services (AMS) creates an incident and initiates
the incident management process for remediation, when impact can be determined. In case impact cannot
be determined, AMS sends an alert notification to the email address associated with your account
through a service notification; see the diagram on AMS monitoring architecture for alert handling process in
[How monitoring works](how-monitoring-works.md "how-monitoring-works.md").
