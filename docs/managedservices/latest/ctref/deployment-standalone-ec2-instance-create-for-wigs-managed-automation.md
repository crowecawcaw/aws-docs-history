Navigate to the **Create RFC** page: In the left navigation pane of the AMS console click **RFCs** to open the RFCs list page, and then click **Create RFC**. 2. Choose a popular change type (CT) in the default **Browse change types** view, or select a CT in the **Choose by category** view. <br>• **Browse by change type**: You can click on a popular CT in the **Quick create** area to immediately open the **Run RFC** page. Open the **Run parameters** area to see the configurations you submitted. Refresh the page to update the RFC execution status.
