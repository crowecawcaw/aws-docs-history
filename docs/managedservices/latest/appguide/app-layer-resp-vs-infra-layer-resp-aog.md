# Application layer responsibilities vs infrastructure layer responsibilities in AMS

By using AMS, your infrastructure, and all it needs for maintenance and growth, is maintained by AMS. However, whatever you need for line-of-business applications or
product applications, is developed, deployed, and maintained by you.

With the help of application deployment tools, such as CodeDeploy and CloudFormation, or Chef, Puppet, Ansible, or Saltstack, your application deployment to your AMS-managed
infrastructure can be fully automated.

For details about what AMS does and does not do, see [What we do, what we do not do](ams-do-not-do.md "ams-do-not-do.md").
