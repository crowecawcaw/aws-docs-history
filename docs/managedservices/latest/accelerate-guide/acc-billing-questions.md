# Billing questions for AMS Accelerate

To submit a billing-related question, complete the following steps:

1. Open the [AWS Support Center](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/") at https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home#/.
2. Choose **Account & billing**.

![The Support console showing the Account & billing option.](images/billingqueries1.png) 3. Choose **Create case**.

![The Create case button.](images/billingqueries2.png) 4. Choose **Account and billing**, and then follow the prompts to submit your case.

![The Support console showing the Account and billing option.](images/billingqueries2.png)
