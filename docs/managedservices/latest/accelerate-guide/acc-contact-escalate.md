# Contact and escalation

You have a designated cloud service delivery manager (CSDM) who provides advisory
assistance across AMS Accelerate, and has a detailed understanding of your use case and
technology architecture for the managed environment. CSDMs work with account managers,
technical account managers, AWS Managed Services cloud architects (CAs), and AWS solution architects
(SAs), as applicable, to help launch new projects and give best practice recommendations
throughout the software development and operations processes. The CSDM is the primary point
of contact for AMS. Key responsibilities of your CSDM are:

- Organize and lead monthly service review meetings with customers.
- Provide details on security, software updates for environment and opportunities for
  optimization.
- Champion your requirements including feature requests for AMS Accelerate.
- Respond to and resolve billing and service reporting requests.
- Provide insights for financial and capacity optimization recommendations.
