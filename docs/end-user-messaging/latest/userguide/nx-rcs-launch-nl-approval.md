

# Proactive brand approval email (required after registration)
<a name="nx-rcs-launch-nl-approval"></a>

**Important**  
In addition to the console registration, you must send a proactive brand approval email. Your registration cannot be fully approved until the carriers receive your brand authorization.

Both Dutch carriers (Odido and VodafoneZiggo) require proactive brand approval. After you submit your registration in the AWS End User Messaging console, send an email from your business email address to `aws-end-user-messaging-rcs-approvals@amazon.com` with the subject `RCS Brand Approval - [Your Brand Name]`, granting permission for Infobip to use your brand name and logo to send RCS messages to users of the Odido and VodafoneZiggo networks in the Netherlands, and including your Agent ID (RCS for Business ID).

**Note**  
The email must be sent from the same business email address listed as the brand contact in your registration form. No additional documents or attachments are required for the Netherlands.

For general compliance guidance that applies to all countries, see [How to get set up](nx-rcs-get-set-up.md).
