

# Registration form (console)
<a name="nx-rcs-launch-es-console"></a>

The Spain launch registration uses a custom form that collects brand information (auto-populated from your testing agent), use case selection, your legal company name, industry, company address (address, city, region, postal code, country), the brand contact mobile number in international format, estimated monthly volume, a screen recording, and privacy policy and terms of service URLs. Spain does not require any out-of-band emails or third-party brand verification letters, but it does require registration in the CNMC National Alias Registry before your RCS agent can be launched.
