

# Registration form (console)
<a name="nx-rcs-launch-mx-console"></a>

The Mexico launch registration uses a custom form with one additional field beyond the standard baseline: a **Company name** field for your legal company name as registered in Mexico, which is required in addition to the brand display name. The form also collects brand information (auto-populated from your testing agent), use case selection, a screen recording, and privacy policy and terms of service URLs.

**Note**  
The Mexico registration form does not require a video URL; the standard screen recording field is used instead. The company name field is separate from the brand display name and should contain your full legal company name. Mexico does not have an out-of-band approval process. After you submit your registration in the AWS End User Messaging console, the carrier review proceeds through the standard approval workflow.
