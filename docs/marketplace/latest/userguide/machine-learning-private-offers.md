

# Creating private offers for machine learning products

 You can negotiate and offer a private offer directly to customers for your machine learning products. For more information on private offers, see [Preparing a private offer for your AWS Marketplace product](private-offers-overview.md). 

**Prerequisites:**
+  You must have a paid listing in AWS Marketplace. 
+  You must have access to AWS Partner Central. 

You create private offers for machine learning products in AWS Partner Central. For the procedures, see [Creating and managing private offers](creating-private-offer.md).

**Private offer settings for machine learning products**
+ On the **Configure offer pricing and duration** page, the pricing options depend on your product. For more information, see [Private offers for ML products](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/private-offers-supported-product-types.html#ml-private-offers).
+ For usage pricing, the usage-based rates apply only during the offer term. For contracts, the usage-based rates apply only after the contract term expires, and they are perpetual.
+ For installment plans, see [Private offer installment plans](installment-plans.md).

**Important**  
For linked accounts to benefit from a private offer:  
On the **Add buyers** page, include the payer AWS account ID.
The payer account must accept the hourly terms of the private offer first.
After the payer account accepts, linked accounts can then accept the private offer.

To send the offer to your buyer after you create it, see [Sending a private offer to a buyer](creating-private-offer.md#send-private-offer).
