

# Add-ons

The **Add-ons** step of your listing collects the AWS Marketplace features that help buyers discover, evaluate, and buy your product, so you can see and turn on all of them in one place.

**Topics**
+ [Opening the Add-ons step](#add-ons-opening)
+ [Available add-ons](#available)

## Opening the Add-ons step
<a name="add-ons-opening"></a>

To open the Add-ons step, do the following:

1. Sign in to the AWS Partner Central console at `https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home` and open your product from the **Products and services** page.

1. On the product's overview page, open the **Add-ons** step of **Complete your listing**.

## Available add-ons
<a name="available"></a>

The following four add-ons are available now. More will be added over time, and as new AWS Marketplace features become available.
+ **Request Demo or Private Offer**
+ **Express Private Offers**
+ **Promotional Media**
+ **Trust Agent**

Before this change, you had to go to different parts of the listing experience to enable these features on your product. With this change, everything is consolidated in one place. The pages for each feature still carry the detail on how it works and what it costs; this page is where you turn them on.


| Add-on | Where the detail lives | 
| --- | --- | 
| Request Demo or Private Offer | [Adding private offer and demo request buttons](creating-private-offer.md#private-offer-requests-demos) | 
| Express Private Offers | [Express private offers](express-private-offers.md) | 
| Promotional Media | [Enhance your AWS Marketplace product with promotional media](promotional-media.md) | 
| Trust Agent | [Connecting a trust center to your product listing](connecting-trust-center.md) | 

**Note**  
Express private offers and the private-offer request buttons are Offers features, documented in the Offers chapters.
