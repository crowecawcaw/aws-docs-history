# Creating private offers

To subscribe to your professional services products, potential buyers must request a
private offer from you. AWS Marketplace sends a request email to the root user address of your
seller account. The following steps explain how to respond to the requests.

###### Note

When you create a private offer worth more than $250,000 through the AWS Marketplace Management Portal,
additional approval may be required. For more information, contact the Private Offer
Success Team (POST), Vendor Finance Success (VFS), or Seller Reporting teams through
the [AWS Marketplace Management Portal](../../../proserve-variable-payment/aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management/contact-us.md "../../../proserve-variable-payment/aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management/contact-us.md").

1. Contact the customer to resolve any questions about the
   request. Agree on the offer terms before creating the offer. The buyer is not obligated to purchase
   your product, so it makes sense to agree before creating the
   offer.
2. Sign in to the [AWS Marketplace Management Portal](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management/ "https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/management/").
3. Select **Offers** from the menu, then select **Create
   private offer**.
4. On the **Create private offer** page, select the
   product that you want to create a private offer for. Only currently available
   products are included.
5. Enter the **Buyer account Ids** that you want to extend a
   private offer to. You can include up to 25 accounts in a single private offer.
   If the buyer used the request an offer feature, the email message that you
   received includes the buyer account Id for the requesting account.
6. Choose whether to allow buyers to pay for the product in installments.
   Typically, short contracts are paid in one payment. Longer contracts may have
   the option to pay in installments, but this is part of the agreement that you
   come to with the buyer. Select **Next**.
7. Complete the offer details, including the following information:
   - Enter **Offer information** such as an
     offer name and description, then select the appropriate **Renewal** option.
   - Enter the **Offer expiration date**. The
     date sets the amount of time the buyer has to subscribe to the offer.
     After 23:59:59 UTC on the set date, the offer becomes unavailable.
   - Choose **Next**, then choose
     **Next** again.

8. On the **Configure offer pricing and duration** page, choose
   a pricing option, contract duration, offer currency, and product dimension.

###### Note

    * To use the installment plan pricing option, refer to [Creating an installment plan for a private offer](installment-plans.md#creating-a-payment-schedule "installment-plans.md#creating-a-payment-schedule"), earlier in this
     guide.
    * To use the variable-payments pricing option, refer to [Adding variable payments to a private
     offer](proserve-variable-payment.md#add-variable-payment "proserve-variable-payment.md#add-variable-payment") later in this
     section.

When finished, choose **Next**. 9. On the **Add buyers** page, provide an AWS account ID for each AWS Marketplace buyer you're extending
the private offer to, then choose **Next**.

###### Note

    * Each buyer must have an AWS account in an AWS Region that supports the offer currency.
    * To add another account ID, choose **Add another buyer**. You can add a
     maximum of 24 buyers to a private offer.

10. On the **Configure legal terms and offer
    documents** page, add any custom legal terms, then choose **Next**.

You can add a maximum of five files related to your private offer, such as
legal terms, a statement of work or a bill of materials. When the system creates
the offer, it merges these files into one document. 11. On the **Review and create** page,
review the details of your private offer then choose **Create offer** to
publish the offer and extend it to the buyers you chose. 12. After the offer appears on the
**Manage private offers** page, open
the **Actions** menu for the
offer, select **Copy offer URL**,
and email the URL to the prospective buyers.

###### Note

- Offers may take some time to publish.
- After the offer publishes, you can use the **Manage private offers** page to edit the offer until a buyer accepts it.
