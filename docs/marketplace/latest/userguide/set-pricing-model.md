

# Choosing your pricing model

You choose how buyers pay for your software when you create your public offer. On the **Set pricing model** page, you choose **Free**, **Usage - hourly**, or **Usage - per inference request**. For what each model means, see [Machine learning product pricing for AWS Marketplace](machine-learning-pricing.md). For the procedure, see [Creating and publishing your listing](create-new-listing.md).

You can't change the pricing model later, so if you plan to charge per inference, choose it now. With per-inference pricing, your container reports the inferences, as described in [Reporting usage for inference pricing](ml-inference-metering.md). Batch transform jobs and algorithm training jobs are always free or charged hourly by instance. With a paid pricing model, you can also offer a free trial of 5 to 31 days.
