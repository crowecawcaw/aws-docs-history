

# Adding test accounts to the allowlist

While your product has **Limited** visibility, only your seller account and the AWS accounts on its allowlist can view and test it. You can add accounts on the product's **Settings** tab, or from the **Share with others for testing** card on the **Complete your listing** progress bar. For the procedure, see [Updating the allowlist](ml-update-allowlist.md).

For more information about product statuses, see [Machine learning product status](ml-product-lifecycle.md#ml-product-status). When you're ready to change the visibility of your product, see [Updating product visibility](ml-update-visibility.md).
