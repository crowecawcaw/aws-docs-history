

# Cancelling a data subscription

To stop delivery, open the subscription on the **Data sharing** page and choose **Cancel subscription**. After you cancel, you stop receiving new files, and systems that read the dataset from your bucket also stop receiving new files. The files that AWS Marketplace already delivered stay in your bucket. You can subscribe to the dataset again later. Cancelling and creating a new subscription is also how you change the bucket or the key of a subscription.
