

# Updating product pricing

 You can modify the rates and free trial period of your machine learning product in AWS Marketplace, though the pricing model itself cannot be changed. Note that for paid models, price increases take effect after a 90-day notice period, on the first day of the following month. Additional price changes cannot be made during this notice period. 

**To update product pricing**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose the **Procurement** tab.

1. On the **Public offer** card, choose **Update**, and then choose **Update pricing terms**.

   The **Update** menu appears after your public offer is released. **Update pricing terms** appears after your product has at least one version.

1. On the **Update pricing terms** page, change the prices that you want to update.

1. Choose **Submit**.

   If your public offer is free, **Submit** is unavailable, because the offer has no pricing terms to update.

Your change request appears on the offer's **Requests log** tab. To open the offer, choose the link under **Offer ID** on the **Public offer** card of the **Procurement** tab. Its status moves from **Under review** to **Update in progress**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. To see why a request failed, choose the request name. While a request is in progress, the **Update** menu on the **Public offer** card is unavailable.
