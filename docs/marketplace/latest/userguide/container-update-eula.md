

# Updating your end-user license agreement (EULA) for container products on AWS Marketplace

As an AWS Marketplace seller, you can update the end-user license agreement (EULA) to use either the [Standard Contract for AWS Marketplace](standardized-license-terms.md#standard-contracts) or a custom EULA. Updates made to the EULA take effect for new users subscribing to your product and product renewals. To update your EULA, use the following procedure.

**To update your EULA**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose the **Procurement** tab.

1. On the **Public offer** card, choose **Update**, and then choose **Update EULA**.

   The **Update** menu appears after your public offer is released.

1. On the **Update EULA** page, for **Legal terms**, choose **Standard Contract for AWS Marketplace (SCMP)** or **Custom legal terms**.

1. If you chose **Custom legal terms**, under **Custom legal terms and other offer documents**, choose **Choose files**, and then select your files.

   You can upload up to 5 files in plain text (.txt) or PDF (.pdf) format. Each file and the total upload can be up to 30 MB. AWS Partner Central combines the files into one PDF, in the order that you upload them.

1. Choose **Submit**.

Your change request appears on the offer's **Requests log** tab. To open the offer, choose the link under **Offer ID** on the **Public offer** card of the **Procurement** tab. Its status moves from **Under review** to **Update in progress**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. To see why a request failed, choose the request name. While a request is in progress, the **Update** menu on the **Public offer** card is unavailable.
