

# Connecting a trust center to your product listing

A trust center is a page where your company shares its security certifications, audit reports, and compliance documents with buyers. When you connect your trust center to your AWS Marketplace listing, buyers can review this information on your product detail page and in agent mode. This helps them evaluate your product faster.

Connecting a trust center is optional.

## How trust center information appears to buyers
<a name="tc-how-it-appears"></a>

What buyers see depends on the provider that hosts your trust center.


| Provider | What appears on your listing | 
| --- | --- | 
| Drata or Vanta | Full compliance details and evidence documents, synced from your trust center. This can include certifications, evidence and documents, security controls, resilience, AI governance, FAQs, and updates. | 
| Other providers, including self-hosted trust centers | A link to your trust center. | 

For Drata and Vanta trust centers, buyers can download your public documents. For documents that you share only under a non-disclosure agreement (NDA), such as full audit reports, buyers request access in agent mode and accept your NDA through your trust center provider. Your trust center provider sends you the request, and the documents are released only if you approve it in your trust center.

## Prerequisites
<a name="tc-prerequisites"></a>
+ An AWS Marketplace seller account with a product listing.
+ A published trust center with a shareable URL. If you don't have one yet, see [Set up a trust center](#tc-set-up).
+ The **Provide product details** task for your listing is complete. The **Connect trust center** task stays locked until you complete it.

## Set up a trust center
<a name="tc-set-up"></a>

If you don't already have a trust center, contact Drata or Vanta to set one up. AWS Marketplace sellers get priority onboarding and support.

1. Sign in to the AWS Partner Central console and open your product.

1. Under **Complete your listing**, in **Step 1: Product details**, find the **Connect trust center** task and choose **Start**.

1. Under **How it works**, choose Drata or Vanta. An email to the provider opens, prefilled with your company name, AWS Marketplace seller account ID, product name, and contact details.

1. Review the email, fill in any missing details, and send it.

1. Add your certifications, audit reports, and security documentation on your provider's site, and publish your trust center. Your provider gives you a shareable URL.

**Note**  
To get started faster, you can sign up on Drata's sign-up page. From there you can set up a new trust center and import compliance items from an existing trust portal.

## Connect your trust center
<a name="tc-connect"></a>

1. Sign in to the AWS Partner Central console and open your product.

1. Under **Complete your listing**, in **Step 1: Product details**, find **Connect trust center** — optional and choose **Start**. You can also choose **Start** in the **Trust center** section of the **Product details** tab. The **Connect trust center** task stays locked until the **Provide product details** task is complete.

1. Under **Trust center details**, for **Provider**, choose the provider that hosts your trust center:
   + Choose Drata or Vanta if one of them hosts your trust center.
   + Choose Other for any other provider or a self-hosted page.

1. For **Trust center URL**, enter the URL of your published trust center, for example `https://trust.yourcompany.com`.

1. Choose **Confirm**.

When your trust center is connected, the **Connect trust center** task shows **View**.

**Note**  
If your company already has a Drata trust center associated with your seller account, AWS Marketplace might connect it automatically. Check the **Trust center** section of the **Product details** tab to confirm.

## View your connected trust center
<a name="tc-view"></a>

On the **Product details** tab, the **Trust center** section shows your provider and trust center URL.

## Edit or remove your trust center
<a name="tc-edit-remove"></a>

1. On the **Product details** tab, in the **Trust center** section, choose **Edit**.

1. Do one of the following:
   + To update your trust center, change the **Provider** or **Trust center URL**, then choose **Save changes**.
   + To disconnect your trust center from your listing, choose **Remove**, then choose **Save changes**.
