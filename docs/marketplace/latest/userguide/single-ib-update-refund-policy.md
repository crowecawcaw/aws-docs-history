

# Updating the refund policy for your EC2 Image Builder product on AWS Marketplace

As an AWS Marketplace seller, you can set the refund policy for your EC2 Image Builder components. If you want to change the refund policy for your product, you can use **Update refund policy**. The following procedure shows you how to update your refund policy.

**To update the refund policy**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose the **Procurement** tab.

1. On the **Public offer** card, choose **Update**, and then choose **Update refund policy**.

   The **Update** menu appears after your public offer is released.

1. Under **Refund policy**, edit your current refund policy.

   Include the terms of your policy and the contact information that buyers use to request a refund, in up to 500 characters. Your text replaces the current refund policy.

1. Choose **Submit**.

Your request appears on the offer's **Requests log** tab. To open the offer, choose the link under **Offer ID** on the **Public offer** card of the **Procurement** tab. The **Request status** moves from **Under review** to **Update in progress**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. While a request is in progress, the **Update** menu is unavailable.
