

# Onboarding customers to your product

A buyer subscribes to your product on the AWS Marketplace website or in the AWS RTB Fabric console. When the buyer accepts your offer, AWS Marketplace creates an agreement and a license for the subscription, and the buyer deploys your module in the AWS RTB Fabric console. AWS RTB Fabric verifies the subscription before the module runs.

For how you set usage instructions and associate your module so that buyers can fulfill the product, see [Creating your product](additional-product-types-creating.md).
