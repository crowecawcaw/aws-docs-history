

# Updating the allowlist

**To update the allowlist**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose the **Settings** tab.

1. In the **Allowlisted AWS accounts** section, choose **Edit**. If the section doesn't list any accounts yet, choose **Add AWS accounts**.

   You can also open this page from the **Share with others for testing** card on the **Complete your listing** progress bar. Both buttons are unavailable while your product has **Restricted** visibility.

1. On the **Update allowlist** page, in **Allowlisted AWS accounts**, enter the 12-digit AWS account IDs that can view and test your product, separated by commas.

   The field already lists the allowlisted accounts. To remove an account, delete its ID. You can enter up to 5,000 account IDs.

1. Choose **Submit**.

Your change request appears on the product's **Change history** tab as **Update product allowlist**. Its status moves from **Preparing** to **Applying**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. To see why a request failed, choose the request name.
