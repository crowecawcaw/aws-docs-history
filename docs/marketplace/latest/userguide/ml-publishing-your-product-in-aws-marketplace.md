

# Listing your machine learning product in AWS Marketplace

After you package your code into model package images or algorithm images, upload your images, and create your Amazon SageMaker AI resources, you can publish your machine learning product in AWS Marketplace.

You create the product on the **Products and services** page in AWS Partner Central. Then you complete the listing from the **Complete your listing** progress bar on the product's overview page, which has these steps:
+ **Step 1: Product details**
+ **Step 2: Delivery configuration**, where you add the first version of your product. For more information, see [Adding the initial version](add-initial-version.md).
+ **Step 3: Procurement configuration**
+ **Step 4: Testing and integrations**
+ **Step 5: Add-ons** (optional)

After you complete Step 2, choose **Advance to procurement configuration** to move your product to **Limited** visibility, so that you and allowlisted AWS accounts can test it. When your listing is ready, choose **Publish**, and then choose **Submit for review**. To change your product after that, see [Managing your machine learning products](ml-product-management.md).

Machine learning products aren't available when you choose the AWS European Sovereign Cloud catalog in AWS Partner Central.
