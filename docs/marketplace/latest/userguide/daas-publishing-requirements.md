

# DaaS publishing requirements

The following guidelines outline requirements for content offered as a Data as a Service (DaaS) product on AWS Marketplace. As the seller of a DaaS product, you're responsible for complying with these guidelines and the Service Terms for AWS Marketplace (including the Service Terms for AWS Marketplace Sellers).
+ DaaS products must not contain any illegal content (including content that infringes on third-party intellectual property rights), viruses, malware, or any other material that is harmful to others.
+ You must disclose your data handling practices (i.e., data collection, storage, usage, sharing, retention, deletion, and backup) to customers through customer-facing documentation or by request.
+ DaaS products may contain personal information (i.e., information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person) only if (a) you are legally authorized to share or sell such data in compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and rules of the relevant jurisdiction(s), and (b) it does not include sensitive personal data.
+ Sensitive personal data includes, but is not limited to, data on biometrics or genetics, racial or ethnic origin, demographic information, political opinions, religious or philosophical beliefs, sex or sexual orientation, trade union membership, personal medical or health information, personal payment or financial information (e.g., credit history), Social Security number or similar identifiers, geolocation data or other sensitive location data, personal contact information, data relating to children under 18 years of age, or personal authentication data.
+ If your DaaS product includes anonymized or aggregated information, it must be aggregated, anonymized, and deidentified such that it cannot be reidentified to a natural person even when combined with other data.
+ Product detail pages must be accurate and must specify if the DaaS product includes personal information or information pertaining to natural persons that is anonymized and aggregated. Product detail pages must not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights or include personal information (though the content itself may include personal information, subject to the restrictions above).
+ It is your obligation to ensure that your DaaS product offering complies with all applicable laws, regulations, and rules in jurisdictions applicable to your company and your customers, such as the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), U.S. DOJ Rule Preventing Access to U.S. Sensitive Personal Data and Government-Related Data by Countries of Concern or Covered Persons (implementing Executive Order 14117), and others.
+ For compliance with applicable laws, regulations, and rules—or for other reasons—you may determine that it's necessary or preferred to restrict where your DaaS product is made available. You may configure country-specific availability of your DaaS product in the **Configure offer availability** section of the product setup process.
