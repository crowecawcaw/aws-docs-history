

# Preparing your solution

## What are solutions?
<a name="what-are-multi-product-solutions"></a>

 A solution positions your products on AWS Marketplace around a customer outcome rather than by product name. There are two types of solution, and which one you use depends on whether the outcome takes one product or several. 
+ **Single-product solution**: One product positioned for a specific customer, audience, or outcome. Use it to make an existing product discoverable by problem rather than by name.
+ **Multi-product solution**: Several products bundled into one customer outcome. This is the only type that uses offer sets, so a customer can transact the bundle as a single purchase.

 AWS Marketplace solutions help customers discover your products and make transactions simpler. You can showcase complete offerings that solve specific customer problems. 

 Solutions provide two key capabilities: 
+ **Solution listings**: Create dedicated solution pages that showcase how your AWS Marketplace products solve customer problems.
+ **Offer sets**: Process groups of private offers as a single transaction. Offer sets apply to multi-product solutions only, making it easier for customers to buy multiple products from your solution.

 Solution listings help customers find your offerings. Offer sets make buying easier when you sell a multi-product bundle to a specific customer.
