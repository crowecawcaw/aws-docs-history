

# DaaS products in AWS Marketplace

With Data as a Service (DaaS) products, you sell data and content through AWS Marketplace and deliver it to buyers yourself. You are responsible for managing buyer access, account creation, and delivery of your data. After you create your DaaS product, you can submit change requests and configure its properties in AWS Marketplace, including your product description, availability by country, pricing, and so on. You also integrate your product with AWS Marketplace to identify buyers, receive subscription events, and check entitlements. For usage-based pricing, you also report usage. The following topics provide you with information that you need to get started.

For assistance with your DaaS products, contact the AWS Marketplace Seller Operations team on the [AWS Partner Central and Marketplace Support page](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/support?region=us-east-1).
