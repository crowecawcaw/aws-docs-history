

# Making your DaaS product public in AWS Marketplace

After you test your Data as a Service (DaaS) product, request that it become public so that buyers can find it and subscribe. The AWS Marketplace Seller Operations team reviews your product against AWS Marketplace policies before it becomes public.

Use the following procedure to request public visibility.

**To make your DaaS product public**

1. In AWS Partner Central, open your product, and then choose **Request changes**, **Update visibility**, **Public**. At this point, you also set the prices of your pricing dimensions.

1. Wait for the AWS Marketplace Seller Operations team to review your product. For the requirements that your product must meet, see [DaaS publishing requirements](daas-publishing-requirements.md).

1. Make any changes or answer any questions that the team requests. The team sends requests by email to your seller account contact.

After the team approves your request, the product status changes to **Public**, and buyers can subscribe. For more information about product statuses, see [DaaS product lifecycle in AWS Marketplace](daas-product-lifecycle.md).
