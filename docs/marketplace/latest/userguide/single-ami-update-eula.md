

# Updating end user license agreement (EULA) for your AMI-based AWS Marketplace product

As an AWS Marketplace seller, you can update the end user license agreement (EULA) that will govern the use of your single Amazon Machine Image (AMI) product. Your EULA is located on the product listing page for public software listings on AWS Marketplace. You can either apply your own EULA or use the [Standard Contract for AWS Marketplace (SCMP)](standardized-license-terms.md). The following procedure shows you how to update the EULA for your single-AMI product.

For more information about the EULA, see [Using standardized contracts in AWS Marketplace](standardized-license-terms.md).

**To update a EULA**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose the **Procurement** tab.

1. On the **Public offer** card, choose **Update**, and then choose **Update EULA**.

   The **Update** menu appears after your public offer is released.

1. Under **Legal terms**, choose **Standard Contract for AWS Marketplace (SCMP)** or **Custom legal terms**.

1. If you chose **Custom legal terms**, under **Custom legal terms and other offer documents**, choose **Choose files**, and then select at least one custom legal terms document.

   You can upload up to 5 plain text (.txt) or PDF (.pdf) files, up to 30 MB in total, including supporting documents such as a statement of work. AWS Partner Central combines the files into one PDF, in the order that you upload them.

   If the offer already has custom legal terms, they appear in the **Offer documents** table, and your files are added after them. To replace them, choose **Remove** in that table before you upload.

1. Choose **Submit**.

Your request appears on the offer's **Requests log** tab. To open the offer, choose the link under **Offer ID** on the **Public offer** card of the **Procurement** tab. The **Request status** moves from **Under review** to **Update in progress**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. While a request is in progress, the **Update** menu is unavailable.
