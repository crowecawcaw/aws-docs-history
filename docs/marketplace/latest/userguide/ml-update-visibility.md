

# Updating product visibility

**To update product visibility**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, and sign in to your seller account.

1. Choose the product.

1. Choose **Edit visibility**.

   This option is available after your product leaves **Draft**.

1. In the **Update listing visibility** dialog box, under **Change to**, choose **Public** or **Restricted**.

   The options depend on your product's **Current visibility**:
   + **Limited**: You can choose **Restricted**. You can choose **Public** only when **Publish** is available on the **Complete your listing** progress bar.
   + **Public**: You can choose **Restricted**.
   + **Restricted**: You can choose **Public**.

1. (Optional) If you choose **Restricted**, enter a **Replacement product ID**.

   Existing subscribers keep access until their contract ends, and then move to the replacement product. If you leave this field blank, existing subscribers keep access to this product, but they can't renew contracts from private offers.

1. Choose **Update visibility**.

Your change request appears on the product's **Change history** tab as **Publish product** or **Restrict product**. Its status moves from **Preparing** to **Applying**, and then to **Succeeded** or **Failed**. To see why a request failed, choose the request name.

To make a product with **Limited** visibility public, you can also choose **Publish** on the **Complete your listing** progress bar, and then choose **Submit for review**. AWS Marketplace reviews your listing before it becomes public. The review usually takes 3 to 5 business days.
