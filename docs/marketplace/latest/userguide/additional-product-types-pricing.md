

# Pricing

RTB Fabric module products support contract pricing only. AWS RTB Fabric validates the buyer's entitlement before the module runs. As a result, these products are not metered. With contract pricing, the buyer purchases a contract for a duration of 1, 12, 24, or 36 months, with static quantities for the `Entitled` dimensions that you define.

Buyers are billed immediately when they accept the contract, unless it is a future-dated agreement.

Private offers are supported for RTB Fabric module products. Channel partner private offers are not available for these products at this time.
