

# Marking your products public

After you test your product, request that it become public so that buyers can find it and subscribe. The AWS Marketplace Seller Operations team reviews your product against AWS Marketplace policies before it becomes public.

Before you begin, make sure that you have finished testing your product in the `Limited` state and are ready to set its public pricing.

**To make your product public**

1. Open the [**Products and services**](https://console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/products) page in AWS Partner Central, open your product, and then choose **Request changes**, **Update visibility**, **Public**.

1. Set the prices of your pricing dimensions.

1. Wait for the AWS Marketplace Seller Operations team to review your product. This review can take several business days.

1. Make any changes or answer any questions that the team requests. The team sends requests by email to your seller account contact.

1. After the team approves your request, the product status changes to `Public`, and buyers can subscribe. For more information about product statuses, see [Product lifecycle for products integrated with AWS services](additional-product-types-lifecycle.md).
