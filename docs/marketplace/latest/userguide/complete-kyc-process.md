

# Step 5: Complete the Know Your Customer (KYC) process
<a name="complete-kyc-process"></a>

The Know Your Customer (KYC) process verifies your business and the people who represent, own, or control it. AWS Marketplace uses regional invoicing entities to manage local tax, reporting, disbursement, and compliance requirements. Each entity is subject to local laws and regulations, including EU anti-money laundering directives and Korean financial transaction reporting requirements. Disbursements are the payments sent to your bank account.

## When KYC verification applies
<a name="when-kyc-applies"></a>

Your KYC requirements depend on where your business is registered and where your buyers are located. Follow the verification options and next steps shown in your seller settings. The following sections explain the requirements for each region.

### If your business is registered in Japan
<a name="when-kyc-applies-japan"></a>

Sellers registered in Japan must complete **Japan Installment Sales Act verification** to sell on AWS Marketplace, regardless of where their buyers are located. This is required under Japanese law and is a separate, self-service process. See [Japan Installment Sales Act verification](#japan-isa-verification).

### If your business is registered in South Korea
<a name="when-kyc-applies-korea"></a>

To receive disbursements from the AWS South Korea invoicing entity, your business and bank account must be verified. Follow the verification options available in your seller settings:
+ **NHN KCP verification:** Verify your business and bank account together in AWS Partner Central. See [South Korea business and bank account verification](#korea-business-verification).
+ **Global KYC through Amazon Payments Europe:** Completed Global KYC with the required bank account verification can also meet South Korea verification requirements. See [Global KYC requirements](#kyc-requirements).

You do not need to complete both KYC processes to receive disbursements from the AWS South Korea invoicing entity.

If you have already submitted verification to NHN KCP, continue that process. Contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support before changing verification processes.

If you have already completed Global KYC, find **South Korea** in the **Global Know Your Customer (KYC)** table in your seller settings. Check **Disbursement status** and **Next step**, and complete any bank account verification requested.

NHN KCP verification covers South Korea. If you use NHN KCP and also want to use the AWS EMEA invoicing entity, complete the [Global KYC requirements](#kyc-requirements) for EMEA separately.

### If your buyers are in Europe, the Middle East, and Africa (EMEA) or South Korea
<a name="when-kyc-applies-buyer-region"></a>

Verification is required to use the AWS Marketplace invoicing entities in the following regions:

1. Europe, Middle East, and Africa: transactions and disbursements

1. South Korea: disbursements

EMEA  
Complete Global KYC to use the AWS EMEA invoicing entity. Transactions through this entity are processed by Amazon Payments Europe, S.C.A. (APE), a licensed electronic money institution in Luxembourg. Until you complete KYC, AWS Inc. is the invoicing entity for your transactions in this region. See [Global KYC requirements](#kyc-requirements) for the requirements and steps.  
For a Channel Partner Private Offer (CPPO), both the Channel Partner and the independent software vendor (ISV) must be KYC verified to use AWS EMEA as the invoicing entity. If either party is not KYC verified, AWS Inc. is the default invoicing entity.  
For information about how the invoicing entity affects tax handling, see [Tax handling for AWS Marketplace sellers](https://aws.amazon.com/tax-help/marketplace-sellers/tax-grid/). For the full terms, see the [AWS Marketplace EMEA Agreement](https://d1.awsstatic.com/onedam/marketing-channels/website/aws/en_US/legal/approved/amazon-payments-europe-aws-marketplace-emea-agreement.pdf).

South Korea  
To receive disbursements from the AWS South Korea invoicing entity, complete business and bank account verification. If your business is registered in South Korea, see [If your business is registered in South Korea](#when-kyc-applies-korea). If your business is registered outside South Korea, follow the verification options and next steps shown in your seller settings.

**Sellers in India**  
This process doesn't apply to sellers in India, as they can only sell to buyers in India. For detailed information, see [Getting started as a seller in India](getting-started-seller-india.md).

**KYC email notifications**  
For Amazon Payments Europe KYC, status updates are sent to your AWS account root email address and the custom email address you provide during registration. Add the people who manage your KYC verification to your custom email notifications so they receive status updates and requests. For more information, see [Adding or updating email addresses](email-notifications.md#adding-updating-email-addresses).  
For NHN KCP verification, monitor the point-of-contact and business-contact email addresses entered in the verification form. NHN KCP can send identity-call invitations and requests for corrections directly to those addresses.

## Japan Installment Sales Act verification
<a name="japan-isa-verification"></a>

Under Japan's Installment Sales Act, sellers registered in Japan must complete verification to sell on AWS Marketplace. This requirement is based on where your business is registered and is separate from Global KYC through Amazon Payments Europe. See [Global KYC requirements](#kyc-requirements). Complete Japan verification in AWS Partner Central.

**Interactive demo available in Japanese**  
To preview the verification screens before you begin, view the [interactive Japan Installment Sales Act verification demo](https://awsmarketplace.storylane.io/share/300dmygmsvyg). The demo walks through each step with screens shown in Japanese.

**What it verifies:** your business details and legal representative identity.

**Before you start:** your business registration number and business address are filled automatically from your **Tax settings** in the Billing console. If your tax registration is incomplete, the verification form is unavailable — complete your tax information in **Tax settings** first, and then return to this page.

**To complete Japan verification**

1. Sign in to AWS Partner Central at [https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home) and choose **Settings**.

1. Choose the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** tab, and then locate **Japan Know Your Customer (KYC)**.

1. Choose **Start verification**. (If you have already verified, choose **Update verification** to make changes.)

1. **Step 1: Business details** — review your business information (registration number and address are pre-filled from your tax settings), add business information, and add any tags. Choose **Save and continue**.

1. **Step 2: Legal representative information** — enter the legal representative's personal and contact details. Choose **Save and continue**.

1. **Step 3: Review and submit** — review the information you've provided, and then choose **Submit**. You can choose **Edit** to return to a previous step.

You can leave the wizard at any time. If you have unsaved changes on a step, you're prompted to save them before navigating away; your saved progress is retained so you can resume where you left off.

**After you submit:** your submission is reviewed, and you receive email notifications to your AWS account root email address and custom email address when your status changes. In **Settings**, the **Japan Know Your Customer (KYC)** card shows these verification statuses:
+ **Not started** — you haven't begun verification.
+ **Under review** — your submission is being verified.
+ **Verified** — verification is complete.
+ **Action required** — more information is needed. Correct the issues shown and resubmit.
+ **Failed** — verification could not be completed. Contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

If you update your details after verification, submitting the changes starts reverification. Check your seller settings and email for updates.

## South Korea business and bank account verification
<a name="korea-business-verification"></a>

This section explains how businesses registered in South Korea verify their business and bank account through NHN KCP in AWS Partner Central. Before you start, review [If your business is registered in South Korea](#when-kyc-applies-korea) for your verification options and requirements.

**Interactive demo available in Korean**  
To preview the verification screens before you begin, view the [interactive South Korea business and bank account verification demo](https://awsmarketplace.storylane.io/share/nml3qn2oaxy6). The demo shows verification screens in Korean.

**What it verifies:** your business, its representatives and owners, and the bank account you use to receive disbursements.

**Before you start:** complete your Korean tax registration in **Tax settings** in the Billing console.

The form fills in your business registration number (사업자등록번호) and registered business address from **Tax settings**. The number has 10 digits and appears as **Tax registration number (TRN)**. You cannot edit these details in the verification form.

Check that the number and address match your registration documents. To correct either one, update **Tax settings** and refresh the verification page before you submit. If you have already submitted an incorrect business registration number, contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

Set up your USD disbursement preference by selecting the bank account for your USD disbursements. Prepare a bankbook copy (통장 사본) for that account. For instructions, see [Step 4: Set disbursement preferences](set-disbursement-preferences.md).

### Prepare your documents
<a name="korea-verification-documents"></a>

The documents you need depend on your business and the people included in the form. Use the list below to prepare, and follow the requirements shown in your verification form:
+ **Business registration document:** select the document type that matches your business and upload it. The form accepts a business registration certificate (사업자등록증), unique identification number certificate (고유번호증), certificate of business registration (사업자등록증명원), or certificate of taxpayer identification number (납세번호증).
+ **Certificate of complete corporate registration (법인등기사항전부증명서, 법인등기부등본):** if requested, provide the complete certificate, issued within the last 3 months. Partial or view-only copies are not accepted.
+ **Identity documents (신분증):** provide the documents requested for the representative or appointed delegate completing verification. These depend on the person's nationality and residency.

  For a Korean contact, the form accepts a resident registration ID (주민등록증) or driver's license (운전면허증). For a foreign contact, it requests a passport (여권) and one of the following: foreigner registration ID (외국인등록증), permanent resident ID (영주증), or domestic residence report ID (외국국적동포 국내거소신고증).
+ **Letter of authorization (위임장):** your legal representative is the person authorized to represent your business. If someone else completes verification on their behalf, provide the authorization document requested in the form.
+ **Corporate seal certificate (법인인감증명서):** provide the certificate requested for each representative. It must have been issued within the last 3 months.
+ **Corporate seal stamp (법인 인감 도장):** if the form requests a separate seal image, upload a clear image of the seal impression that matches the certificate.
+ **Use seal certificate (사용인감계):** if you use a different seal (사용인감) instead of the registered corporate seal, provide this certificate as instructed in the form.
+ **Proof of actual owner (실제소유자 확인 서류):** provide documents showing who owns or controls the business registered as your AWS Marketplace seller. The form accepts a stockholders' list (주주명부), auditor's report (감사보고서), statement of changes in shareholders' equity (주식변동사항명세서), employee list (사원명부), or list of investors/contributors (출자자명부).

  If the form offers an exemption from individual owner details, upload the requested supporting documents. For an exemption based on DART financial reporting, upload the requested business or annual report (사업보고서, DART 공시). NHN KCP reviews your exemption and supporting documents.
+ **Bankbook copy (통장 사본):** provide the copy for the bank account selected for your USD disbursements.

Follow the instructions displayed with each upload. If a document contains a Korean resident registration number (주민등록번호), cover the last 7 digits as instructed. Documents must be clear and readable. NHN KCP can request additional documents after reviewing your submission.

### Complete NHN KCP verification
<a name="korea-verification-steps"></a>

If you have already submitted verification to NHN KCP, continue that process. Contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support before changing verification processes. For your options, see [If your business is registered in South Korea](#when-kyc-applies-korea).

**Open the NHN KCP verification form**

1. Sign in to AWS Partner Central at [https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home) and choose **Settings**.

1. Choose the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** tab and locate **South Korea Know Your Customer (KYC)**, if available.

1. Choose **Start verification** to begin or **Update verification** to continue an existing verification. You can also open the [NHN KCP verification form](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/seller-verification/business-verification/KR?region=us-east-1) directly. Follow the verification options and next steps shown for your account.

The following steps describe a new verification. When updating a submitted verification, choose **Continue** where these steps say **Save and continue**, and follow any correction requests in the form.

**Complete the verification form**

1. On **Provide business details**, check the information from your tax settings. Enter the remaining business details and upload the requested documents. Choose **Save and continue**.

1. On **Provide representative information**, enter the details for each representative and contact requested by the form. Upload the required identity, seal, and authorization documents. Choose **Save and continue**.

1. On **Add beneficial owner information**, enter the requested details about the people who own or control your business (실제소유자), or select an exemption if it applies. Upload the required documents and choose **Save and continue**.

1. On **Verify bank account**, check that the last four digits match your USD disbursement account, and upload its bankbook copy (통장 사본). Choose **Save and continue**.

1. On **Review and submit**, check your information and documents. Choose **Edit** if you need to correct a previous step. When ready, choose **Submit**.

You can leave the form and return later to continue from your saved progress.

If you cannot start verification, or a message says your business registration number is already in use, contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

### After you submit
<a name="korea-verification-review"></a>

After you submit, NHN KCP reviews your information and documents. Check your seller settings for verification updates and requests for information. Review time varies, particularly if NHN KCP needs more documents or a video call to verify identity.

In **Settings**, the **South Korea Know Your Customer (KYC)** card can show these verification statuses:
+ **Not started** — verification has not started. Follow the available verification option.
+ **Under review** — verification is being reviewed. Check for requests in the form or email.
+ **Verified** — the card shows a completed verification status. Check your disbursement status for any remaining requirements.
+ **Action required** — follow the requested corrections, document uploads, or bank account verification steps.
+ **Failed** — verification could not be completed. Contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

To check your South Korea disbursement status, open the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** tab in **Settings**. Under **Global Know Your Customer (KYC)**, find **South Korea** in the table, check **Disbursement status**, and follow **Next step**. A **Verified** status on the verification card does not by itself confirm that disbursements are enabled.

NHN KCP may email the point of contact and business contact listed in your form to arrange a recorded video call. Check these email accounts, including their spam folders. Follow the invitation to schedule the call for your representative or authorized contact and prepare the required identification.

During the call, the representative or authorized contact shows their identification to the camera. When showing a resident registration ID (주민등록증), cover the last 7 digits of the resident registration number (주민등록번호), as instructed by NHN KCP.

If NHN KCP requests corrections, check the messages in your form and any email from NHN KCP. Open your existing verification and correct the requested information or replace rejected documents. Check the requested changes for each affected representative and beneficial owner, then resubmit.

If the required action is unclear or you cannot make the change, contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support. Include the status and message shown in the form.

If the verification page displays **Verification could not be completed**, contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

### If you change your bank account
<a name="korea-verification-bank-changes"></a>

If you change the bank account selected for USD disbursements after business verification is complete, the new account must also be verified.

In your seller settings, return to **South Korea Know Your Customer (KYC)** and choose **Update verification**, if available. If the form opens directly at **Verify bank account**, follow these steps:

1. On **Verify bank account**, check that the last four digits match your new USD disbursement account. Upload that account's bankbook copy (통장 사본).

1. Continue to **Review and submit**. Check the bank account information and choose **Submit**. You do not need to enter your approved business information again.

If the form shows the wrong account, check your USD disbursement preference and refresh the page. If the account still does not match, the bank step is unavailable, or you cannot submit, contact AWS Marketplace Seller Support.

## Global KYC requirements
<a name="kyc-requirements"></a>

Global KYC through Amazon Payments Europe requires information and documents to verify your identity and business. Before you start, make sure you can monitor your AWS account root email address. The KYC team sends status updates and requests to that address. For more information, see [Managing account communications](managing-account-communications.md) in [Managing your seller account](seller-account-management.md).

The following requirements apply to the Global KYC process. If you use NHN KCP for South Korea verification, follow [South Korea business and bank account verification](#korea-business-verification) for that process.

Global KYC includes business verification, bank account verification, and optional secondary-user verification:

Step 1: Business verification  
Provide information and documents about your business. Register and verify key individuals in your organization, including the point of contact, business owners, and legal representatives.  
Provide identity and proof of address documents for these individuals. If someone is not legally authorized to represent the business, you might also need an authorization document on company letterhead, signed by a legal representative.

Step 2: Bank account verification  
After business verification is complete, you must provide a bank statement on the **Payment information** tab before you can receive disbursements through Amazon Payments Europe. See [Step 6: Complete bank account verification](complete-bank-verification.md).

Step 3: Secondary-user verification (optional)  
Only authorized users can manage your KYC and financial details after verification. Complete this optional step if you want other users in your organization to manage these details, such as finance team members who manage disbursement settings. See [Managing secondary users for Know Your Customer (KYC)](managing-secondary-users.md).

**Important**  
All provided documents must be clear, legible, and on official letterhead where applicable. Business documents should be signed by a legal representative and issued within 180 days unless otherwise specified.  
The verification team can request additional information or documents after reviewing your submission. Follow the requirements shown in your form and any requests from the team.

## Complete Global KYC business verification
<a name="complete-kyc-process-steps"></a>

Follow these steps for Global KYC business verification through Amazon Payments Europe. If you use NHN KCP for South Korea verification, follow [Complete NHN KCP verification](#korea-verification-steps) instead.

**Note**  
For accepted document types, templates, and formatting requirements referenced throughout these steps, see the [Templates and best practices for completing the KYC process](#kyc-best-practices) section at the end of this page.  
This process includes reviewing and accepting the [AWS Marketplace EMEA Agreement](https://d1.awsstatic.com/onedam/marketing-channels/website/aws/en_US/legal/approved/amazon-payments-europe-aws-marketplace-emea-agreement.pdf). Review it before you begin.

1. Sign in to AWS Partner Central at [https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home](https://us-east-1.console.aws.amazon.com/partnercentral/home) and choose **Settings**.

1. In the **Account summary** section, confirm that the **Country** that is shown is correct.
**Note**  
Choose the **Info** link to see how to change your country.

1. In the same section, review your **KYC Verification** status to see where you are in the verification process.

1. To start or review Global KYC, choose the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** tab.
**Note**  
Under **Global Know Your Customer (KYC)**, the table shows customer regions, invoicing entities, transaction status, and disbursement status. It identifies the invoicing entity that issues your customer's invoices. If **Transaction status** or **Disbursement status** is **Blocked**, follow **Next step**.

1. To start or review your business verification, choose **Update Business Verification**. The KYC Registration Portal opens.

1. Enter the **Basic details** as directed, including selecting your entity type (such as privately-owned business or publicly listed company). After you review the Amazon Payments Europe Terms & Conditions, choose **Agree and continue**.
**Important**  
If the business registered as your AWS Marketplace seller is not publicly listed on a stock exchange, choose 'privately-owned business'.
Ensure the business name you enter matches your AWS Marketplace seller account name, as this is the company being verified.
Enter your business details exactly as shown in your official registration documents, including country of incorporation, entity type, name, and registration number.
Any discrepancies or mismatched information may delay the verification process.

   By continuing to the next page or step, you accept the Amazon Payments Europe Terms & Conditions.

   If you have questions, see **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)** located on the right side of the console.

1. Enter the required **Business information** as directed, and then choose **Next**.
**Note**  
A registration extract is your company registration or incorporation document.
For US companies, if the registration extract or incorporation document is older than 180 days, also provide a certificate of good standing, certificate of (account) status, or an unexpired business license.
For accepted proof of address documents, see [Accepted proof of address documents](#kyc-proof-of-address).
Your information is saved each time you choose **Next**.

1. Enter the required **Point of contact information** as directed, and then choose **Next**.
**Note**  
The point of contact manages the AWS Marketplace account on behalf of your business. Where possible, use the seller account's root user. If this person does not have legal capacity to represent your business, upload a letter of authorization (LOA) in **Additional documents**. See [Letter of authorization template](#kyc-loa-template).
Provide accepted identity and proof of address documents. See [Templates and best practices for completing the KYC process](#kyc-best-practices).

1. Choose whether the **Beneficial owner** is the same as the point of contact, add beneficial owners (up to four) if necessary, confirm your additions, and then choose **Next**.
**Note**  
If the beneficial owner is not the point of contact, register at least one beneficial owner or senior managing official on the account.
For a privately owned company, register an individual who directly or indirectly owns more than 25% of the shares or voting rights in your business. If this does not apply, register a senior managing official who controls the company through other means, such as a chief executive officer, chief financial officer, managing or executive director, or president.
For a publicly listed company, register a senior managing official, such as a chief executive officer, chief financial officer, managing or executive director, or president. Enter their details and upload their documents in **Beneficial owner**.
Provide accepted identity and proof of address documents. See [Templates and best practices for completing the KYC process](#kyc-best-practices).

1. Choose whether the **Legal representative** is the same as the point of contact or beneficial owner. If the legal representative is a different person, provide the required information, save your entry, and then choose **Next**.

1. In the **Additional documents** section, upload your letter of authorization (if applicable) and statute documents.
**Note**  
**Letter of authorization (LOA):** If the point of contact does not have legal capacity to represent your business, provide a letter confirming their authorization. See [Letter of authorization template](#kyc-loa-template).
**Statute document:** This is a governing document for your company. It should include articles of association, bylaws, or the most recent share allotment document, such as a statement of capital, annual return, or share register. For a privately owned company, it should include the full names of each beneficial owner who directly or indirectly owns more than 25% of the shares. If this information is missing, provide an organization chart showing the entire structure of your business. See [Organization chart template](#kyc-org-chart). Requirements vary by country and legal entity type. Provide a document that most closely matches these descriptions.
Ensure these additional documents are signed by a legal representative, on company letterhead or stamped, and dated within 180 days. If more documents are needed, the KYC team contacts you at your AWS account root email address and can provide an example template.

1. On **Review and Submit**, check your information and documents.

   Choose **Edit** to correct a previous section.

1. Choose **Submit for verification**.

Amazon Payments Europe reviews your submission and emails you when the review is complete.

Return to **Settings** to check your status in **Account summary**. Choose the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** tab for verification updates, disbursement status, and next steps.

**Important**  
After your KYC is verified, you must provide a bank statement on the **Payment information** tab before you can receive disbursements through Amazon Payments Europe.

If you need to add secondary users who can manage KYC information and financial details, see [Managing secondary users for Know Your Customer (KYC)](managing-secondary-users.md) in [Managing your seller account](seller-account-management.md).

## Templates and best practices for completing the KYC process
<a name="kyc-best-practices"></a>

The following identity and proof-of-address document requirements describe the Amazon Payments Europe process. For documents requested by NHN KCP for South Korea verification, see [Prepare your documents](#korea-verification-documents). For authorization documents, follow the instructions and template linked from your verification form.

### Accepted identity documents
<a name="kyc-identity-documents"></a>

If you need to provide an identity document for an individual, the following documents are accepted:
+ Passport
+ National identity card
+ US passport card
+ Driving license
+ Residence permit

Requirements for identity documents:
+ Document copy/image must be high quality, in color, unobstructed, and legible.
+ Document size should be less than 10MB.
+ Accepted formats include: .png, .tiff, .tif, .jpg, .jpeg, and .pdf.
+ The document must be a copy of a government-issued ID document containing a photo and personal information.
+ The document must contain full name, date of birth, place of birth, and country of citizenship. If one identity document does not contain all these details, provide two documents together, such as a driving license and birth certificate.
+ The document must not be expired.
+ If the identity document has two sides, both sides must be uploaded.
+ The signature page of the document should be provided, where applicable.

### Accepted proof of address documents
<a name="kyc-proof-of-address"></a>

The following documents are accepted as proof of address:
+ Utility bill (gas, water, electricity, TV, Internet, mobile phone, or landline)
+ Bank statement (documents issued by a financial services provider other than a bank, such as third-party providers or online digital banks, are not acceptable as proof of address)
+ Credit union or building society statement
+ Credit card statement or bill
+ Mortgage statement
+ Rent receipt from a local council or letting agent

Requirements for proof of address documents:
+ The proof of address must show the provider's logo.
+ The proof of address must be addressed to the corresponding person or legal entity (names should match the ID/legal document provided).
+ The full name and country of residence must be visible on the document. Other sensitive information such as account balance or card number can be covered.
+ The document must not be a screenshot.
+ The document must be dated within 180 days.

### Letter of authorization template
<a name="kyc-loa-template"></a>

If you need a letter of authorization, you can use the following sample:

```
Letterhead of the company

POWER TO ACT ON BEHALF OF THE COMPANY
The undersigned **Enter Company name here** (herein after, the "Company"), duly represented

approve new Secondary users added to the account and if required, grant them access to
update listings, respond to buyers and initiate refunds.

    Dated this:

    --------------------------------------------------------------------------
    Signed By:
```

### Organization chart template
<a name="kyc-org-chart"></a>

If requested, provide an organization chart showing the entire structure of your business with your statute document. The following example includes parent companies, subsidiaries, and beneficial owners:

![Example ownership chart showing parent companies and Sally Griffith's 39% indirect ownership. The following text describes the chart and its notes.](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/images/kyc-organization-chart.png)


In this example, three parent companies own the seller business:
+ General Partnership A owns 10%. Its partners are Mr John Smith and Company D. The chart includes the partners of a general partnership, including any parent company.
+ Company B owns 40%. Company E owns 80% of Company B, and Company F owns the other 20%. Company E has multiple shareholders, none of whom owns more than 25% of Company E.
+ Company C owns 50%. Company G owns 22% of Company C, and Company H owns the other 78%. Sally Griffith owns all of Company H. She therefore indirectly owns 39% of the seller business (50% × 78% × 100%).

The chart includes each company's registration number and country of incorporation. It also includes the state or province for companies in the United States or Canada.

The example's summary identifies Sally Griffith as a beneficial owner and gives her ownership percentage. It states that no other person directly or indirectly owns more than 25% of the seller business's shares. The notes describe beneficial owners as people who directly or indirectly own more than 25% of the shares or voting rights, or control the business through other means. The summary includes their full names, ownership percentages, and any information needed to explain the ownership structure.

The notes also describe a chart signed by a legal representative or senior managing official, such as a chief executive officer, chief financial officer, managing director, or president. The chart is dated within 180 days, includes the signer's full name, date, and position, and is printed on registered company letterhead or stamped with a registered company stamp.

### General best practices
<a name="kyc-general-best-practices"></a>

Consider these best practices when completing the KYC process:
+ Prepare the required documents before you start.
+ Use clear, legible documents that meet the date requirements for your verification process.
