

# Getting support for AWS Marketplace Storefront
<a name="getting-support-storefront"></a>

For help with AWS Marketplace Storefront, contact the Storefront support team. For questions about your AWS account or AWS Marketplace billing, contact AWS Customer Service. For questions about the software that you buy through a storefront, contact the seller of that product.

Use the following table to find the right contact for your question.


| If your question is about | Contact | How to contact | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| Setting up, configuring, or using a storefront, including branding, single sign-on (SSO), connectors, AWS Marketplace accounts, users, and insights | AWS Marketplace Storefront support | Email [awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com](mailto:awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com). For details, see [Contacting AWS Marketplace Storefront support](#contact-storefront-support). | 
| Your AWS account, AWS Marketplace charges, invoices, or payment methods | AWS Customer Service | Create a case in the [AWS Support Center console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home). | 
| Installing, using, or troubleshooting a product that you bought through a storefront | The product seller | Use the support information on the product detail page. | 
| Private offers, disbursements, or seller account setup, if you sell or resell products through your storefront | AWS Marketplace seller support teams | Use the support form in AWS Partner Central. For details, see [Getting support for AWS Marketplace](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/latest/userguide/getting-support.html) in the *AWS Marketplace Seller Guide*. | 

## Contacting AWS Marketplace Storefront support
<a name="contact-storefront-support"></a>

To contact the Storefront support team, send an email to [awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com](mailto:awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com). You can contact the team for help with the following:
+ Creating, designing, and deploying storefronts
+ Setting up SSO and managing storefront users
+ Linking AWS Marketplace accounts to your organization
+ Setting up and troubleshooting connectors
+ Viewing insights and reports
+ Changing your Storefront subscription tier or add-ons
+ Reporting an issue or requesting a feature

**Protect your credentials**  
Don't include passwords, access keys, client secrets, certificates, or other credentials in your support request.

## Information to include in your support request
<a name="storefront-support-before-you-contact"></a>

To help the support team resolve your issue faster, include the following information in your request:
+ Your AWS account ID. For instructions, see [Finding your AWS account ID](#storefront-support-find-account-id).
+ Your organization name and the name or URL of the affected storefront.
+ A description of the issue, including the steps that you took and the result that you expected.
+ The date and time that the issue occurred, including your time zone.
+ The exact text of any error messages, and screenshots if available.
+ The connector or identity provider involved, if the issue is related to a connector or SSO.

### Finding your AWS account ID
<a name="storefront-support-find-account-id"></a>

**To find your AWS account ID**

1. Sign in to the [AWS Management Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/console/home).

1. In the navigation bar on the upper right, choose your account name.

   Your 12-digit account ID appears in the menu.

## Support options by subscription tier
<a name="storefront-support-by-tier"></a>

The support options available to you depend on your Storefront subscription tier. All tiers include email support, and the Storefront support team responds to support requests within 2 business days.


| Tier | Support included | 
| --- | --- | 
| Standard | Email support | 
| Professional | Email support and priority support | 
| Enterprise | Email support, priority support, and a dedicated technical account manager (TAM) | 

A dedicated TAM helps Enterprise customers plan storefront rollouts, set up connectors and procurement workflows, and resolve escalated issues.

**TAM availability**  
Dedicated TAM availability varies by country. To confirm availability in your location, contact [awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com](mailto:awsmp-storefront-support@amazon.com).

For subscription tier features and pricing, see the [AWS Marketplace Storefront product listing](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace/pp/prodview-x7mdp3gstc6xa) in AWS Marketplace.

## Getting help with billing and your AWS account
<a name="storefront-support-aws-cs"></a>

You buy your Storefront subscription through AWS Marketplace, and AWS Marketplace handles all Storefront billing. For questions about Storefront charges, invoices, payment methods, or your AWS account, create a case with AWS Customer Service in the [AWS Support Center console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/support/home).

**Required permissions**  
You must have the required permissions to create cases in the AWS Support Center console. For more information, see [AWS Support identity-based policy examples](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/awssupport/latest/user/security_iam_id-based-policy-examples.html) in the *AWS Support User Guide*.
