# Amending your future dated

agreements

You can increase your purchased units of a particular dimension in your FDA after the
agreement start date. This option is possible when the agreement doesn't have a flexible payment
schedule. For more details, see [Flexible payment scheduler](../userguide/flexible-payment-scheduler.md "../userguide/flexible-payment-scheduler.md").

You will be charged the pro-rated amount on the agreement start date when your amendment is
complete. If your start date is in the past, you'll be charged immediately.
