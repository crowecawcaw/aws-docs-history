# Product types eligible for private offers

You can get private offers for the following product types.

| Offer type                            | Description                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Data products                         | For more information, see [Accepting a<br>Private Offer](../../../data-exchange/latest/userguide/subscribe-to-private-offer.md "../../../data-exchange/latest/userguide/subscribe-to-private-offer.md") in the _AWS Data Exchange User Guide_.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| SaaS contract                         | With a software as a service (SaaS) contract, you can commit to upfront payment<br>for your expected usage of a SaaS product, or negotiate a flexible payment schedule<br>with the seller. Contract durations are one-month, one-year, two-year, or three-year<br>terms, or select a custom duration in months, up to 60 months. If you commit to an<br>upfront payment, you are billed in advance for the use of the product software.<br>If the seller offers a flexible payment schedule, you are billed along the<br>payment schedule dates at the amounts listed on the private offer.<br>The seller may also include negotiated pay-as-you-go pricing for usage above<br>your contracted usage.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| SaaS subscription                     | With a SaaS subscription, you agree to a price for use of a product. The seller<br>tracks and reports your usage to AWS Marketplace, and you're billed for what you use.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| AMI hourly                            | With Amazon Machine Image (AMI) hourly, you negotiate an hourly rate for using<br>an AMI, rounded up to the nearest hour.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| AMI hourly with annual                | With AMI hourly with annual, you negotiate the hourly and long-term pricing per<br>instance type. The long-term pricing is for the duration of the private offer, which<br>can be between 1 day and 3 years. If the seller creates a private offer without a<br>flexible payment schedule, you can run Amazon EC2 instances at the hourly price<br>determined in the private offer and optionally purchase upfront commitments for the<br>duration of the contract at the long-term price set in the private offer. If the<br>seller creates a private offer with a flexible payment schedule, you are billed with<br>the payment schedule dates for the amounts listed on the private offer regardless of<br>usage. In this type of private offer, the seller can include a number of Amazon EC2<br>instances per instance type that you can run without being charged the hourly price.<br>Any usage above what is included is then charged at the hourly price set in the<br>private offer. |
| AMI contract                          | With AMI contracts, you negotiate a contract price and the duration of the<br>contract, which can be between 1 and 60 months. If the seller creates a private<br>offer without a flexible payment schedule, at the time of acceptance, you can<br>configure the contract according to the price and options set in the private offer.<br>If the seller creates a private offer with a flexible payment schedule, you are<br>billed with the payment schedule dates at the amounts listed on the private offer.<br>In this type of private offer, the seller configures the contract in the private<br>offer and it can't be configured at the time of acceptance.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Container products                    | With container products, you negotiate hourly or annual pricing for the<br>container products that you use, by pod, task, or custom unit, matching the product<br>that you are purchasing. Container product private offers match AMI product private<br>offers.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| Machine learning hourly/per-inference | With hourly or per-inference pricing, you negotiate usage-based rates with the seller for a custom<br>duration. At the end of the duration, pricing reverts to public pricing and any instances that continue to run are billed at<br>the usage rates that the seller sets in the public offer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Machine learning contracts            | Private offers can be a contract with a fixed upfront fee for a specified number<br>of days. If the seller creates a private offer with a flexible payment schedule, you are<br>billed with the payment schedule dates at the amounts listed on the private offer. At the end of the contract,<br>any instances that continue to run are billed perpetually at the usage rates that the seller sets in the private offer.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| Professional services                 | All professional services offers are private offers. You must work with the<br>seller to create the private offer. See [Purchasing professional services products in AWS Marketplace](buyer-proserv-products.md "buyer-proserv-products.md") for more information.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
