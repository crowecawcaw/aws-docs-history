

# Understanding Offer Sets
<a name="understanding-offer-sets"></a>

When purchasing a Multi-Product Solution through a private offer, you'll receive an **Offer Set** — a grouped collection of private offers for each product in the solution.

## How Offer Sets Work
<a name="how-offer-sets-work"></a>

### Unified Review Process
<a name="unified-review-process"></a>
+ All offers are presented together with a complete view of products, pricing, and terms
+ Each product maintains its own pricing, payment terms, duration, and End User License Agreement (EULA)
+ You can review the total solution cost and individual component costs in one place

### One-Action Acceptance
<a name="one-action-acceptance"></a>
+ Accept all offers simultaneously with a single action
+ Streamline your internal approval process with consolidated cost visibility
+ Reduce time spent on procurement coordination

### Separate Agreements
<a name="separate-agreements"></a>
+ Each product creates its own agreement after acceptance
+ Manage individual product agreements independently
+ Maintain flexibility for renewals, modifications, and cancellations
