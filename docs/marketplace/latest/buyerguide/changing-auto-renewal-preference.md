# Changing your auto-renewal preference

You can opt in or opt out of auto-renewal any time before the renewal decision deadline.
Choose **Edit auto-renewal** in any of three places:

- On the **Manage subscriptions** page, select the agreement, and choose
  **Edit auto-renewal**.
- On the **Manage subscriptions** page, select the agreement, choose
  **Actions**, and then choose **Edit auto-renewal**.
- On the agreement's **Renewal details** tab, choose **Edit
  auto-renewal**.
  Your change takes effect immediately and applies to the upcoming renewal cycle. After the
  renewal decision deadline, you can no longer change your preference for that cycle. If the
  seller opts out, you see **Seller opted out** and can no longer opt in; to
  continue, request a new private offer from the seller.
