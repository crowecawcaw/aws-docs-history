# Purchasing a Multi-Product Solution

## Step 1: Request a Private Offer

###### Note

You can proceed directly to Step 3 if you have negotiated with the partner outside of Marketplace and have an offer set from the partner

- Navigate to the solution detail page for your chosen solution
- Click "Request Private Offer" to initiate contact with the seller
- Your request goes through automated verification and is sent to the partner
- The partner will contact you to discuss your specific requirements

## Step 2: Negotiate and Customize

Work with the partner to:

- Refine the solution components based on your needs
- Add or remove products from the solution
- Negotiate pricing, payment terms, and contract duration for each component
- Define implementation timelines and support requirements
- Discuss any custom integration or configuration needs

## Step 3: Review the Offer Set

Once the partner extends an offer set to you:

- **Access the Offer Set**: You'll receive an email notification with a link to review the offer set. Alternatively you can go AWS Console → AWS Marketplace → Private Offers to view the offer set.
- **Review Each Offer**: Click the offer set ID to see all included offers and their terms
- **Check Details**: Review pricing, contract duration, payment terms, and EULAs for each offer
- **Verify Total Cost**: Confirm the complete solution cost meets your budget
- **Attach Purchase Order (PO)**: Attach purchase orders if required (can use same PO or separate POs per charge)

## Step 4: Accept the Offer Set

- Review all offers one final time
- Click "Accept offer set" to accept all included offers simultaneously
- Receive confirmation that agreements have been created for each product
- Access your new subscriptions through AWS Marketplace
- Set up your account/launch from the confirmation page or Manage Subscriptions page
