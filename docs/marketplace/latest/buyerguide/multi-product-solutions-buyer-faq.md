## How is purchasing a Multi-Product Solution different from buying products individually?

With Multi-Product Solutions, you work with one partner who coordinates all components, complete one transaction that covers all products, and receive a unified view of your solution. However, each product still creates its own agreement, giving you flexibility to manage components independently after purchase.

## Can I still purchase individual products if I don't want the complete solution?

Yes. Each product in a solution maintains its own product detail page and can be purchased independently. The solution listing is an option for customers who want the complete integrated offering.

## What happens if I only want to renew some products from my solution?

Each product has its own agreement with independent terms. When renewal time comes, you can choose to renew specific products while letting others expire. Work with your partner to adjust your solution based on your current needs.

## Can I use my AWS committed spend (EDP/PPA) for Multi-Product Solutions?

Yes, if the products are eligible for AWS committed spend drawdown. Products deployed on AWS typically qualify. Eligibility is determined separately for each product in the solution.

## What if I have issues with one product in my solution?

Contact your partner who serves as the primary point of contact for the complete solution. They will coordinate with individual product vendors if needed. You also maintain the ability to contact product vendors directly through their normal support channels.

## How do I know which partner to choose if multiple partners offer similar solutions?

Evaluate partners based on their AWS competencies, customer reviews, industry expertise, implementation methodology, and support capabilities. Request private offers from multiple partners to compare pricing and terms. Use AWS Marketplace's AI-powered comparison tools to assess key differences.

## Are Multi-Product Solutions only available through private offers?

Yes. Multi-Product Solutions are currently offered through private offers, which allow partners to customize solutions for your specific requirements and negotiate terms that fit your needs.

## What happens when a product's contract expires?

Each product has its own expiration date. For contract-based products, you'll lose entitlements when the term expires unless you renew. For pay-as-you-go products, they typically fall back to the public offer pricing. Your partner will typically reach out before expiration to discuss renewal options.

## What notifications do buyers receive for offer sets?

When an offer set is published for a buyer's AWS account, they receive a single consolidated email containing the offer set ID, expiration date, and a complete list of included products with their Product IDs, Offer IDs, and seller names. The email includes a call-to-action link to review and subscribe to the offer set, along with links to view all available offers and subscription instructions. Individual offer published emails are suppressed for buyers when offers are part of an offer set—they receive only the consolidated offer set notification rather than separate emails for each component offer.
