

# Free trials with Express Checkout

If you start a free trial with AWS Marketplace Express Checkout:
+ You enter a credit or debit card when you start the trial. You aren't charged during the trial. Your payment method and billing address are needed to start your free trial. Review the pricing terms to see what happens when your trial ends.
+ **Subscriptions (SaaS contracts with a set term, for example, monthly):** unlike usage-based products, these trials don't convert to a paid subscription automatically. You'll get an email 3 days before the trial ends with a link to subscribe. Your card is already on file. If you don't subscribe, the trial ends and you lose access.
+ **Usage-based products:** the trial converts to a paid subscription automatically when it ends, and usage is billed at the rates in your agreement. You'll get reminder emails 15, 3 and 1 days before it converts. To avoid charges, cancel in [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the AWS Marketplace Express Checkout catalog before the trial ends.

View your trials and their end dates in My Account.
