

# Updates to the seller's terms for Express Checkout purchases

When you buy a product with AWS Marketplace Express Checkout, you accept the seller's end user license agreement (EULA) for that product. Sellers can update their EULA.

## What happens when a seller updates the EULA
<a name="express-checkout-eula-updates-what-happens"></a>
+ You'll get an email with a link to the new terms and the date they take effect.
+ The new terms apply starting on that date. 
+ You don't need to do anything to keep your subscription.

## If you don't agree with the new terms
<a name="express-checkout-eula-updates-if-you-disagree"></a>

You can cancel your subscription in [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the AWS Marketplace Express Checkout catalog before the new terms take effect. Canceling stops the next renewal, and you keep access through the period you've already paid for. See [Refunds and cancellations for Express Checkout purchases](express-checkout-refunds-cancellations.md).
