# Reviewing and opting in when you accept an offer

You can see which offers include auto-renewal before you accept, and choose your preference
during acceptance.

On the **Private offers** page, the **Available offers**
tab includes an **Auto-renewal** column that shows whether each offer includes
renewal terms: **Available** or **Not available**. This shows
availability only, not your opt-in status.

Open the offer to review it. When an offer includes auto-renewal, the procurement page
shows an **Auto-renewal** section with terms.

The renewal terms shown depend on the renewal pricing type. For a price uplift with a
range, they include:

- **Renewal pricing** – for example, **Price
  uplift – range**.
- **Price uplift** – the allowed range, for example,
  0% – 20%.
- **Renewal count** – how many times the agreement
  renews before it ends, for example, 2.
- **Renewal decision deadline** – the last day you
  can change your auto-renewal preference, for example, 10 days before agreement
  expiration.
- **Default uplift** – the percentage applied if the
  seller does not finalize a value by the adjustment deadline, for example, 5%.
- **Adjustment deadline** – the date by which the
  seller finalizes the exact uplift, for example, 15 days before agreement expiration.
  For a fixed price uplift, you see the fixed percentage instead of a range, with no default
  uplift or adjustment deadline. For no price uplift, the agreement renews at the same price and
  no uplift fields appear.

If the offer has a payment schedule, a renewal payment schedule preview shows the schedule
that applies if you opt in.

Under **Auto-renewal preferences**, choose the renewal option for your
contract:

- **Automatically renew this contract when it expires**
- **Do not automatically renew this contract**
  You can opt in or opt out during initial acceptance, and you can change that preference any
  time before the renewal decision deadline from the **Manage subscriptions**
  page.
