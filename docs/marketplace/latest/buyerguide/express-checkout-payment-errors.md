

# Payment errors with Express Checkout

If a payment for an AWS Marketplace Express Checkout purchase doesn't go through, you'll get an email, and your subscription might not start or might be paused until the payment is made. Use this page to fix common problems.

## Accepted payment methods
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-accepted-methods"></a>

Credit and debit cards. Prepaid cards might not be accepted.

## Common errors and how to fix them
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-common-errors"></a>


| Error | What it means | What to do | 
| --- | --- | --- | 
| Card declined | Your bank declined the charge. | Contact your bank, or use a different card. | 
| Card expired | The card on file has expired. | Go to [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the AWS Marketplace Express Checkout catalog and update your card. | 
| Invalid card details | The card number, expiration date, security code or billing address doesn't match. | Re-enter your card details. | 
| Card not supported | The card type or the country where the card was issued isn't supported. | Use a different card. | 
| Authentication required | Your bank asked you to confirm the payment (for example, with a code sent to your phone). | In My Account, choose Account settings, and then choose Pay Now to complete the payment. | 

## Updating your card
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-updating-card"></a>

1. Go to [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the Express Checkout catalog and sign in.

1. Choose the subscription, then choose **Change** next to your payment method.

1. Enter your new card details and save.

You can also update your card in **Account settings** > **Plan & Billing** by choosing **Change**.

## Paying an outstanding charge
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-paying-charge"></a>

1. In [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the Express Checkout catalog, choose **Account settings**.

1. Choose **Pay Now** next to the outstanding charge.

1. Follow the steps from your bank to confirm the payment.

## If you were charged but don't see your subscription
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-charged-no-subscription"></a>

If your bank asked you to confirm the payment and it wasn't completed in time, your purchase might not have gone through even though a charge appears on your card. Contact [AWS Marketplace support](https://account.global.app.aws/settings/support) with the date and amount of the charge.

## When to contact support
<a name="express-checkout-payment-errors-when-to-contact"></a>

Contact [AWS Marketplace support](https://account.global.app.aws/settings/support) if:
+ You fixed the problem and the payment still fails
+ You see a charge you don't recognize
+ You can't sign in to My Account
