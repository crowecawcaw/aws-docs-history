

# Managing your Express Checkout purchases in My Account

If you bought software with AWS Marketplace Express Checkout, you manage your purchases in [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the AWS Marketplace Express Checkout catalog. You don't need the AWS console.

## Signing in to My Account
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-signing-in"></a>

1. Go to [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the Express Checkout catalog. You'll also find a My Account link in your purchase emails.

1. Sign in the same way you did at checkout: with your email address or the social account you used.

If you can't sign in, see [Getting help with Express Checkout purchases](express-checkout-getting-help.md). 

## Viewing your subscriptions
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-viewing-subscriptions"></a>

My Account lists your subscriptions on two tabs:
+ **Active:** subscriptions you're paying for or trying for free
+ **Inactive:** subscriptions that ended, expired or were canceled

For each subscription, you can see the product, the seller, the price, the start and end dates and the renewal date.

The end date is the end of your current term. If renewal is on, a new term starts on the renewal date.

You can search the list and sort by column.

## Subscription details
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-subscription-details"></a>

Choose a subscription to see its details: the product, the seller, the agreement ID, the offer, the start and end dates, auto-renewal status, the seller's end user license agreement (EULA) and what you bought (for example, number of seats).

From the subscription page you can:
+ **Set up product:** go to the seller's website to finish setup. The button appears after your payment is confirmed and your subscription is ready.
+ **Download agreement:** save a copy of your agreement.
+ **Actions** menu:
  + **Product:** Set up product, Usage instructions, Write review
  + **Subscription:** View terms, Cancel subscription

You can also choose the seller's name to go to the seller's website.

## Updating your payment method
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-updating-payment"></a>

1. In My Account, choose the subscription.

1. Choose **Change** next to your payment method.

1. Enter your new card details and save.

The new card is used for future charges. You can also update your card in **Account settings** > **Plan & Billing** > **Change**. 

## Canceling a subscription
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-canceling"></a>

1. Go to [My Account](https://expresscheckout.marketplace.aws.com/subscriptions) on the Express Checkout catalog and sign in the same way you did when you purchased.

1. Choose **Subscriptions**, and then choose the subscription you want to cancel.

1. Choose **Actions**, and then choose **Cancel subscription**.

1. On the page that opens, turn off renewal for the next term, and then confirm.

Because you pay upfront for each term, you're paid through the current term and keep full access until it ends. Your subscription then ends and won't renew, and you won't be charged again.

For details, see [Refunds and cancellations for Express Checkout purchases](express-checkout-refunds-cancellations.md).

## Account settings
<a name="express-checkout-my-account-account-settings"></a>

In My Account, choose **Account settings** in the left menu. Your settings open in a new browser tab, with three sections:
+ **Plan & Billing:** shows the card on file (card type and last four digits). To update it, choose **Change**. **Billing history** lists past charges with their status, amount and due date. To download an invoice, use the **Action** column. You won't see any records until your first billing cycle completes.
+ **My account:** shows your name and email, which come from your AWS Builder ID. To change your name, email, password or multi-factor authentication, choose **Manage profile & security**. Your **Account ID** is also here. Choose **Copy Account ID** if support asks for it.
+ **Support:** opens AWS Support in a new tab, where you can create a case and check existing cases.
