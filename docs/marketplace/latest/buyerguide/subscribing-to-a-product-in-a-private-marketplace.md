# Private marketplaces for users

To subscribe to a product in your private marketplace as a user, navigate to the product's
details page and choose **Continue**. This redirects you to the product's
subscription page. On the subscription page, you can make your configuration selections, and
then choose **Subscribe**.

If the product is not approved in your private marketplace, **Subscribe**
isn't available. A red banner at the top of the page indicates that the product is not
currently approved for procurement. If software requests are enabled, you can choose
**Create request** to submit a request to your administrator requesting
that the product be added to your private marketplace.
