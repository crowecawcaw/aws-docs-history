# Using a flexible

payment scheduler with future dated agreements

You can use the flexible payment scheduler with future dated agreements. You can set up
payments for purchases at a time of your choosing between your agreement sign date and agreement
end date. This approach includes payments before and after the agreement start date.

The seller of record creating the private offer chooses payment dates and amounts. For more
details, see [Flexible payment scheduler](../userguide/flexible-payment-scheduler.md "../userguide/flexible-payment-scheduler.md").
