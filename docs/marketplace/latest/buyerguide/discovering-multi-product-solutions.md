# Discovering Multi-Product Solutions

## AI-Powered Discovery

AWS Marketplace offers an AI-powered discovery experience that helps you find solutions matching your specific needs:

- **Describe Your Use Case**: Enter your business challenge or requirements in natural language
- **Upload Documentation**: Optionally provide business or technical requirements documents
- **Review Recommendations**: Receive AI-generated summaries explaining why each solution matches your needs
- **Compare Options**: Evaluate solutions across key criteria like customer reviews, features, and security credentials

## Search and Browse

You can also discover Multi-Product Solutions by:

- **Searching by Use Case**: Filter solutions by predefined use cases relevant to your industry
- **Browsing Categories**: Explore solutions organized by technology category or business function
- **Viewing Partner Solutions**: See all solutions from specific AWS Partners you trust

## Solution Detail Pages

Each Multi-Product Solution has a dedicated detail page that provides:

- **Solution Overview**: Comprehensive description of how the solution addresses your business challenge
- **Product Components**: List of all included products with links to individual product pages
- **Integration Details**: Explanation of how the products work together
- **Use Case Examples**: Specific scenarios where the solution delivers value
- **Visual Resources**: Architecture diagrams, demo videos, and implementation guides
- **Pricing Information**: Available through private offers customized to your needs
- **Deployed on AWS**: Indicates whether all products in the solution are deployed on AWS infrastructure, making the solution eligible for AWS committed spend programs.
