

# Products integrated with AWS services

Some AWS Marketplace products are delivered through an AWS service rather than through the seller's website or software that you deploy. You subscribe to the product in AWS Marketplace, and you use it inside the AWS service that it is integrated with. For RTB Fabric module products, you deploy a module in AWS RTB Fabric. Your subscription is checked automatically when you use the product.

RTB Fabric module products use contract pricing. You can subscribe in the following ways:
+ [Subscribing with upfront commitments (contracts)](#buyer-integrated-contracts)
+ [Subscribing to free trials](#buyer-integrated-free-trials)

Some products don't show a price. For these products, choose **Request private offer** on the product page, and the seller sends you an offer with negotiated terms that you accept in AWS Marketplace. For more information, see [Private offers in AWS Marketplace](buyer-private-offers.md).

You subscribe on AWS Marketplace, or in the AWS RTB Fabric console, and then you use the product through AWS RTB Fabric:
+ **RTB Fabric module products** are bid-traffic modules from advertising technology vendors that you deploy in the AWS RTB Fabric console.

## Finding these products
<a name="buyer-integrated-finding"></a>

You find RTB Fabric module products on the AWS Marketplace website, on the AWS Marketplace console, the AWS Marketplace MCP server, or the AWS RTB Fabric console. On the AWS Marketplace search, to narrow your search to these products, use the **Delivery method** filter and select **RTB Fabric Module**. The product detail page shows the AWS service that the product is delivered through under **Delivery method**, and the **Usage information** section describes what to do in that service after you subscribe.

You can also reach these products from the AWS RTB Fabric console, which links to the AWS Marketplace listing.

## What happens after you subscribe
<a name="buyer-integrated-after-subscribe"></a>

You subscribe on AWS Marketplace, and then you deploy the module in the AWS RTB Fabric console. For more information about how to use the product, see [RTB Fabric module documentation](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/rtb-fabric/latest/userguide/discovering-attaching-modules.html).

Your subscription is checked by AWS RTB Fabric when you use the product. Your entitlement is usually active within a few minutes of subscribing. Charges appear on your AWS bill under the product title.

## Subscribing with upfront commitments (contracts)
<a name="buyer-integrated-contracts"></a>

With a contract, you pay upfront for a set quantity of the product for a fixed duration of 1, 12, 24, or 36 months. The seller defines the dimensions that you can purchase, such as access to a module.

**To subscribe with a contract**

1. On the product detail page, choose **View purchase options** to go to the procurement experience.

1. If the product has multiple available offers, choose one from **Available offers**.

1. Select the contract duration and the quantity for each dimension (if applicable).

1. Review the total, and accept the EULA.

1. Choose **Subscribe**.

Your agreement starts immediately, and you are invoiced for the full contract value. If the seller offered a payment schedule in a private offer, you are invoiced on that schedule instead. To review your current subscriptions, see the subscriptions page in the [AWS Marketplace console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/marketplace/home?region=us-east-1#/subscriptions).

Contracts renew automatically at the end of the term unless you turn off auto-renewal. During the term, you can upgrade a contract to a higher quantity or a higher-value dimension and receive a prorated credit. You can't reduce a contract until renewal.

You can't cancel a contract agreement for these products yourself. To cancel, contact the seller. The seller cancels the agreement, including public-offer agreements, and issues any refund from AWS Partner Central, and you approve the request in the AWS Marketplace console. You can also contact AWS Support.

**Note**  
To request a private offer, choose **Request a private offer** on the product details page. You can also view available private offers on the purchase page or through the private link that the seller provides.

## Subscribing to free trials
<a name="buyer-integrated-free-trials"></a>

Some vendors offer free trials so that you can evaluate a product. You can search products and filter to show only those that offer a free trial. All free-trial products display the **Free trial** badge next to the product logo. On the product procurement page, you find the duration of the free trial and what happens when it ends.

A contract free trial expires when it ends. To continue, subscribe to a public offer or negotiate a private offer. If you no longer want the product, let the free trial expire.

To view your subscriptions, choose **Manage subscriptions** in the AWS Marketplace console.

**Note**  
Each AWS account can use one free trial per product.

**To subscribe to a contract free trial offer**

1. Sign in to the AWS Marketplace console, and choose **Discover products**.

1. In **Refine results**, go to **Free trial** and select **Free trial**.

1. For **Delivery methods**, select **RTB Fabric Module**.

1. For **Pricing model**, select **Upfront Commitment** to view products that offer free trials.

1. Select the product.

1. Choose **Try for free** on the product detail page.

1. For **Offer type**, select a free trial option.

1. For **Purchase**, choose **Try for free**.

1. To start using the product, use the AWS RTB Fabric console.

## Managing and canceling your subscription
<a name="buyer-integrated-managing"></a>

You view and manage subscriptions to these products the same way as your other AWS Marketplace subscriptions. For more information, see [Managing subscriptions in AWS Marketplace](buyer-managing-subscriptions.md).

To stop a subscription, cancel it from your AWS Marketplace subscriptions. For more information, see [Canceling product subscriptions](cancel-subscription.md).
