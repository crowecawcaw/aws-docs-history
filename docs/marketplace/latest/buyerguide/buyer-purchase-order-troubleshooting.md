# Troubleshooting purchase orders

The information in the following table can help you troubleshoot issues with purchase orders (POs) and understand what happens in different scenarios.

| Scenario                                                                 | Details                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Insufficient permissions—during purchase                                 | The note appears near the *_Purchase order entry_<br>• field if you<br>don't have the `aws-marketplace:Subscribe` permission to subscribe. The<br>management account must also enable the AWS Billing integration. For information<br>about enabling the integration, see [Creating a service-linked role for AWS Marketplace](using-service-linked-roles-purchase-orders.md#create-service-linked-role-purchase-orders "using-service-linked-roles-purchase-orders.md#create-service-linked-role-purchase-orders").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Insufficient permissions—after purchase                                  | To add or update a PO after subscribing, the following permissions<br>are required: `aws-marketplace:ListAgreementCharges` and<br>`aws-marketplace:UpdatePurchaseOrders`.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| PO added in AWS Marketplace not appearing in Billing and Cost Management | Purchase orders are added to Billing and Cost Management by AWS Marketplace. If you added a PO<br>but it does not appear, this behavior is expected. AWS Marketplace adds the<br>PO to Billing and Cost Management upon the first invoice date for scheduled payments. For<br>example, if you accepted a private offer with a scheduled invoice date<br>in the future and do not yet see the PO, the PO appears after the<br>first invoice is issued.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| PO not appearing on invoice                                              | To troubleshoot a PO that is not appearing on your invoice,<br>complete the following steps:<br>1. Ensure that the PO was added in AWS Marketplace for the account<br>that subscribed to the software. You can update the PO<br>for future invoices within AWS Marketplace on the subscribing account.<br>For more information, see [Updating a purchase order after a transaction](buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase "buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase").<br>2. Verify that the PO is not suspended on the payer<br>account in Billing and Cost Management.<br>3. If the PO is still not associating, check whether a tax<br>registration number (TRN) is added to the PO in Billing and Cost Management. A TRN<br>prevents the PO from associating with a different bill-from entity<br>(seller of record). For example, if you created a TRN for Amazon<br>Web Services EMEA SARL and associated it with a PO, that PO does not<br>associate with invoices from Amazon Web Services, Inc. |
| Missing PO notifications                                                 | POs without contact information (including AWS Marketplace POs) do not receive email notifications. You can add contact information to a PO in the [Purchase orders dashboard<br>in the Billing and Cost Management](https://aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/aws-purchase-order-management "https://aws.amazon.com/aws-cost-management/aws-purchase-order-management") console.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| Incorrect PO number added                                                | If you enter an incorrect PO number and need to update it, you can<br>add the PO number from the AWS Marketplace console for any future charges. If you've already<br>received the invoice, contact Support to update the PO number. For more<br>information, see [Updating a purchase order after a transaction](buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase "buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Subscribing account moves to a different organization                    | If a linked account moves to a new AWS Organization, the purchase order will not automatically<br>carry over to the new AWS Organization. The PO will still appear in AWS Marketplace but will not appear<br>on the invoice. You must re-apply purchase orders within AWS Marketplace (for future charges) for all<br>subscriptions where they are needed, following the transition of a linked account to a new AWS Organization.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| PO option unavailable when checking out                                  | After purchasing a product, go to your subscription page in AWS Marketplace to update the<br>PO. If you're unable to update the PO, contact Support. For<br>more information, see [Updating a purchase order after a transaction](buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase "buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| Contracts with pay-as-you-go                                             | You can choose to use a PO that covers all charges, only<br>pay-as-you-go (usage) charges, or individual charges. For more information, see<br>[Using purchase orders for AWS Marketplace transactions](buyer-purchase-orders.md#buyer-using-purchase-orders "buyer-purchase-orders.md#buyer-using-purchase-orders").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| One PO for all AWS Marketplace charges                                   | If you want to use one PO number for all charges, you can [add a PO with blanket usage](buyer-purchase-orders.md#buyer-using-blanket-usage "buyer-purchase-orders.md#buyer-using-blanket-usage")<br>in the AWS Billing console.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Suspended PO                                                             | When a PO number is provided, and the PO is marked as<br>suspended in the *_Purchase Orders_<br>• dashboard in the Billing and Cost Management console, the new line item is<br>added to the PO, but the invoice doesn't include the PO.<br>The billing admin for the AWS account must make the PO active, and<br>contact Support to regenerate the invoice with the active PO.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Expired PO                                                               | When you provide a PO number that's expired,<br>a new line item is created, and the PO is marked as active. The line<br>item's end date is used as the new PO expiration date.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| Balance tracking                                                         | Balance tracking is not enabled for AWS Marketplace line items.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| Procurement system integration                                           | From your management account, you must set up the AWS Billing integration for<br>AWS Marketplace to display purchases orders provided by an integrated procurement system on<br>invoices.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Flexible payment schedule — multiple POs                                 | If you need your individual payments for a flexible payment schedule to show up<br>with different POs, you can add multiple POs at the time of purchase, or<br>update the PO from the AWS Marketplace subscription page before invoice creation.<br>For more information, see [Updating a purchase order after a transaction](buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase "buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| PO option missing from procurement page                                  | POs are supported for most AWS Marketplace products, but you can't add<br>a PO for the following:<br>1. Free products<br>2. BYOL products<br>If you're purchasing products outside of AWS Marketplace, such as in the<br>Amazon EC2 console, you cannot add a PO while subscribing. You can, however, update<br>POs post-purchase in the AWS Marketplace console for machine learning products and<br>products purchased through other AWS consoles. For more information, see [Updating a purchase order after a transaction](buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase "buyer-purchase-orders.md#update-po-post-purchase").                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| POs with license manager                                                 | Your PO applies only to subscription-related charges. Grant<br>charges will not inherit the PO applied to the original subscription.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
