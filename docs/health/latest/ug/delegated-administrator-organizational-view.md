# Managing delegated administrator

views for an organization

With AWS Health, you can leverage the delegated administrator feature from AWS Organizations that
allows an account other than the management account to view aggregated AWS Health events on the
[AWS Health
Dashboard](https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/technology/aws-health-dashboard "https://aws.amazon.com/premiumsupport/technology/aws-health-dashboard") or programmatically through the [AWS Health API](health-api.md "health-api.md"). The delegated administrator
feature provides the flexibility for different teams to view and manage health events across your
organization. It's an AWS security best practice to delegate responsibilities outside of the
management account where possible.
