# Joining a meeting with muted audio

You can join meetings with your audio already muted. Follow these steps.

###### To join with muted audio

1. In the desktop client or the web app, choose your name, and on the menu that appears, choose **Settings**.
2. On the **Settings** page, in the left bar, choose **Audio and video**.
3. Under **Audio settings**, select the **Join muted** checkbox.
4. Close the **Settings** page.
   Amazon Chime now mutes your audio each time you join a meeting. You can unmute your audio at any time by selecting the **Unmute mic**
   icon at the bottom of the meeting window.

![A microphone with a diagonal slash.](images/icon-unmute-mic.png)
To join meetings with your audio unmuted, repeat the steps listed above and clear the **Join muted** checkbox.
