# Joining a meeting

The topics in this section explain how to use the desktop client and web app to join an
Amazon Chime meeting. The method you use depends on your device, location, and network
availability. For information about joining meetings in the mobile app, see [Signing in to the mobile app](mobile-sign-in.md "mobile-sign-in.md"), later in this guide.

You can sign in to the Amazon Chime desktop client or web app and join meetings as an _authenticated user_, or without signing in as
an _anonymous user_. Authenticated users sign in with their Amazon Chime account credentials. Anonymous users don't
have Amazon Chime accounts, or they use another method of joining, such as dialing into the meeting using the 10-digit meeting ID provided in the meeting
invite.
