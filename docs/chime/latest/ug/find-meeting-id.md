# Finding meeting IDs

To join Amazon Chime meetings without a registered user account, or to join meetings early, you need to enter a
meeting ID. You find meeting IDs in meeting invitations.

###### To find a meeting ID

1. Open your calendar app.
2. Choose the meeting's entry on your calendar.
3. From the meeting invitation, copy the meeting ID. You can also use this ID
   to call in to an Amazon Chime meeting.
