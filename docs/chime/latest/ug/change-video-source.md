# Changing video sources during a meeting

In the Amazon Chime desktop client and web app, you can change video sources during a meeting. To
switch, you must have more than one video source connected to your device. The source that you
choose becomes your new default setting.

###### To change video sources during a meeting

1. If your video is turned on, choose the **Video** icon in the call control
   bar to turn it off.
2. Open the **Video** menu (**^**) and choose one of the
   available video sources.
