# Getting ready for a meeting

Before a meeting, make sure your device works with Amazon Chime. You can choose your
preferred video and audio sources before entering a meeting. If using an external device
for audio or video, such as a headset or web cam, ensure the devices are compatible with
Amazon Chime. You can also set up phone numbers for dialing in to a meeting from a phone,
as well as have Amazon Chime call you when a meeting starts.
