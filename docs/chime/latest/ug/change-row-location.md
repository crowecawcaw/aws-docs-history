# Changing the video row location

By default, attendee video tiles appear in a row at the top of the meetings window. You can move that row to the bottom of the window before and
during a meeting. Typically, you move the row to make featured content easier to see.

###### To change the location before a meeting

1. Open **Settings**. For more information, see [Amazon Chime global program settings](chm-settings.md "chm-settings.md").
2. Choose **Meetings**.
3. Under **Video row location**, choose **Above featured content** or **Below featured content**.

###### To change the location during a meeting

1. In the left control bar, choose the **Media layout** icon (

![An icon showing a stylized app screen with three panels.](images/left-control-5.png)

). 2. Point to **Video row location**, and on the menu that appears, choose **Above featured content** or
**Below featured content**.
