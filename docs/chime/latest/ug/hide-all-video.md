# Hiding attendee video tiles during a meeting

If you need more room for a screen share, or if you find attendee video tiles distracting, you can hide them during a meeting.

###### Note

Your video tile remains visible after you hide the other videos. For information about hiding your video tile, see
[Hiding your video tile during a meeting](hide-your-video.md "hide-your-video.md"), later in this guide.

###### To hide all video

1. On the left toolbar, open the **Media layout** menu (

![An icon showing a stylized app window with three panels.](/images/chime/latest/ug/images/left-control-5.png)

). 2. Choose **Hide all available video**.
To show the video tiles, repeat these steps.
