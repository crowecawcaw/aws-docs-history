# Recording a meeting

Only meeting organizers or hosts can record a meeting. When you finish recording, Amazon Chime
processes the video and sends it to you as a regular chat message in your
**Messages** view.

###### To record a meeting

1. Choose the vertical ellipsis, then choose **Meeting settings**.
2. Choose **Record this meeting**, then choose **Record
   Meeting**.

###### To stop recording a meeting

1. Choose the vertical ellipsis, then choose **Meeting settings**.
2. Choose **Stop recording**.
