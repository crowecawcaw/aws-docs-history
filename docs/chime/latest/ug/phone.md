# Dialing phone numbers with Amazon Chime

If your Amazon Chime administrator enables the calling options for your account, you can use the
Amazon Chime desktop client and web app to dial phone numbers. You can also send text messages,
phone numbers, add phone numbers to your contacts, and access voicemail. For more
information, contact your Amazon Chime administrator or your manager.

You can also use **Call history** to call or send messages to phone
numbers or people on your contacts list from the past 30 days.

###### Note

Amazon Chime is not always a replacement for your telephone. You can only make emergency calls in the U.S.
