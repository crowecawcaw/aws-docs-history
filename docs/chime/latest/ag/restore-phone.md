# Restoring deleted phone numbers

You can restore deleted phone numbers from the **Deletion queue**
for up to 7 days after you delete them. Restoring a phone number moves it back into your
**Inventory**.

###### To restore deleted phone numbers

1. Open the Amazon Chime console at [https://chime.aws.amazon.com/](https://chime.aws.amazon.com "https://chime.aws.amazon.com").
2. In the navigation pane, under **Calling**, choose **Phone number
   management**.
3. Choose the **Deletion queue** tab, then select the phone number or numbers
   that you want to restore.
4. Choose **Move to inventory**.
