# Managing chat retention policies

If you administer one or more Amazon Chime Enterprise accounts, you can set chat retention
policies for the following:

- Chat conversations that include only members of your Enterprise
  account.
- Chat rooms created by members of your Enterprise account.
  A retention policy automatically deletes messages based on the time period that you
  set. You can set time periods lasting from one day to 15 years.

###### Note

Amazon Chime Enterprise accounts have a retention period of 90 days. The policy applies
to conversations involving users who belong to the account, and to users who don't
belong to the account.

Retention policies do not apply to the following:

- Chat conversations that do not include members of Amazon Chime Enterprise
  accounts
- Chat rooms created by users who don't belong to an Amazon Chime Enterprise
  account
