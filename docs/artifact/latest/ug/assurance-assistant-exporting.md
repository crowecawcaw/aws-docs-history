# Exporting a completed questionnaire

After you have reviewed and finalized your responses, you can export the completed
questionnaire.

###### To export a completed questionnaire

1. Open the [AWS Artifact
   console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/artifact/home "https://console.aws.amazon.com/artifact/home").
2. In the navigation pane, choose **Assurance
   Assistant**.
3. On the **Compliance inquiries list** page, select the
   questionnaire you want to export.
4. On the details page, select your preferred option:
   - **Export Selected** – Exports the
     questionnaire with selected questions and responses in a spreadsheet
     format.
   - **Export All** – Exports the entire list of
     questions and responses.

5. Select if you want to include source citations in the export.
6. Choose **Export**.
