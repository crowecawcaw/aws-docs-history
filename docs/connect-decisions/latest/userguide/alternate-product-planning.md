

# Alternate Product Planning
<a name="alternate-product-planning"></a>

The Alternate Product Planning feature enables customers to define alternate product relationships in supply planning. When an alternate product relationship is defined, supply planning considers available inventory and supply of the alternate product at the same site when fulfilling demand for the main product. The system consumes the available alternate product supply first. Once that supply is exhausted, the system will then create new planned orders for the main product.

## Key Benefits
<a name="alternate-product-planning-key-benefits"></a>
+ **Support product supersession:** Manage transitions from an older product version to a newer version while maintaining continuity in supply planning.
+ **Consume existing supply first:** Use available inventory and supply of the alternate product before creating new supply orders.
+ **Reduce obsolete inventory:** Help minimize excess or stranded inventory during product transitions.
+ **Control replenishment during transitions:** Prevent new planned orders for the original product after the designated alternate supply has been consumed.

## Example
<a name="alternate-product-planning-example"></a>

A company is transitioning from Product A (Version 1) to Product B (Version 2). The company wants to consume the remaining supply of Version 1 before ordering additional supply of Version 2.

By defining Version 1 as an alternate product for Version 2, supply planning can use the available Version 1 inventory and supply at the same site to satisfy demand for Version 2. After the available Version 1 supply is exhausted, the system will generate new planned orders for Version 2.

This capability provides a controlled way to manage product supersession and version transitions, helping customers consume existing supply before moving fully to the replacement product.
