

# Publishing Demand Plans
<a name="publishing-demand-plans"></a>

After planners have reviewed and refined forecasts, you can publish the final plan to make it available for downstream processes.

**To publish a demand plan:**

1. Review the aggregate performance metrics to ensure forecast quality meets your standards.

1. Coordinate with your planning team to confirm all necessary adjustments have been completed.

1. Click **Publish Plan** to process your request.

1. The system automatically saves the published plan to your configured Amazon S3 folder.
