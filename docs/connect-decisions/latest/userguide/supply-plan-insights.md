

# Supply Plan Insights
<a name="supply-plan-insights"></a>

Plan Insights is a new AI-powered experience in Planning Hub that proactively identifies opportunities to improve supply plans and helps planners understand the expected impact before making changes. A new Insights tab is added to provide recommendations generated by the AI teammate, along with quantified impact, explanations, and an interactive feedback experience. The first insight available with this release is Inventory Policy Recommendation, which helps planners identify opportunities to improve inventory performance while meeting their desired service levels.

## Inventory Policy Recommendation
<a name="supply-plan-insights-inventory-policy-recommendation"></a>

**AI-Recommended Inventory Policies:** When a supply plan is generated, the AI teammate automatically analyzes planning inputs such as demand volume, demand variability, lead time, replenishment cadence, and desired service level to determine a recommended inventory policy. The recommendation is designed to help planners maintain sufficient inventory to support customer demand while avoiding unnecessary inventory. The AI teammate considers both demand and supply characteristics when determining the appropriate inventory policy rather than relying on a single static inventory parameter. The desired service level used to generate the recommendation can be adjusted based on customer feedback.

Before presenting a recommendation, Plan Insights evaluates its expected impact. The system generates a scenario plan behind the scene using the recommended inventory policy and performs a forward-looking simulation of both the existing baseline plan and the recommended-policy scenario. It compares the performance metrics to quantify the expected impact of adopting the recommendation surfaces opportunities for improvement. Planners can interact with the AI teammate directly from the insight to understand why a policy change is being recommended, how the recommendation was determined, and which demand, supply, and service-level considerations contributed to it. Planners can also provide feedback on recommendations. The AI teammate uses this feedback to refine the service-level assumptions used for future recommendations. Users can request that Plan Insights immediately regenerate the recommendations based on their feedback, rather than waiting for the next plan run.

## Apply Recommended Inventory Policies
<a name="supply-plan-insights-apply-recommended-inventory-policies"></a>

Once a planner is satisfied with a recommendation, the recommended inventory policy can be downloaded as a CSV file. The recommendation can then be used to prepare an updated Inventory Policy file and uploaded through Data Management ingestion flow for use in subsequent plan runs.

With Plan Insights and Inventory Policy Recommendation, planners can move from manually evaluating inventory settings to an AI-guided, simulation-based approach that identifies improvement opportunities, quantifies their expected impact, and continuously incorporates planner feedback.
