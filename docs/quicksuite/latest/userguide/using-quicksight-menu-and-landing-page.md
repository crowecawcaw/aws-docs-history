# Using the Amazon Quick Suite menu and

landing page

After you sign in to Amazon Quick Suite, you see the Amazon Quick Suite landing page. This page
provides tabs for your analyses, dashboards, folders, data, and more. It also provides a
menu bar at the top, with options for the following:

- Searching Amazon Quick Suite
- Accessing your user profile (managing Quick Suite, language selection,
  and help)
- Interacting with the Quick Suite chat agent
- Choosing the AWS Region that you want to work in
- Discovering helpful resources (documentation, how-to videos, community forum,
  blogs, and more)

###### Note

Consult your administrator before changing your AWS Region. Your default
AWS Region is configured by your Amazon Quick Suite administrator. Changing the AWS
Region changes where your work is stored.

## Navigating the homepage

Using the navigation menu on the left, you can explore how to work with
dashboards, build powerful analyses, manage your data sources, organize content with
folders, leverage advanced analytics capabilities, and more.

The following are all the available Amazon Quick Suite capabilities:

- **Agents** – Build AI agents that intelligently
  analyze, plan, and work with your data to help complete tasks.
- **Spaces** – Work with Amazon Q using specific
  files, data, and information to complete projects or organize teams.
- **Scenarios** – Elevate your data exploration in
  the AI-driven workbook canvas that is enhanced with generative analysis
  capablities. Allow AI to suggest relevant data to enrich your insight
  discovery.
- **Stories** – Use Amazon Q prompts and visuals to
  produce a story that incorporates the details that you provide.
- **Topics** – Create a new generative topic to
  introduce the generative Q&A experience for users.
- **Dashboards** – Share interactive data
  visualizations with your audience. You can explore and filter visualizations
  according to your specific needsm and generate snapshots for easy
  sharing.
- **Analyses** – Dive deep into your data with
  robust visualization and analysis tools. Create charts and graphs to uncover
  insights before publishing to a wider audience.
- **Favorites** – Favorite the dashboards and
  analyses you want to easily find later.
- **Folders** – Organize your analyses and
  dashboards in folders.
- **Automations** – Create automated processes that
  reduce manual intervention and save valuable time.
- **Flows** – Create and share intelligent workflows
  to accomplish goals quickly and predictably.
- **Data** – Connect to data and visualize, create
  specialized chat assistants, or mix both.
- **Actions** – Perform tasks such as creating and
  updating tickets, managing incidents, and accessing project information
  directly.

## Customizing the homepage layout

The Quick Suite homepage provides a customizable workspace where you can add
widgets that enhance your analytics experience. The homepage features three key
widget types:

- **Quick Suite use cases** – Offers quick
  access to tutorial videos and onboarding resources.
- **Recents** – Displays recently viewed resources
  for faster navigation.
- **Business Metrics** – Allows you to pin important
  visualizations and metrics directly from your dashboards.

You can easily add, remove, and rearrange these widgets to create a personalized
homepage that suits your workflow.

###### To add a widget

1. From the Quick Suite homepage, choose **Add
   widget**.
2. You can choose to add either **Quick Suite use
   cases**, **Recents**, or **Business
   metrics**.
   1. If **Quick Suite use cases** is already
      added to the homepage, this option will be unavailable. If you
      previously deleted the widget, choose **Add
      widget** then **Quick Suite use
      cases** to add it back to the homepage.
      **Quick Suite use cases** always appears
      at the top of the homepage to ensure easy access to learning
      resources, even if previously dismissed.
   2. If you choose **Recents**, a widget containing
      recently viewed resources is added to the homepage. Choose a
      resource to quickly pick up where you left off.
   3. If you choose **Business metrics**, you will be
      redirected to the **Dashboards** page. From there,
      you can open a dashboard and choose which visual you would like to
      pin to the homepage by selecting the home + icon.

###### To delete a widget

1. Navigate to the widget you would like to delete.
2. Choose the three-dot menu on the top right of the widget.
3. Choose **Delete**.
4. The selected widget should be removed from the homepage.

###### To reorder a widget

1. Select the widget you would like to reorder.
2. When the widget is selected, the handle icon appears on the top left of
   the widget.
3. Choose the handle icon and drag the widget to swap its place with another
   widget.

###### To resize a widget

1. Select the widget you would like to resize.
2. When the widget is selected, a resize icon appears on the bottom
   right.
3. Choose the resize icon and drag the widget up or down to reach your
   desired size.

## Accessing user profiles

To access the user profile menu, choose the user icon at the upper right of any
page in Amazon Quick Suite. Use this menu to manage Amazon Quick Suite features, choose a
language, change your AWS Region, get help from the documentation, or sign out of
Amazon Quick Suite.

The following options are available from the user profile menu:

- **Manage Quick Suite** – If you have
  appropriate permissions, you can access administrative functions such as
  managing users, subscriptions, [SPICE](spice.md "spice.md") capacity, and account settings.
- **Language setting** – Choose the language you
  want to use in the Amazon Quick Suite user interface.
- **Region setting** – Choose the AWS Region that
  you want to work in.

###### Note

Consult your administrator before changing your AWS Region. Your
default AWS Region is configured by your Amazon Quick Suite administrator.
Changing the AWS Region changes where your work is stored.

- **Help** – This will open the official
  Quick Suite documentation, which you can view online, in Kindle, or as
  a PDF.
- **Sign out** – Choose this option to sign out of
  Amazon Quick Suite and your AWS session.
