# Disconnect an

Amazon Q Business application from an Amazon Quick Suite account

Quick Suite account admins can use the following procedure to disconnect an
Amazon Q Business application from a Quick Suite account.

1.  Open the [Quick Suite console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2.  Choose the user icon at the top right, and then choose **Manage
    Quick Suite**.
3.  Choose **Security & permissions**.
4.  On the **Quick Suite access to AWS services**
    page, choose **SELECT APPLICATION**.
5.  Perform one of the following options:

        1. To disconnect a single Amazon Q Business application from a
         Quick Suite account, navigate to the application that you want
         to remove, open the dropdown, and choose
         **NONE**.
        2. To disconnect all Amazon Q Business applications from a Quick Suite
         account, uncheck the **Amazon Q Business application**
         checkbox.

    When you disconnect an Amazon Q Business application from a Quick Suite account,
    the Amazon Q Business application that you created for Quick Suite is not deleted.
    The application, index, retriever, and any unstructured data source connections that
    you configured remain in your Amazon Q Business account.
