# Managing identities

Identity management in Amazon Quick Suite involves controlling who can access your
Quick Suite resources and what actions they can perform. This section covers the
essential tasks for managing user identities, including adding and removing users,
organizing them into groups for easier permission management, and transitioning between
different identity management systems. Whether you're using IAM Identity Center,
federated identity providers, or other authentication methods, these topics will help
you establish proper access controls and maintain security for your Quick Suite
environment.

Before managing user access, it's important to understand how Amazon Quick Suite
subscription names map to user roles. For information about subscription types and their
capabilities, see [Understanding Amazon Quick Suite subscriptions and
roles](user-types.md "user-types.md").
