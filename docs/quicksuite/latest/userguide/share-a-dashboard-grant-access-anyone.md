# Granting anyone on the

internet access to an Amazon Quick Sight dashboard

|                                     |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Applies to:** Enterprise Edition  | You can also share your Amazon Quick Sight dashboard with anyone on the internet from the **Share** menu in the Amazon Quick Suite console. When you do this, anyone on the internet will be able to access the dashboard, even if they aren't a registered user on your Quick Suite account, when you share the dashboard link or embed the dashboard.
