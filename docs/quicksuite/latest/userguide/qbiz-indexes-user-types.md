# User types and capabilities

Different user types have different capabilities when working with Amazon Q Business indexes in Amazon Quick Suite:

**Amazon Q Business Administrator**

Manages Amazon Q Business indexes in the Amazon Q Business console.

**Amazon Quick Suite Administrator**

Enables permissions for Amazon Quick Suite to access Amazon Q Business indexes in the same AWS account and region.

**Amazon Quick Suite Author**

Creates knowledge bases from Amazon Q Business indexes, shares them with other users, and adds them to spaces.

**Amazon Quick Suite Author**

Creates agents and automations that leverage Amazon Q Business index knowledge bases.

**Amazon Quick Suite Reader**

Uses agents and automations that leverage Amazon Q Business index knowledge bases, and queries these knowledge bases through the Amazon Quick Suite web application.
