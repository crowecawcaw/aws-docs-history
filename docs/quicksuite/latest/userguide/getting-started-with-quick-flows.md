# Getting started with Quick Flows

This section provides essential information to help you get started with Amazon Quick Flows. It covers user personas to help you identify your role, key concepts and terminology you'll encounter when working with flows, prerequisites to ensure your environment is properly configured, and an overview of the Amazon Quick Flows landing page interface.

Begin by understanding the different user personas and identifying which role best describes your responsibilities within your organization. Familiarize yourself with the core concepts and terminology that form the foundation of Quick Flows. Review the prerequisites to ensure your environment is properly configured, and explore the Amazon Quick Flows landing page to understand how to navigate the interface.

These foundational topics will prepare you to start creating and using your own flows effectively.
