# Sharing Amazon Q Business index knowledge bases

After creating a knowledge base from an Amazon Q Business index, Author users can share it with other users in Amazon Quick Suite:

1. Navigate to **Knowledge bases**.
2. Select the Amazon Q Business index knowledge base you want to share.
3. Choose **Share**.
4. Select the users or groups you want to share the knowledge base with.
5. Choose **Share** to confirm.
   Users who have access to an Amazon Q Business index in Amazon Q Business automatically have access to the corresponding knowledge base in Amazon Quick Suite.
