# Map and geospatial chart formatting options in Quick Suite

In Amazon Quick Suite, you can choose from multiple formatting options for your maps and
geospatial charts. You can view formatting options by opening the
**Properties** pane from the on-visual menu located at the top
right of the currently selected geospatial map.

Quick Suite authors and readers can also toggle the different formatting options
of a geospatial map visual from the on visual menu.
