# Removing datasets from a Amazon Quick Sight

topic

You can remove datasets from a topic. Removing datasets from a topic doesn't
delete them from Quick Sight.

Use the following procedure to remove a dataset from a topic.

###### To remove a dataset from a topic

1. Open the topic that you want to change.
2. On the **Summary** page, choose **Data**. Then, under
   **Datasets**, choose the dataset menu (the three dots)
   at right, and then choose **Remove from topic**.
3. On the **Are you sure you want to delete?** page that
   opens, choose **Delete** to remove the dataset from the
   topic. Choose **Cancel** if you don't want to remove the
   dataset from the topic.
