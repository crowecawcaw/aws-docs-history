# Bring Your Own Amazon Q Business Index (BYOI)

Amazon Quick Suite enables you to use your existing Amazon Q Business indexes as data sources. You can leverage your enterprise data without having to recreate indexes. This capability, known as Bring Your Own Index (BYOI), lets you connect your Amazon Q Business indexes to Amazon Quick Suite and use them alongside other data sources for comprehensive analytics and intelligent responses.
