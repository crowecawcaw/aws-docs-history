# Deleting bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight

Use the following procedure to delete a bookmark.

###### To delete a bookmark

1. In a published dashboard, choose the bookmark icon at upper right to open
   the **Bookmarks** pane.
2. In the **Bookmarks** pane, choose the context menu (the
   three vertical dots) for the bookmark that you want to delete, and then
   choose **Delete**.
3. In the **Delete Bookmark** pane that opens, choose
   **Yes, Delete Bookmark**.
