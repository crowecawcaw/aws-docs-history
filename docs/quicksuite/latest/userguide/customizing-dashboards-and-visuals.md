# Customizing dashboard layouts in

Amazon Quick Sight

You can customize a dashboard's layout to organize your data to fit your business
requirements. You can choose from three dashboard layouts. You can also change the size,
background color, border color, and interactions of a visual to create a fully
customized dashboard.

visuals.
