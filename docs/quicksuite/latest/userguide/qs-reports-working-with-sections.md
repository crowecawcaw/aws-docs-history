# Working with sections

A _section_ is a container for different visuals that grow
vertically to contain contents. Each section is rendered to completion, one after
the other, accommodating configured page breaks and section settings. Headers and
footers are special types of section that have a predefined size, location, and
repetition throughout each page of a report.

Each section in a pixel perfect report can be formatted independently from other
sections in the report. Visuals can be dragged and dropped anywhere you want,
similar to a free-form layout in an interactive sheet. Visuals can also be
overlapped, resized, or brought forward or to the back of the section. Additionally,
you can change the margins within a section to make the grouping of visuals stand
out from the rest of the report.

Every report in Quick Sight needs at least one section. You can add multiple
sections to group different sets of visuals together, or to control the rendering
order for different groupings of visuals.

Each pixel perfect report sheet supports up to 30 sections, including headers and
footers.
