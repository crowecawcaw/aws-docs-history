# IAM policy assignments

IAM policy assignments allow you to control access to AWS resources for users and
groups within Amazon Quick Suite. By assigning IAM policies, you can define and manage
permissions for various AWS services and resources that Quick Suite may need to
access. Assignments can be enabled, disabled, or deleted at any time.

###### To add a new IAM policy assignment:

1. Open the [Quick Suite console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Choose the user icon at the top right, and then choose **Manage
   Quick Suite**.
3. Under the **Permissions** section, choose **IAM policy
   assignments**.
4. Choose **Add new assignment**.
5. Enter a name for this assignment and choose **Next**.
6. Select an IAM policy by either searching for it directly or scrolling through
   the displayed list, and then choose **Next**.
7. Assign users or groups to the IAM policy by either searching by name, email
   address, or group name, or scrolling through the displayed list and selecting the
   appropriate users or groups. You can also choose to assign the policy to all users
   and groups by selecting the checkbox; this will apply the policy to all current and
   future users and groups.
8. Choose **Next**.
9. Review your selections and choose **Save and enable**.
10. You can enable or disable the assignment at any time by selecting the checkbox
    next to the assigment name and choosing **Enable** or
    **Disable**. You can view the status of the policy assignment
    under the **Status** column.

###### To edit an IAM policy assignment:

1. Open the [Quick Suite console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Choose the user icon at the top right, and then choose **Manage
   Quick Suite**.
3. Under the **Permissions** section, choose **IAM policy
   assignments**.
4. Choose the edit (pencil) icon.
5. Review the sections and choose **Edit** to make any
   changes.
6. Choose **Save and enable** to apply your updates.

###### To delete an IAM policy assignment:

1. Open the [Quick Suite console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Choose the user icon at the top right, and then choose **Manage
   Quick Suite**.
3. Under the **Permissions** section, choose **IAM policy
   assignments**.
4. Choose the delete (trash) icon.
5. A confirmation modal will appear to ensure you want to procede with the deletion.
   Choose **Delete** to delete the IAM policy assignment.
