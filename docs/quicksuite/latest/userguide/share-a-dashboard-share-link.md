# Sharing a link a shared

dashboard

After you grant users access to a dashboard, you can copy a link to it and send it
to them. Anyone with access to the dashboard can access the link and see the
dashboard.

###### To send users a link to the dashboard

1. Open the published dashboard and choose **Share** at
   upper right. Then choose **Share dashboard**.
2. In the **Share dashboard** page that opens, choose
   **Copy link** at upper left.

The link to the dashboard is copied to your clipboard. It's similar to the
following,

`https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/sn/accounts/`accountid`/dashboards/`dashboardid`?directory_alias=account_directory_alias`

Users and groups (or all users on your Quick Suite account) who have
access to this dashboard can access it by using the link. If they are
accessing Quick Suite for the first time, they will be asked to sign
in with their email address or Quick Suite user name and password for
the account. After they sign in, they will have access to the
dashboard.
