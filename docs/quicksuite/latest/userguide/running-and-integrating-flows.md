# Running and integrating flows

This section covers how to run Amazon Quick Flows and integrate them with other Amazon Quick Suite capabilities. You'll learn about executing flows in different runtime modes, using pre-built flows, and invoking flows from custom agents and My Assistant.

Running Flows in runtime allows end users to interact with your flows through structured or conversational interfaces. Pre-built flows provide ready-to-use templates for common business scenarios. Integration capabilities enable you to invoke Flows from custom agents and My Assistant, extending their functionality and reach within your organization.

These topics will help you maximize the value of your flows by ensuring they can be effectively executed and integrated with other systems and capabilities.
