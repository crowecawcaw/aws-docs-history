# Amazon Q in AWS Supply Chain

###### Note

**Powered by Amazon Bedrock:** AWS implements [automated abuse detection](../../../bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.md "../../../bedrock/latest/userguide/abuse-detection.md"). Because Amazon Q in AWS Supply Chain is built on Amazon Bedrock, users can take full advantage of the controls implemented in Amazon Bedrock to enforce safety, security, and the responsible use of artificial intelligence (AI).

Amazon Q in AWS Supply Chain is an interactive generative artificial intelligence (GenAI) assistant that helps you operate your supply chain more efficiently by analyzing the data
in your AWS Supply Chain Data Lake, providing important operational and financial insights, and answering immediate supply chain questions. For example, you can ask Amazon Q in AWS Supply Chain, "What is my demand forecast over the
next two weeks for Apples in Austin?" and you will get an accurate answer within seconds.
