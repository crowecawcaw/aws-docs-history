# Access AI Assistant chat for OpenSearch-related questions

If you have questions about Amazon OpenSearch Service, including conceptual or procedural questions
related to OpenSearch Service features or functionality, you can ask AI Assistant for information. The
chatbot supports a conversational chat that preserves context of your discussion. It
also saves a history of your conversations for later reference. To access the chatbot,
click the AI Assistant icon at the top-right corner of any OpenSearch Service page. Type a question in the
text box and click **Go**. AI Assistant can take up to 30 seconds to generate
a response.
