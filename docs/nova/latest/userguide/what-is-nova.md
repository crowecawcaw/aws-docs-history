# What is Amazon Nova?

Amazon Nova foundation models deliver frontier intelligence and industry-leading price-performance. Organizations can build and scale generative AI applications with Amazon Nova that are safe, reliable, and cost-effective. Amazon Nova accelerates AI innovation across modalities and use cases - including text, image, video, speech, API calling, and agentic AI. To start building with Amazon Nova, you must access the models through an API using Amazon Bedrock.

Amazon Nova delivers powerful foundation models across understanding, image, video, and speech capabilities.

###### Understanding models

Amazon Nova understanding models are designed to comprehend diverse inputs, including text, images, video, documents, and code. These models excel in powering interactive chat interfaces, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems, agentic applications, video analysis, and UI workflow automation solutions.

- **Amazon Nova Micro** is a text-only model that delivers the
  lowest latency responses at very low cost.
- **Amazon Nova Lite** is a very low cost multimodal model that is
  lightning fast for processing image, video, and text inputs.
- **Amazon Nova Pro** is a highly capable multimodal model and our best combination of accuracy, speed, and cost for a wide range of tasks.
- **Amazon Nova Premier** is our most capable multimodal model for complex tasks and our best teacher for distilling custom models for cost-effective applications.
  Amazon Nova understanding models are available in US East (N. Virginia) and AWS GovCloud (US-West). Amazon Nova Micro, Lite, and Pro are also available in Asia Pacific (Sydney) and Europe (London). The models are accessible in the following regions via cross-region inference profiles: US West (N. California), US West (Oregon), Europe (Stockholm), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Ireland), Europe (Paris), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), Asia Pacific (Singapore), and Asia Pacific (Sydney).

###### Creative models

Amazon Nova creative models transform text and image inputs into professional-grade images and videos. They are designed to deliver customizable visual content with control features, making creative content generation accessible and efficient for media, entertainment, retail, marketing, and advertising industries.

- **Amazon Nova Canvas** is a high-quality image generation
  model with customization and control.
- **Amazon Nova Reel** is a video generation
  model designed to democratize creative content generation.
  Amazon Nova Canvas and Amazon Nova Reel are available in US East (N. Virginia), Europe (Ireland), and Asia Pacific (Tokyo).

###### Speech model

Amazon Nova Sonic is a foundation model for conversational speech understanding and generation in five languages: English (US, UK), French, Italian, German, and Spanish. The model accepts speech as input and provides speech with text transcriptions as output. Amazon Nova Sonic offers a natural, human-like conversational AI experience with contextual richness. It is the first model to feature bidirectional streaming API capabilities, allowing for real-time, low-latency multi-turn conversations.

- **Amazon Nova Sonic** delivers real-time, human-like voice conversations.
  Amazon Nova Sonic is currently available in US East (N. Virginia), Europe (Stockholm), and Asia Pacific (Tokyo) and for English (US, UK), French, Italian, German, and Spanish.

For full model and region support information in Amazon Bedrock, see [Supported foundation models in Amazon Bedrock](../../../bedrock/latest/userguide/models-supported.md "../../../bedrock/latest/userguide/models-supported.md")
