# Camera controls

The follow terminology will be useful in achieving specific camera shots, framing, and
movement in your videos.

For camera speed effects, you can use the following terminology:

- slow motion, slow-motion, slo-mo, slow-mo, slomo, slowmo
- time-lapse, timelapse, time-lapse
- hyperlapse
- walklapse
- spacelapse
