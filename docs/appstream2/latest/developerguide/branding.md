# Add Your Custom Branding to Amazon AppStream 2.0

To create a familiar experience for your users when they stream applications, you can
customize the appearance of AppStream 2.0 with your own branding images, text, and website links, and you can choose from one of several color palettes.
When you customize AppStream 2.0, your branding is displayed to users during application streaming
sessions rather than the default AppStream 2.0 branding.
