# Inviting members to a collaboration

As a collaboration creator, after you have created a collaboration, you can send an
invitation link to the members listed on the **Members** tab.

###### To invite members to a collaboration

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console and open the [AWS Clean Rooms console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cleanrooms/home "https://console.aws.amazon.com/cleanrooms/home").
2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Collaborations**.
3. Choose the collaboration that you created.
4. Choose the **Members** tab.
5. In the **Members** table, choose the **Copy invitation
   link** button.

The invitation link is copied. 6. Paste the invitation link in the secure communication method of your choice and
send it to each member of the collaboration.
