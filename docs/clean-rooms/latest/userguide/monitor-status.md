# Monitoring members

As a collaboration creator, after you have created a collaboration, you can monitor the
status of all members on the **Members** tab. This helps ensure proper
access control and cost management across your collaboration.

###### To monitor the status of a member

1. Sign in to the AWS Management Console and open the [AWS Clean Rooms console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/cleanrooms/home "https://console.aws.amazon.com/cleanrooms/home").
2. In the left navigation pane, choose **Collaborations**.
3. Choose the collaboration that you created.
4. Choose the **Members** tab.
5. In the **Members** table, review the **Status** of
   each member.
6. In the **Member abilities** table, review which members can query,
   receive results, contribute data, and perform other tasks.
7. In the **Payment configuration** table, review which members are
   paying for queries, ID mapping tables, and ML modeling.
