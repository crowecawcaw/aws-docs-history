# Example bot descriptions for descriptive bot builder

Here are some helpful example bot descriptions you can use with descriptive bot builder in Amazon Lex V2.

| Industry           | Example prompt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| ------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Financial services | We are a financial card service that helps users perform with<br>tasks when they receive a new card such as activate card, email<br>or mail a pin, verify a new card (using a zip code). We also<br>help them with tasks associated with their existing card, such<br>as enquire about credit card benefits, report a lost card,<br>request a new card, reset a card pin, or pay a card<br>bill.                                                                                                                                                               |
| Food services      | I want a bot to help customers order food (using item ID,<br>quantity, size), check order status, and cancel an order. Use<br>Order ID for indexing orders.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| Airline            | We are an airline domain that helps users book flight<br>tickets, check details of a reservation, obtain receipt for a<br>booked flight, enquire flight status, reschedule booked flights,<br>elicit flight details, and cancel booked flights. You can also<br>generate additional intents if they help support functions in<br>the domain description.                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| Insurance          | Objective: We are an insurance company that sells car, home<br>and annuity insurance policies. I want a bot that can check<br>claim status, file a claim, make policy payments and cancel a<br>policy. We use policy_id and last 4 of SSN for account<br>identification and validation.I expect the bot to have at least<br>the following intents and slots: authentication<br>• policy_id,<br>last4SSNpolicy type: car, home, annuitypolicy status: check<br>balance, check due date, check coveragemake a payment: one-time<br>payment, installments, amount |
| Vehicle management | We are building a Towed Cars Lookup bot that helps drivers in<br>a city with whose car has been towed to find where the car is<br>located. This bot should ask the address or location of where<br>the automobile was towed from, and details about the vehicle<br>such as license plate and make, model, and year of car. The bot<br>should reply with location of the towed car parking lot, and<br>hours of operation.                                                                                                                                      |
| Travel             | I am a travel agent and I want a bot to help my customers<br>book a trip to Disney. Disney has several parks all over the<br>world to choose from, and also has hotels, dining, and special<br>entertainment that can be reserved. Users of the bot should be<br>able to modify or cancel their booking. Bookings must include at<br>a minimum the park, dates, and hotel. Including dining or<br>entertainment is optional and can be added or changed<br>later.                                                                                              |
