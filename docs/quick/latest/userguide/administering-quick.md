# Administering Amazon Quick

The administration experience for Amazon Quick depends on how your account was created. Choose the guide that matches your account type:

- **Free and Plus accounts** – For accounts created with email or social on [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick "https://aws.com/quick"). See [Administering Amazon Quick (Free/Plus)](standalone-admin-guide.md "standalone-admin-guide.md").
- **Console accounts** – For accounts provisioned through the AWS Management Console, including IAM Identity Center configuration, SPICE capacity management, and integration with other AWS services. See [Administration for Amazon Quick](qsysadmin.md "qsysadmin.md").
