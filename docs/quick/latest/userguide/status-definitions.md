# Status definitions

Quick Research projects progress through various status states during their lifecycle. Understanding these status definitions helps you track the progress of your research and know what actions you can take at each stage.

- _Draft_ - Research project is being created and configured but has not yet been submitted for processing
- _In Progress_ - Quick Research is actively gathering and analyzing information from your selected sources
- _Review_ - Initial research is complete and available for your review and feedback
- _Updating_ - Quick Research is processing your feedback and generating an updated version of the report
- _Complete_ - Research is finalized and ready for download, sharing, or archiving
- _Error_ - An issue occurred during processing that requires attention or retry
  You can view the current status of all your research projects from the main Quick Research dashboard.
