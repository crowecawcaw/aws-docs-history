

# Discover

Discover is where you find and install agents, skills, flows, connectors, plugins, and apps in AWS Quick. It brings together capabilities that Quick and trusted partners publish, along with capabilities that your organization shares with you. To open Discover, choose **Customize**, and then choose **Discover**.

## Browsing capabilities
<a name="browsing-capabilities"></a>

You can browse capabilities in Discover in several ways.
+ **Browse by role** – Find capabilities grouped by the work they support, such as Sales, Finance, Human Resources, Marketing, and Project Management. The General group holds capabilities that fit any role.
+ **Browse by capability type** – Find capabilities grouped as agents, skills, flows, connectors, plugins, and apps.
+ **Official** – Browse capabilities that Quick and trusted partners publish.
+ **My organization** – Browse capabilities that your organization shares with you.

Each of the **Agents**, **Skills**, **Flows**, **Connectors**, and **Plugins** tabs has a **Browse** button that opens this same list filtered to that type.

## Searching and filtering
<a name="searching-filtering-discover"></a>

When you choose **Browse all**, you can search for a capability by name and narrow the results with filters.
+ Filter by capability type, source, industry, role, or owner.
+ Choose **Clear all** to remove the filters you applied.

Each capability shows its name, publisher, type, and a short description. A capability that you already installed shows a check mark.

## Installing a capability
<a name="installing-a-capability"></a>

You install a capability from Discover. When the capability needs a connector, skill, or agent to work, you set that up as part of installing it.

**To install a capability**

1. Choose **Customize**, and then choose **Discover**.

1. Find a capability by browsing by role or by capability type, or by choosing **Browse all** and then searching or filtering.

1. Choose the capability to open its details page.

1. Choose **Install**. You can also choose the add icon (\+) on the capability's card.

1. If the capability needs a connector, skill, or agent, set it up from the details page, or ask Quick to help. You sign in to connectors with your own account.

When you install a plugin, Quick installs every capability in the plugin in one step.

For more information about using and uninstalling each type of capability, see [Agents in AWS Quick](working-with-agents.md), [Teach agents with skills](working-with-skills.md), [Flows](working-with-flows.md), [Work with integrations in AWS Quick](working-with-integrations.md), and [Plugins](working-with-plugins.md).
