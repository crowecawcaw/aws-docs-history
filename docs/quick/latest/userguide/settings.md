

# Settings

Settings is where you configure AWS Quick. You open it from the account menu, at the bottom left of the navigation panel. The settings are organized into the following groups: **Account**, **Appearance and language**, **Chat**, **General**, **Privacy controls**, and **Advanced**. The account menu is also where you open **My context**, which groups your memories and your knowledge graph.

## Account
<a name="settings-account"></a>

On the **Account** tab, you manage your account identity and view your plan and usage. This tab shows your profile. If your organization manages your account, it also shows an indicator that your organization manages it, a session-usage meter for agent hours, and an **Account region** selector that controls where your account data is stored and processed.

## Appearance and language
<a name="settings-appearance"></a>

On the **Appearance and language** tab, you control how AWS Quick looks and which language and timezone it uses.
+ **Color mode** – Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System**.
+ **Language** – Choose the interface language.
+ **Timezone** – Set the timezone used for schedules, timestamps, and calendar availability.

## Chat
<a name="settings-chat"></a>

On the **Chat** tab, you set defaults that apply to your conversations.
+ **Instructions** – Global instructions that Quick sends with every message across all agents, such as your preferred language, format, and style.
+ **Default agent** – The agent that Quick uses for new chats.

## General
<a name="settings-general"></a>

On the **General** tab, you control notifications, browser behavior, keyboard shortcuts, background tasks, and updates. Several of these settings apply only in the desktop app, as noted.
+ **Desktop notifications** – In the desktop app, show operating-system notifications when Quick is in the background.
+ **Browser** – In the desktop app, choose the default browser that Quick opens for web tasks and automation (**System default**, **Chrome**, **Edge**, **Brave**, or **Firefox**). You can also turn on **Use my existing browser** to connect Quick to your running browser, where your logins, cookies, and extensions are available.
+ **Keyboard shortcuts** – In the desktop app, set a screenshot-capture shortcut and the key combination that sends a message, including a custom option that you record.
+ **Maximum parallel tasks** – Use the slider, from 1 to 20, to control how many background tasks run at the same time.
+ **Updates** – In the desktop app, turn on automatic updates so that Quick downloads new versions in the background and installs them on restart.

### Use my existing browser
<a name="settings-existing-browser"></a>

In the desktop app, when you connect Quick to your running browser, follow the setup steps to enable remote debugging, and then test the connection. Remote debugging lets applications on your computer connect to the browser and access open pages, cookies, and authenticated sessions. Turn it off when you are not using this feature. When this option is off, the agent launches a separate browser instance with a copy of your profile.

## Privacy controls
<a name="settings-privacy-controls"></a>

On the **Privacy controls** tab, you control whether Quick learns from your conversations, searches your conversation history, and extracts entities from connected services. You can also view, edit, and delete individual memories. For more information, see [Your knowledge graph and My context](knowledge-graph.md).

## Advanced
<a name="settings-advanced"></a>

On the **Advanced** tab, you can export diagnostics. In the desktop app, choose a time range and export diagnostic logs for troubleshooting.

## My context
<a name="settings-my-context"></a>

My context is where you review and manage what Quick knows about you and your work. You open it from the account menu. It groups two things: your memories and your knowledge graph.

Your memories are the facts and preferences that Quick learns from your conversations. You review, edit, and delete memories from your chat preferences. For more information, see [Memory and response personalization](using-quick-chat.md#chat-memory) and [Managing your memories](using-quick-chat.md#manage-memories).
