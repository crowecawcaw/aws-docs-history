

# Getting the app
<a name="mobile-getting-the-app"></a>

Download Amazon Quick from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amazon-quick/id1148226615) on iOS or from [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amazonaws.quicksight.mobile) on Android. After you install the app, open it and sign in to get started.

**Note**  
Some capabilities require a recent version of iOS or Android. To get the full mobile experience, keep the app and your operating system up to date.
