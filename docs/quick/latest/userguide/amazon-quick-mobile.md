

# Amazon Quick on mobile
<a name="amazon-quick-mobile"></a>

Amazon Quick is available as a companion app for iOS and Android that works alongside the desktop application. Use the mobile app to chat with Quick, review your activity feed, and open dashboards while you are away from your computer. You sign in with the same account, and your conversations stay in sync across your devices.

The mobile app focuses on the tasks you are most likely to do on a phone. Some capabilities that are available on desktop are limited or not available on mobile. This chapter describes what you can do on mobile and how the mobile experience differs from desktop.
