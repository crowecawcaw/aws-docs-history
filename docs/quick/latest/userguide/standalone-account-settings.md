# Account settings for Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts

This topic describes how to view and manage account-level settings for Amazon Quick
Free and Plus accounts created at [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick "https://aws.com/quick").
Account settings include your profile information, account details, and support
access.

###### Note

This section applies to Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts. If your organization
manages Amazon Quick through the AWS Management Console, account settings are managed through your
AWS account and IAM Identity Center. For more information, see [Administering Amazon Quick](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/qsysadmin.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/qsysadmin.md").

## Accessing account settings

The account settings are located in the Amazon Quick account settings portal,
alongside Plan & Billing.

###### To access account settings

1. In Amazon Quick, from the navigation bar, choose your username.
2. Choose **Manage account**.
3. In the left navigation of the Manage Account page, choose **Plan
   and billing**.

The settings portal opens in a new browser tab at
`account.global.app.aws`. 4. In the left navigation of the settings portal, choose **My
account**.

## My account

The My account page displays your profile information and account details.

###### Topics

- [Profile & security](#standalone-account-settings-profile-security "#standalone-account-settings-profile-security")
- [Account details](#standalone-account-settings-details "#standalone-account-settings-details")

### Profile & security

The Profile & security section displays the following information:

- **Name** – Your display name. This
  is the name associated with your AWS Builder ID.
- **Email** – Your email address. This
  is the email associated with your AWS Builder ID.
- **Manage profile & security** –
  Choose this link to open the AWS Builder ID profile management page in
  a new browser tab. From there, you can update your name, nickname, email,
  password, multi-factor authentication settings, and privacy
  preferences.

###### Note

Your name and email address in Amazon Quick are managed through your AWS
Builder ID. To change these details, choose **Manage profile &
security** to open the Builder ID profile page, or choose
**Edit profile** from the Amazon Quick profile menu. For more
information, see [User profile and preferences in
Amazon Quick](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-profile.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-profile.md").

### Account details

The Account details section displays the following information:

- **Account ID** – A unique numeric
  identifier for your Amazon Quick account. You may need this ID when
  contacting AWS Support.
- **Copy Account ID** – Choose this
  button to copy your Account ID to the clipboard.
- **Close account** – Choose this
  button to permanently close your Amazon Quick account.

###### Warning

Closing your account is a permanent action. When you close your account,
all data, assets, and resources associated with the account are permanently
deleted. This includes chat history, agents, spaces, automations, and any
uploaded files. This action cannot be undone. All users associated with the
account will lose access immediately.

## Support

To access AWS Support for Amazon Quick:

- In the left navigation of the settings portal, choose
  **Support**.

The AWS Support portal opens in a new browser tab at
`support.aws.amazon.com`.

From the AWS Support portal, you can:

- Create a new support case for Amazon Quick.
- View the status of existing support cases.
- Access self-service troubleshooting resources.

###### Tip

You can also access support resources from within Amazon Quick by choosing your
username, then choosing **Help** in the profile menu. The Help
submenu includes links to Getting Started guides, Quick Documentation,
the Quick Community forum, and How to Videos.
