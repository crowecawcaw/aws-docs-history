# Renaming bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight

Use the following procedure to rename a bookmark.

###### To rename a bookmark

1. In a published dashboard, choose the bookmark icon at upper right to open
   the **Bookmarks** pane.
2. In the **Bookmarks** pane, choose the context menu (the
   three vertical dots) for the bookmark that you want to rename, and then
   choose **rename**.
3. In the **Rename bookmark** pane, enter a name for the
   bookmark, and then choose **Save**.
