# Understanding usage on the Amazon Quick Plus plan

Amazon Quick uses limits to keep your usage predictable and to spread heavy activity
evenly across your billing month. Understanding how these limits work helps you avoid
interruptions, decide when to enable more usage, and get the most from your plan.

###### Note

This page applies to Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts created with email or social
login at [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick "https://aws.com/quick"). It does not apply to
Amazon Quick Professional or Enterprise plans managed through the AWS Management Console, which
handle usage differently.

## How usage limits work

Your usage is governed by two limits that work together: a monthly limit and a
session limit.

- **Monthly limit** – The total amount of
  work you can do with Quick in a billing month. This limit resets at
  the start of each billing cycle.
- **Session limit** – A shorter, rolling
  window that limits activity in any single stretch. The session limit is a
  percentage of your monthly limit. It refreshes continuously as time passes, so
  you no longer need to wait until the next billing month. When you reach a session
  limit, Quick pauses your session briefly. It does not block you for
  the rest of the month.

###### Note

Reaching a session limit is temporary. It clears on its own as the rolling window
moves forward, so you can keep working after a short pause.

Quick measures your usage by the length and complexity of your work, the
features you use, the model selected, and the thinking level. Heavier tasks use more;
lighter tasks use less. Because usage depends on these factors, the same activity can
consume different amounts on different days.

### What to do when you hit a limit

| Limit         | Free plan                                                                         | Plus plan                                                                                 |
| ------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Session limit | Wait for the session window to refresh, or upgrade to Plus for more<br>usage.     | Wait for the session window to refresh, or enable additional usage<br>to keep working.    |
| Monthly limit | Upgrade to Plus, or wait for the reset at the start of the next<br>billing cycle. | Enable additional usage, or wait for the reset at the start of the<br>next billing cycle. |

## How to get more usage

If you need more usage, you can enable additional usage and set a maximum monthly
limit—a spend cap that you control.

- **You control the cost** – You only pay
  for what you actually use, up to the cap you set. The cap is a ceiling, not a
  charge.
- **What changes** – Once enabled, your
  monthly capacity expands up to your cap, and your session windows adjust
  accordingly, so you're less likely to be paused mid-task.

###### To enable additional usage

1. In Amazon Quick, from the navigation bar, choose your username, and then
   choose **Manage account**.
2. Enable additional usage.
3. Set your maximum monthly limit (spend cap).

## Best practices to conserve usage

Use the following practices to make your usage go further before you need to enable
additional usage:

- **Start fresh for new tasks** – Start a
  new chat for a new topic. Long, growing conversations carry their whole history
  with each turn and consume more usage. A fresh chat is lighter.
- **Match the thinking level to the task**
  – Use a lower thinking level for routine tasks, and reserve the highest
  thinking level for genuinely complex work. Higher thinking levels consume more
  usage.
- **Be concise and specific** – Clear,
  scoped prompts avoid long back-and-forth and re-work, which saves usage.
