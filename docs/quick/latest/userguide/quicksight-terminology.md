# Amazon Quick concepts

The following are some important terms that you will encounter in this guide.

## Data preparation

Data _preparation_ is the process of transforming data for use in
an analysis. This includes making changes like the following:

- Filtering out data so that you can focus on what's important to you.
- Renaming fields to make them easier to read.
- Changing data types so that they are more useful.
- Adding calculated fields to enhance analysis.
- Creating SQL queries to refine data.

## SPICE

_SPICE (Super-fast, Parallel, In-memory Calculation
Engine)_ is the robust in-memory engine that Quick
uses. SPICE is engineered to rapidly perform advanced calculations and
serve data. The storage and processing capacity available in SPICE speeds
up the analytical queries that you run against your imported data. By using
SPICE, you save time because you don't need to retrieve the data
every time that you change an analysis or update a visual.

## Data analysis

A data _analysis_ is the basic workspace for creating data
visualizations, which are graphical representations of your data. Each analysis contains
a collection of visualizations that you arrange and customize.

## Data visualization

A data _visualization_, also known as a
_visual_, is a graphical representation of data. There are many types
of visualizations, including diagrams, charts, graphs, and tables. All visuals begin in
AutoGraph mode, which automatically selects the best type of visualization for the
fields that you select. You can also take control and choose your own visuals. You can
enhance your analytics by applying filters, changing colors, adding parameter controls,
custom click actions, and more.

## Index capacity

The amount of storage available for indexing your documents and data sources in
Amazon Quick. Index capacity is measured in megabytes (MB).

## Machine learning

Machine learning (ML) _Insights_ propose narrative add-ons that are
based on an evaluation of your data. You can choose one from the list, for example
forecasting or anomaly (outlier) detection. Or you can create your own. You can combine
insight calculations, narrative text, colors, images, and conditions that you
define.

## Plan tier

The subscription level for Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts. There are two plans:
Free and Plus. For more information, see [Amazon Quick plans and pricing](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-plans.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-plans.md").

## Sheet

A _sheet_ is a page that displays a set of visualizations and
insights. You can imagine this as a sheet from a newspaper, except that it's filled
with charts, graphs, tables, and insights. You can add more sheets, and make them work
separately or together in your analysis.

## Free and Plus accounts

A Amazon Quick account created directly at [quick.aws.com](https://quick.aws.com "https://quick.aws.com"), as opposed to an account provisioned through the AWS Management Console.
Free and Plus accounts use email or social login for identity management and have their own
in-app administration interface for managing users, assets, and billing. Most product
features are shared across account types, but some features such as Amazon Quick Sight and
Amazon Quick Automate are available only for accounts provisioned through the
AWS Management Console.

## Dashboard

A _dashboard_ is the published version of an analysis. You can
share with other users of Amazon Quick for reporting purposes. You specify who has
access and what they can do with the dashboard.
