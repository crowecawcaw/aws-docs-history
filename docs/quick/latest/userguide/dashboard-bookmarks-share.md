# Sharing bookmarks in Amazon Quick Sight

After you create a bookmark, you can share a URL link for the view with others who
have permission to view the dashboard. They can then save that view as their own
bookmark.

###### To share a bookmark with another dashboard subscriber

1. In a published dashboard, choose the bookmark icon at upper right to open
   the **Bookmarks** pane.
2. In the **Bookmarks** pane, choose the bookmark that you
   want to share so that the dashboard updates to that view.
3. Choose the share icon at upper right, and then choose **Share this
   view**.

You can copy the URL link that Quick Sight provides and paste it in an
email or IM message to share it with others. The recipient of the URL link
can then save the view as their own bookmark. For more information about
sharing views of a dashboard, see [Sharing your view of a Amazon Quick Sight dashboard](share-dashboard-view.md "share-dashboard-view.md").
