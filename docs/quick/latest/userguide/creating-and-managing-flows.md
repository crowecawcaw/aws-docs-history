# Creating and managing flows

This section covers the core processes for creating and managing Amazon Quick Flows. You'll learn how to create new flows, edit existing ones, manage versions, and share your flows with others in your organization.

Creating a flow involves defining its structure, components, and logic. Once created, you can edit your flows to refine their functionality or add new capabilities. Sharing enables collaboration and allows others to benefit from the flows you've created.

These topics provide comprehensive guidance on the full lifecycle of flow development and management, from initial creation through ongoing maintenance and collaboration.
