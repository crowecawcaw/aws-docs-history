# Plan and billing for Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts

This topic describes how to view and manage your plan and billing information for
Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts created at [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick "https://aws.com/quick").

###### Note

This section applies to Amazon Quick Free and Plus accounts. If your organization
manages Amazon Quick through the AWS Management Console, billing is handled through your
AWS account. For more information, see [AWS
Billing and Cost Management](../../../billing/latest/userguide/billing-what-is.md "../../../billing/latest/userguide/billing-what-is.md").

## Accessing Plan & Billing

The Plan & Billing page is located in the Amazon Quick account settings
portal.

###### To access Plan & Billing

1. In Amazon Quick, from the navigation bar, choose your username.
2. Choose **Manage account**.
3. In the left navigation of the Manage Account page, choose **Plan
   and billing**.

The Plan & Billing settings page opens in a new browser tab at
`account.global.app.aws`.

The settings page has its own navigation panel with the following sections:

- **Plan & Billing** – View your
  current plan, estimated charges, and billing history.
- **My account** – View your account
  profile and account details. For more information, see [Account settings for Amazon Quick Free and Plus
  accounts](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-account-settings.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-account-settings.md").
- **Support** – Access AWS Support
  (opens externally).

## Viewing your current plan

The Plan & Billing page displays the following information about your current
subscription:

- **Current plan** – The name and type of
  your active plan. For example, **FREE** or
  **PLUS**.
- **Manage plan** – Choose this button to
  view available plans and change your subscription. For more information,
  see [Upgrading your Amazon Quick plan](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-upgrade.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-upgrade.md").
- **Current estimated charges** – The
  estimated cost for your current billing period, displayed in USD.
- **Days remaining** – For trial plans,
  the number of days remaining in the trial period along with the expiration
  date.
- **Payment method** – The card type and
  last four digits of the payment method on file. A **Change**
  link is available to update your payment method.
- **Estimated charges breakdown** – An
  expandable section that provides a detailed breakdown of the charges for the
  current billing period.

## Billing history

The **Billing history** section displays a table of past billing
records with the following columns:

| Column             | Description                                                                  |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **ID**             | A unique identifier for the billing record.                                  |
| **Type**           | The type of charge (for example,<br>subscription).                           |
| **Status**         | The payment status (for example, paid,<br>pending).                          |
| **Due date**       | The date the payment is due.                                                 |
| **Payment method** | The payment method used for the charge.                                      |
| **Amount**         | The total amount charged.                                                    |
| **Charge type**    | The category of the charge.                                                  |
| **Action**         | Available actions for the billing record, such as<br>downloading an invoice. |

###### Note

New accounts will not have any billing records until the first billing cycle
completes. The table displays "No billing records to display" until charges are
processed.

## Billing preferences

Choose the **Preferences** button on the Plan & Billing page to
configure your billing notification preferences.

## Changing your plan

To upgrade, downgrade, or change your Amazon Quick plan, choose **Manage
plan** on the Plan & Billing page. For detailed instructions on the
upgrade process, including entering contact information and payment details, see
[Upgrading your Amazon Quick plan](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-upgrade.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-upgrade.md").

For information about the available plans and their features, see [Amazon Quick plans and pricing](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-plans.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/standalone-plans.md").
