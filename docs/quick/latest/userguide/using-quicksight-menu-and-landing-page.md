

# Using the AWS Quick menu and landing page

After you sign in to AWS Quick, you see the AWS Quick landing page. This page provides tabs for your analyses, dashboards, folders, data, and more. It also provides a menu bar at the top, with options for the following:
+ Searching AWS Quick
+ Accessing your user profile (managing Quick, language selection, and help)
+ Interacting with the Quick chat agent
+ Choosing the AWS Region that you want to work in
+ Discovering helpful resources (documentation, how-to videos, community forum, blogs, and more)

**Note**  
Consult your administrator before changing your AWS Region. Your default AWS Region is configured by your AWS Quick administrator. Changing the AWS Region changes where your work is stored.

## Navigating the homepage
<a name="navigating-the-homepage"></a>

When you sign in, Quick opens to a chat-first homepage, where you start by typing a question or request. Using the navigation menu on the left, you can explore how to work with dashboards, build powerful analyses, manage your data sources, organize content with folders, leverage advanced analytics capabilities, and more.

The homepage shows a time-aware greeting, with the chat composer as the main entry point. It also surfaces connection setup cards for data sources that you have not set up yet. In addition, it shows **Quick Briefs**, a summary of your highest-priority feed items, with a link to open the full feed.

From the chat composer, you choose which agent handles the conversation. You set the response mode to **Fast**, **Balanced**, or **Smart**. You also set the thinking effort to **Off**, **Low**, **Medium**, or **High**. You can start an **Incognito** chat, a private conversation that Quick does not save to your history.

The following are all the available AWS Quick capabilities:
+ **Chat** – Start and continue conversations with Quick. Chat is the primary way that you work.
+ **Feed** – Review a prioritized activity feed, your calendar, and your to-dos, drawn from connected services. For more information, see [Stay on top of your work with your proactive feed](working-with-feed.md).
+ **Apps** – Open and build interactive applications in AWS Quick.
+ **Library** – Find and reuse your content in one place. Library is the home for your recently viewed, shared, and favorite items. For more information, see [Use Library to find your work](working-with-library.md).
+ **Agents** – Build AI agents that intelligently analyze, plan, and work with your data to help complete tasks.
+ **Spaces** – Work with Amazon Q using specific files, data, and information to complete projects or organize teams.
+ **Scenarios** – Elevate your data exploration in the AI-driven workbook canvas that is enhanced with generative analysis capabilities. Allow AI to suggest relevant data to enrich your insight discovery.
+ **Stories** – Use Amazon Q prompts and visuals to produce a story that incorporates the details that you provide.
+ **Topics** – Create a new generative topic to introduce the generative Q&A experience for users.
+ **Dashboards** – Share interactive data visualizations with your audience. You can explore and filter visualizations according to your specific needs, and generate snapshots for easy sharing.
+ **Analyses** – Dive deep into your data with robust visualization and analysis tools. Create charts and graphs to uncover insights before publishing to a wider audience.
+ **Favorites** – Favorite the dashboards and analyses you want to easily find later.
+ **Folders** – Organize your analyses and dashboards in folders.
+ **Automations** – Create automated processes that reduce manual intervention and save valuable time.
+ **Flows** – Create and share intelligent workflows to accomplish goals quickly and predictably.
+ **Data** – Connect to data and visualize, create specialized chat assistants, or mix both.
+ **Actions** – Perform tasks such as creating and updating tickets, managing incidents, and accessing project information directly.

To select and reorder the items that appear in the navigation panel, choose **Edit navigation**.

## The homepage
<a name="customizing-the-homepage-layout"></a>

When you sign in, Quick opens to a chat-first homepage. You start by typing a question or request in the chat box, and you use the navigation panel on the left to reach the rest of Quick. The homepage no longer uses customizable widgets.

Your recently viewed, shared, and favorite items now live in **Library**, the single place to find and reuse your content. For more information, see [Use Library to find your work](working-with-library.md).

The metric and dashboard widgets that you pinned from your dashboards now live in **Mission Control**. For more information, see [Monitor and manage agents and flows with Mission Control](mission-control.md).

## Accessing user profiles
<a name="accessing-user-profiles"></a>

To open the account menu, choose your account icon at the bottom left of the navigation panel in AWS Quick. From this menu, you open **Settings** and **My context**, choose a language, change your AWS Region, get help from the documentation, or sign out of AWS Quick.

Your account icon is also where you view your current session usage.

The following options are available from the account menu:
+ **Settings** – Configure Quick, including your account, appearance, chat, privacy, and more. For more information, see [Settings](settings.md).
+ **My context** – Review and manage what Quick knows about you and your work, including your memories and your knowledge graph. For more information, see [Your knowledge graph and My context](knowledge-graph.md).
+ **Manage Quick** – If you have appropriate permissions, you can access administrative functions such as managing users, subscriptions, [SPICE](spice.md) capacity, and account settings.
+ **Language setting** – Choose the language you want to use in the AWS Quick user interface.
+ **Region setting** – Choose the AWS Region that you want to work in.
**Note**  
Consult your administrator before changing your AWS Region. Your default AWS Region is configured by your AWS Quick administrator. Changing the AWS Region changes where your work is stored.
+ **Help** – This will open the official Quick documentation, which you can view online, in Kindle, or as a PDF.
+ **Sign out** – Choose this option to sign out of AWS Quick and your AWS session.

## Finding recent conversations
<a name="using-quicksight-recents"></a>

Below the navigation panel, the **Recents** area lists your previous conversations, organized into folders and by date. You can organize conversations into folders, where each folder shows a count, and you can pin a conversation. From a conversation's menu, you can rename, move, archive, or delete it. You can also search across your conversations. Use **Auto-clean up** to sort chats into folders by topic.

## Working with panels
<a name="using-quicksight-panels"></a>

Overlay panels give you access to content and tooling without leaving your current chat view. These panels include session activity, the **Feed**, **Mission Control**, the **All data and apps** panel, and **Session tabs**. The **All data and apps** panel shows the spaces, local folders, and skills available to the current conversation. **Session tabs** provide an in-conversation reader where documents open beside the chat. For more information about your pinned metrics and dashboards, see [Monitor and manage agents and flows with Mission Control](mission-control.md).
