# Granting anyone on the

internet access to an Amazon Quick Sight dashboard

|                                            |
| ------------------------------------------ |
| \*_Applies<br>to:_<br>• Enterprise Edition |

You can also share your Amazon Quick Sight dashboard with anyone on the internet from the
**Share** menu in the Amazon Quick console. When you do
this, anyone on the internet will be able to access the dashboard, even if they
aren't a registered user on your Quick account, when you share the
dashboard link or embed the dashboard.

Use the following sections to grant anyone on the internet access to dashboard
when you share it.
