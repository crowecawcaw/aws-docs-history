# Upgrading your Amazon Quick plan

You can upgrade your Amazon Quick Free
plan to the Plus plan at any time. This page walks you through the full upgrade
process, including entering contact information, verifying your phone number, and adding a
payment method.

###### Note

This page applies to Amazon Quick accounts created with email or social at [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick "https://aws.com/quick"). For information about
subscription management for Amazon Quick accessed through the AWS Management Console, see [Administering Amazon Quick](../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/qsysadmin.md "../../../quicksuite/latest/userguide/qsysadmin.md").

## Accessing plan management

You can access the plan management page in two ways.

###### Option 1: Through the profile menu

1. In the Amazon Quick navigation bar, choose your username.
2. Choose **Upgrade plan**. The plan management page opens in
   a new browser tab.

###### Option 2: Through Manage Account

1. In the Amazon Quick navigation bar, choose your username.
2. Choose **Manage account**.
3. In the left navigation of the Manage Account page, choose **Plan and
   billing**.
4. On the Plan & Billing page, choose **Manage plan**.

## Step-by-step upgrade procedure

###### To upgrade to the Plus plan

1. **Review available plans.**

On the **Manage plan** page, you see the available
plans.

Your current plan is indicated with a "Current plan" badge and the number of
days remaining (for trial plans).

To see a detailed feature comparison, expand the **Compare
plans** section at the bottom of the page. 2. **Select your new plan.**

Under the plan you want to upgrade to, choose **Choose
plan**. A **Continue** button appears at the
bottom of the page.

Choose **Continue** to proceed. 3. **Enter your contact information.**

You are redirected to a contact information form.

Choose **Continue** after completing all required
fields. 4. **Verify your phone number.**

Amazon Quick needs to verify your phone number for security purposes.

###### Note

If you don't receive the code, choose **Resend code**
after the countdown timer completes. Make sure your phone can receive SMS
messages from short codes.

    1. On the **Verify your phone number** page, confirm
     that the displayed phone number is correct. If not, choose
     **Edit** to change it.
    2. Choose **Send text message**. Amazon Quick sends a
     4-digit verification code to your phone via SMS.
    3. Enter the 4-digit verification code in the **Verification
     code** field.
    4. Choose **Continue**.

5. **Enter payment information.**

###### Note

You might be redirected to your bank's website to authorize a
verification charge. This is a temporary hold and is not an actual
charge. 6. **Complete the upgrade.**

After entering your payment information, choose **Upgrade
plan**.

Amazon Quick processes your payment method and activates your new plan. You
are redirected to the **Plan & Billing** page.

Your upgrade takes effect immediately. All team collaboration features are
now available.

## Billing information

- **Billing cycle:** Monthly, starting from the
  date of your upgrade.
- The estimated charges shown on the Plan & Billing page reflect your current
  user count.
- **Billing history:** Past invoices appear in
  the **Billing history** table on the Plan & Billing page.
  The table shows the invoice ID, type, status, due date, payment method, amount,
  and charge type.
- **Changing payment method:** On the Plan &
  Billing page, choose **Change** next to your payment method to
  update your card information.

## Downgrading your plan

To downgrade from Plus to Free:

1. Navigate to **Plan and billing** → **Manage
   plan**.
2. Under **Free**, choose **Choose
   plan**.
3. Follow the confirmation prompts.

###### Important

Downgrading to the Free plan reduces your account to a single user and removes
access to team collaboration features. All data and configurations are preserved,
but only the account owner retains access.
