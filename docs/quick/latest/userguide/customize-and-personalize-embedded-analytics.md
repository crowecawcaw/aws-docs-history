# Embedding custom

Amazon Quick Sight assets into your application

You can use Amazon Quick Sight embedded analytics to embed custom Amazon Quick Sight assets
into your application that are tailored to meet your business needs. For embedded
dashboards and visuals, Amazon Quick Sight authors can add filters and drill downs that
readers can access as they navigate the dashboard or visual. Amazon Quick Sight developers can
also use the Amazon Quick Sight SDKs to build tighter integrations between their SaaS
applications and their Amazon Quick Sight embedded assets to add datapoint callback
actions to visuals on a dashboard at runtime.

For more information about the Amazon Quick Sight SDKs, see the
`amazon-quicksight-embedding-sdk` on [GitHub](https://github.com/awslabs/amazon-quicksight-embedding-sdk "https://github.com/awslabs/amazon-quicksight-embedding-sdk") or
[NPM](https://www.npmjs.com/package/amazon-quicksight-embedding-sdk "https://www.npmjs.com/package/amazon-quicksight-embedding-sdk").

Following, you can find descriptions of how to use the Amazon Quick Sight SDKs to
customize your Amazon Quick Sight embedded analytics.
