

# Build web applications with apps in Amazon Quick
<a name="using-amazon-quick-apps"></a>

You can use apps in Amazon Quick to build interactive web applications by describing what you need in natural language. You tell the apps in Quick agent what your app should do, who it is for, and what data it needs. The agent builds a working app in real time while you watch. You refine it through ongoing conversation. You can then publish and share the app for daily use across your organization.

With Apps in Quick you can bridge the gap between data visualization and custom application development. Whether you need a dashboard, an internal tool, a data entry interface, or a document viewer, you can create it conversationally and publish it for your team in minutes.

## Key capabilities
<a name="quick-apps-key-capabilities"></a>

With Apps in Quick, you get the following capabilities:
+ **Conversational authoring** — Describe what you want, and the AI builds it.
+ **Live dashboard embeds** — Embed interactive Amazon Quick Sight visuals directly in your app.
+ **Connectors** — Call external APIs and services from your app. For more information, see [Work with integrations in Amazon Quick](working-with-integrations.md).
+ **Built-in AI inference** — Add AI-powered features such as text summarization, classification, and content generation using foundation models.
+ **Spaces integration** — Read and manage documents and resources from Amazon Quick spaces. For more information, see [Organize, collaborate, and share resources with spaces in Amazon Quick](working-with-spaces.md).
+ **Persistent data storage** — Save and retrieve data across sessions with built-in key-value storage.
+ **Enterprise authentication** — Inherits Quick's security model with single sign-on support.
+ **One-click publishing** — Share apps with your team instantly.

## How apps in Quick works
<a name="quick-apps-how-it-works"></a>

Building an app follows this workflow:

1. **Describe** — Tell the AI what you want to build.

1. **Generate** — The AI writes the code in real time.

1. **Preview** — See a live preview instantly and test the app.

1. **Iterate** — Refine with follow-up requests.

1. **Publish** — Share with your team in one click.
