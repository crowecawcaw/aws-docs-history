

# What is AWS Quick?

AWS Quick is an AI-powered service for automating tasks, analyzing data, building web applications, and conducting research. You interact with Quick through natural language chat, and Quick uses AI agents to process your requests against connected data sources and applications.

Quick includes the following features:
+ **AWS Quick Sight** – Interactive data visualization and business intelligence. Connect to data sources, build dashboards, and embed analytics in applications.
+ **AWS Quick Flows** – Empowers any business user to automate repetitive, routine tasks without needing technical expertise.
+ **AWS Quick Automate** – Enables power users to orchestrate end-to-end business processes at enterprise scale.
+ **AWS Quick Index** – Connect your organization's documents and data sources so AI responses are grounded in your information.
+ **AWS Quick Research** – Conduct in-depth research across the web and your data, delivered as a cited report.
+ **Apps in AWS Quick** – Build interactive web applications by describing what you need in natural language.

Quick also keeps your work together as you move between surfaces. A Library gives you one place to find your recently viewed, shared, and favorite items; a proactive feed surfaces what needs your attention; and always-on agents keep working in the background and deliver results when they are ready. These are available whether you work on the web, desktop, or mobile.

**Note**  
AWS Quick evolved from Amazon QuickSight. QuickSight continues as AWS Quick Sight, a feature within Quick. All existing QuickSight APIs, SDKs, and integrations continue to work without changes.

## Benefits of Quick
<a name="benefits-overview"></a>

**Everything in one place**  
Agents, research, automation, analytics, and app building are all built in. You don't need separate products for each capability.

**Fully managed**  
No infrastructure to provision, no models to host, no ML expertise required.

**Connects to your tools and data**  
Quick integrates with the apps, databases, and systems your organization already uses through knowledge bases, action connectors, extensions, and structured data connections. Open standards including Model Context Protocol (MCP) and OpenAPI let you connect any system.

**Meets you where you work**  
Use Quick from the web, the desktop application, or directly inside Chrome, Slack, Microsoft Teams, and Microsoft 365 applications. The same conversations, agents, and context follow you across the web, desktop, and mobile, so you can start work on one surface and pick it up on another.

**AI you can trust**  
Quick runs on AWS with the same identity, network, and compliance controls your security team already approved. Your data stays in your control.

## Accessing Quick
<a name="accessing-quick"></a>

To start using AWS Quick, you have two options:

1. **Sign up at [aws.com/quick](https://aws.com/quick)** – Use your email or social credentials (Google, Apple, Amazon, GitHub). No AWS account required. Free and Plus plans available.

1. **Provision through the AWS Management Console** – Configure Quick for your organization with IAM Identity Center or IAM Federation. Per-user subscriptions with standard AWS billing.
