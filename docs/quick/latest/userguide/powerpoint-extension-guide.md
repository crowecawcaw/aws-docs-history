# Amazon Quick Microsoft PowerPoint extension

The Amazon Quick extension for PowerPoint helps you create compelling,
data-driven presentations by integrating your enterprise data, AI-powered design
assistance, and intelligent content generation directly into
PowerPoint.

You can search for Amazon Quick in the Microsoft PowerPoint app store
or visit the [Quick
for PowerPoint Microsoft store page](https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/WA200010606 "https://marketplace.microsoft.com/en-us/product/WA200010606") to add the extension.

Key capabilities include:

- **Presentation creation with enterprise data:**
  Generate complete presentations using data from Quick dashboards,
  spaces, and knowledge bases. Pull real-time metrics and insights directly from
  your organization's data sources, or create presentations from outlines, topics,
  or natural language descriptions.
- **Visual narratives from enterprise knowledge:**
  Use Quick's enterprise knowledge to create contextual, accurate
  presentations. Import and visualize data from your Quick spaces,
  dashboards, and knowledge bases in compelling slides.
- **Brand consistency and professional design:**
  Apply your organization's themes and templates to presentations, respect
  existing presentation formatting and primary slide layouts, and generate
  professionally formatted tables, layouts, and design elements.
- **Natural language editing:** Select slides or
  content and request modifications using conversational prompts. Add, delete,
  reorder, or reorganize slides effortlessly, and chat about your presentation
  content to get insights and suggestions.

###### Important

- The Amazon Quick PowerPoint extension uses generative AI
  to create and execute code within your PowerPoint application
  sandbox. AI can make mistakes and perform inaccurate actions within your
  PowerPoint slide deck. No presentation data is read when the
  side panel is closed, and no data is sent to Amazon Quick unless you
  explicitly send a prompt.
- Amazon Quick does not use your user data for service improvement or for
  training its underlying large language models (LLMs).

## Amazon Quick Microsoft PowerPoint extension usage guidelines

As a user, you are responsible for keeping company information safe. The
following guidance helps you use Amazon Quick apps securely while maintaining
data privacy and compliance.

### Conversation retention and accuracy

Each conversation is stored for 30 days. You can review and manage your
conversation history by choosing the conversation history button in the
PowerPoint extension.

Amazon Quick uses generative AI. You should review responses for
accuracy.

Usage of the Amazon Quick extension for Microsoft PowerPoint is
subject to the [AWS Responsible AI Policy](https://aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/policy/ "https://aws.amazon.com/ai/responsible-ai/policy/").

### Security considerations

To protect your organization's data, carefully evaluate usage and
plan deployment with data privacy in mind. Amazon Quick maintains strict
data privacy by not using customer data for service improvements, not using
customer data to enhance language models, and not indexing Microsoft
PowerPoint conversations into your company's Amazon Quick
instance.

## Sample prompts

The following prompts demonstrate common ways to use Quick within
PowerPoint. You can adapt these to your specific needs.

- "Create a 10-slide presentation on [topic] including key metrics and
  recommendations, pulling data from my Quick
  dashboard."
- "Add a slide with a competitive comparison showing our advantages
  over the top 3 competitors."
- "Make this deck more executive-friendly by reducing text and
  emphasizing key numbers."
- "Reorganize this presentation to lead with results instead of
  methodology."
- "Create a chart slide comparing actual vs. target performance by
  department using data from my Quick space."

###### Tip

For best results, be specific in your prompts. Include names, dates, and
goals. You can also reference your Quick spaces, dashboards, and
knowledge bases for personalized results.
