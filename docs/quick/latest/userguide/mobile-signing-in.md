

# Signing in
<a name="mobile-signing-in"></a>

Sign in to the mobile app with your organization's Amazon Quick credentials. The app supports enterprise single sign-on (SSO), including IAM Identity Center federation.

For added security, you can turn on biometric app lock (Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint) so that Amazon Quick requires biometric verification each time you open it. You manage this in Settings, under Security. Your administrator can also require biometric lock for your organization.
