# Manage topic permissions through

dashboards in Amazon Quick Sight

Quick enables Authors to manage permissions for dashboards and their
linked topics from a single location. When sharing dashboards with Q&A enabled,
Authors can control topic viewer access directly from a dashboard's sharing preferences,
eliminating the need to manage permissions in multiple locations.

###### To enable Q&A on a dashboard with a linked topic:

1. Open the [Quick console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Open the analysis of the dashboard with Q&A enabled and topic linked that
   you want to publish.
3. Choose **Publish**.
4. Check the **Allow data Q&A** check box.
5. Choose **MANAGE Q&A** and select **Use a linked
   topic for Build visual and Q&A**.
6. Select the desired linked topic from the dropdown menu.
7. Choose **APPLY CHANGES**, then choose **Publish
   dashboard**.

###### To conveniently manage topic access from a dashboard:

1. Open the [Quick console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Open the dashboard with a linked topic that you are a co-owner of.
3. Select the share icon and choose **Share dashboard**.
4. In the row of your selected user, flip on/off the **Share as "topic
   viewer"** toggle to grant/revoke viewer access to the linked
   topic.
5. In the row of your selected shared folder, flip on/off the **Add topic
   to folder** toggle to add/remove the linked topic to/from the
   shared folder.

###### To share the dashboard and its linked topic to all users and groups:

1. Open the [Quick console](https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/ "https://quicksight.aws.amazon.com/").
2. Open the dashboard with a linked topic that you are a co-owner of.
3. Select the share icon and choose **Share dashboard**.
4. On the bottom-left of the panel, under **Auto-share linked topic
   for**, flip the **All dashboard users and groups**
   toggle on. This will grant viewer access to the linked topic when the dashboared
   is shared. Flip the toggle off to cancel this behavior.
   After the dashboard with a linked topic has been shared, users will immediately be
   able to ask questions about their data. Navigate to **Ask a question about
   <topic name>** at the top of the dashboard to start asking
   questions.
