# Understanding the carbon emissions data

export

You can use the carbon emissions data export to get carbon emissions estimates from the
Customer Carbon Footprint Tool for all usage accounts linked to your payer account. For more
information about how these carbon emissions are calculated and categorized, see [Viewing
your carbon footprint](../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/ccft-overview.md "../../../awsaccountbilling/latest/aboutv2/ccft-overview.md") in the _AWS Billing User Guide_.
