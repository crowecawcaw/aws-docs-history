

# Cost Analytics columns

These columns appear in your CUR 2.0 export when you enable **Include standardized cost and rate columns** during setup. These columns have the prefix cost\_analytics\_ and are grouped as Cost Analytics columns. They contain data about the rate and the cost that apply to the line item.



| Column name | Description | Data Type | Nullability | Properties | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| cost\_analytics\_billed\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the cost serving as the basis for invoicing. This includes the impacts of all reduced rate, purchasing options, and discounts while excluding the amortization of relevant purchases paid to cover future eligible charges. | double | Not nullable | This field is not null for all charge types<br />This field represents cost inclusive of all discounts, reduced rates for purchasing options (for example, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances) as well as tiered pricing<br />The sum of this field for a billing period equals the invoice amount for that billing period | 
| cost\_analytics\_billed\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU, inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rate purchasing options and discounts while excluding the amortization of relevant purchases paid to cover future eligible charges. | double | Nullable | This field is null when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Fee, Credit, Tax, or Refund<br />This field is calculated as **cost\_analytics\_billed\_cost** divided by **line\_item\_usage\_amount**<br />This field contains a non-negative value | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_billed\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the billed cost without contracted or private discounts, inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rate and purchasing options. For pricing models with contracted pricing, this shows the equivalent public price of the pricing model. | double | Not nullable | This field is not null for all charge types<br />This field reflects the equivalent public pricing cost without discounts<br />This field is inclusive of reduced rate for purchasing options (for example, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances) | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_billed\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU at public pricing rates, inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rate and purchasing options, but exclusive of contracted or private discounts. | double | Nullable | This field is calculated as **cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_billed\_cost** divided by **line\_item\_usage\_amount**<br />This field reflects public rates without contracted or private discounts | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_on\_demand\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the cost at public on-demand rates, excluding the impact of discounts, tiered pricing, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, and EC2 Spot. | double | Nullable | This field is null when a charge is not applicable to on-demand pricing (for example, tax, credits) <br />This field is null when no public on-demand rate exists for the SKU<br />This field shows the value of the highest tier price for SKUs with multiple pricing tiers<br />This field shows the value of the public price of the subscription for products with subscription<br />This field does not include any contracted or purchased discounts | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_on\_demand\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU at public on-demand rates, excluding the impact of discounts, tiered pricing, Savings Plans, Reserved Instances, and EC2 Spot. | double | Nullable | This field is null when no public on-demand rate exists for the SKU<br />This field is not null and contains the on-demand rate for SKU when it's available in the AWS Price List API<br />This field represents the highest tier rate for SKUs with multiple pricing tiers<br />This field is always equal to **pricing\_public\_on\_demand\_rate** for all line item types | 
| cost\_analytics\_list\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the cost based on the published on-demand prices for AWS services with consideration of tiered pricing, exclusive of all reduced rate purchasing options, discounts, and amortization adjustments. | double | Nullable | This field is populated only when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Usage or SavingsPlanCoveredUsage, and is null for all other line item types. It is also null for EC2 Spot and EC2 Capacity Blocks usage.<br />This field considers tiered pricing and does not reflect discounts or commitment-based adjustments | 
| cost\_analytics\_list\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU based on published on-demand prices with consideration of tiered pricing, exclusive of all reduced rate purchasing options, discounts, and amortization adjustments. | double | Nullable | This field is populated only when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Usage or SavingsPlanCoveredUsage, and is null for all other line item types. It is also null for EC2 Spot and EC2 Capacity Blocks for ML usage, and when **line\_item\_usage\_amount** is 0.<br />This field reflects the published on-demand unit price with tiered pricing consideration<br />This field reflects **line\_item\_unblended\_rate**, including tiered pricing, before negotiated discounts and before Savings Plans or Reserved Instances benefits. | 
| cost\_analytics\_contracted\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the cost after applying contracted discounts and tier-based pricing while excluding commitment-based discounts like Savings Plans or Reserved Instances. Used for calculating savings achieved through contract negotiations. | double | Nullable | This field is populated when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Usage, SavingsPlanCoveredUsage, or DiscountedUsage, and is null for all other line item types<br />This field excludes commitment-based discounts | 
| cost\_analytics\_contracted\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU after applying contracted discounts and tier-based pricing while excluding commitment-based discounts like Savings Plans or Reserved Instances. | double | Nullable | This field is not null for usage and purchase line items<br />This field is null for Tax line items and charges that have no unit price (for example, Credit, Refund)<br />This field equals **cost\_analytics\_list\_rate** when no negotiated discounts apply<br />This field excludes commitment-based discounts | 
| cost\_analytics\_effective\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents a cost inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rates purchasing options and discounts, augmented with the amortization of upfront or recurring fee payments. | double | Not nullable | This field is 0 when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Fee, SavingsPlanUpfrontFee, SavingsPlanNegation, or Discount.<br />For Usage line items, this field equals **line\_item\_net\_unblended\_cost**, except for EC2 Capacity Blocks for ML usage, where it reflects the amortized reservation cost.<br />For SavingsPlanCoveredUsage line items, this field equals **savings\_plan\_net\_savings\_plan\_effective\_cost**. For SavingsPlanRecurringFee line items, it shows the unused portion of the Savings Plans commitment, after negotiated discounts.<br />For DiscountedUsage line items, this field equals **reservation\_net\_effective\_cost**. For RIFee line items, it equals the sum of **reservation\_net\_unused\_amortized\_upfront\_fee\_for\_billing\_period** and **reservation\_net\_unused\_recurring\_fee**.<br />For CapacityBlockRecurringFee, Tax, Credit, Refund, and other line item types, this field equals **cost\_analytics\_billed\_cost**.<br />When both fields have values, this field is less than or equal to **cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_effective\_cost**. | 
| cost\_analytics\_effective\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the unit price of a SKU inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rates purchasing options and discounts, augmented with the amortization of upfront or recurring fee payments. | double | Nullable | This field is populated when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Usage, SavingsPlanCoveredUsage, or DiscountedUsage, and is null for all other line item types. It is also null when **line\_item\_usage\_amount** is 0.<br />This field is calculated as **cost\_analytics\_effective\_cost** divided by **line\_item\_usage\_amount** | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_effective\_cost | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the hypothetical effective cost at public pricing rates, inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rate purchasing options and augmented with the amortization of relevant purchases, but exclusive of contracted or private discounts. | double | Nullable | This field is null when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Fee, SavingsPlanUpfrontFee, SavingsPlanNegation, or Discount.<br />For Usage line items, this field equals **line\_item\_unblended\_cost**, except for EC2 Capacity Blocks for ML usage, where it equals **reservation\_effective\_cost**.<br />For DiscountedUsage line items, this field equals **reservation\_effective\_cost**. For RIFee line items, it equals the sum of **reservation\_unused\_amortized\_upfront\_fee\_for\_billing\_period** and **reservation\_unused\_recurring\_fee**.<br />For SavingsPlanCoveredUsage line items, this field reflects the usage at Savings Plans rates before negotiated discounts. For SavingsPlanRecurringFee line items, it shows the unused portion of the Savings Plans commitment before negotiated discounts.<br />For CapacityBlockRecurringFee, Tax, Credit, Refund, and other line item types, this field equals **line\_item\_unblended\_cost**.<br />This field includes Savings Plans and Reserved Instances benefits but excludes negotiated discounts. When both fields have values, it is greater than or equal to **cost\_analytics\_effective\_cost**.<br />For a Savings Plan or Reserved Instance, the SUM of this field over a billing period represents that period's amortized commitment cost before negotiated discounts, not the total amount paid for the term. | 
| cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_effective\_rate | **Table configuration:** Added by: INCLUDE STANDARDIZED COST AND RATE<br />Represents the hypothetical unit price of a SKU at public pricing rates, inclusive of the impacts of all reduced rate purchasing options and augmented with the amortization of relevant purchases but exclusive of contracted discounts. | double | Nullable | This field is populated when **line\_item\_line\_item\_type** is Usage, SavingsPlanCoveredUsage, or DiscountedUsage, and is null for all other line item types. It is also null when **line\_item\_usage\_amount** is 0.<br />This field equals **cost\_analytics\_public\_equivalent\_effective\_cost** divided by **line\_item\_usage\_amount**.<br />This field excludes negotiated discounts but includes Savings Plans and Reserved Instances benefits. |
