# Partner AI apps

Amazon SageMaker Unified Studio provides access to Amazon SageMaker AI Partner AI apps. Partner AI apps include generative
AI and machine learning (ML) development applications that are built, published, and
distributed by industry-leading application providers.

Amazon SageMaker AI Partner AI apps are full application stacks that include an Amazon EKS cluster and
accompanying services such as Application Load Balancer, Amazon RDS, Amazon S3 buckets, or Amazon SQS queues.

To view the partner AI apps, complete these steps:

1. Sign in to Amazon SageMaker Unified Studio using the link that your administrator gave you.
2. From the **Explore** drop-down menu, choose **Partner AI apps**.
   For more information about SageMaker AI partner AI apps, see [Partner AI Apps overview](../../../sagemaker/latest/dg/partner-apps.md "../../../sagemaker/latest/dg/partner-apps.md") in the _Amazon SageMaker AI Developer Guide_.
