# Managing accounts and preferences

The Accounts page of the Compute Optimizer console lists the recommendation preferences that are activated
for the account or organization, such as enhanced
infrastructure metrics.

For the management account of an organization, the Accounts page also lists your
organization's member accounts and their opt-in status to Compute Optimizer. Management accounts
can [opt in](getting-started.md "getting-started.md") member accounts of the organization to AWS Compute Optimizer.
When a member account is opted in, Compute Optimizer analyzes the member account's supported
resources for potential optimization.
