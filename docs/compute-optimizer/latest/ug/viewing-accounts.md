

# Managing accounts and preferences

The **Accounts** page of the Compute Optimizer console lists the recommendation settings that are turned on for your account or organization, such as [enhanced infrastructure metrics]().

If you use the management account of an organization, the page also lists your member accounts. It shows whether each one is opted in to Compute Optimizer. From this page, you can [opt in](getting-started.md) member accounts of the organization to AWS Compute Optimizer. When an account is opted in, Compute Optimizer checks its supported resources to find ways to save.
