For alternatives to Cloud Directory, explore [Amazon DynamoDB](https://aws.amazon.com/dynamodb/ "https://aws.amazon.com/dynamodb/") and [Amazon Neptune](https://aws.amazon.com/neptune/ "https://aws.amazon.com/neptune/").
