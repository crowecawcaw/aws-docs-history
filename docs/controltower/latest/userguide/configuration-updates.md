# Configuration update management in AWS Control Tower

It is the responsibility of the members of your central cloud administrators' team to keep
your landing zone updated. Updating your landing zone ensures that AWS Control Tower is patched and updated. In
addition, to protect your landing zone from potential compliance issues, the members of the central
cloud administrator team should resolve drift issues as soon as they're detected and
reported.

###### Note

The AWS Control Tower console indicates when your landing zone needs to be updated. If you don't see an
option to update, your landing zone is already up to date.
