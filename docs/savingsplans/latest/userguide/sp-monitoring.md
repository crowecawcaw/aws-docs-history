# Monitoring your Savings Plans

Monitoring is an important part of your Savings Plans usage. Understanding the Savings Plans that you own,
how they are applying to your usage, and what usage is being covered are important parts of
optimizing your costs with Savings Plans. You can monitor your usage in the Savings Plans
**Inventory** page or by using utilization and coverage reports. You
can also set budgets for your Savings Plans utilization, coverage, and costs.
