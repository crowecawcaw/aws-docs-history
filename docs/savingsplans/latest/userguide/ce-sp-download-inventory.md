# Downloading your Savings Plans inventory

You can download a CSV file with all of the detailed information for Savings Plans in your
Savings Plans table.

###### To download your Savings Plans inventory

file

1. Open the Billing and Cost Management console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/").
2. In the navigation pane, under **Savings Plans**, choose
   **Inventory**.
3. In the **Savings Plans** section, choose **Download
   CSV**.

You can download all Savings Plans on your **Inventory** page, or
select any number of Savings Plans before downloading the CSV file.
