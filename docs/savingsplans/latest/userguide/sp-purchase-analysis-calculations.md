# Understanding your analysis

calculations

A Savings Plan purchase analysis examines your cost, coverage, and utilization over a
selected time period. Based on the analysis, we calculate what your On-Demand spend
could be if you were to purchase an additional Savings Plan commitment for that time
period.

###### Important

- The analyses don’t forecast your usage. Analyses are based on your
  historical usage over the selected lookback period. Be sure to select a
  lookback period that reflects your future usage. If you've recently switched
  to CPU-optimized EC2 instances, select a lookback period after the change
  since license costs are no longer eligible for Savings Plans coverage.
  Analyses don't account for any queued or scheduled purchases because
  analyses are based on the usage in the lookback period. Analyses are also
  generated for immediate purchases, and not for future purchases.
- Analyses at the management account level are calculated considering usage
  across all of the accounts in your AWS organization that have Savings Plans
  discount sharing enabled, to recommend a commitment that maximizes savings
  across accounts or to calculate the impact of a custom commitment. Member
  account analyses are calculated at the individual account level, to
  recommend a commitment that maximizes savings or calculate custom commitment
  impact for each isolated account.
