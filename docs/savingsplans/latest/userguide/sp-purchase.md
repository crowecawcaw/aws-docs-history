# Purchasing Savings Plans

You can access Savings Plans in the AWS Billing and Cost Management console. You can add your selection to your Savings Plans
cart from the **Recommendations** page or from the **Purchase
Savings Plans** page. We suggest that you use the **Recommendations**
page to see Savings Plans recommendations based on your past usage. You can also use
**Purchase Analyzer** to estimate the cost, coverage, and utilization
impact of your next Savings Plan purchase.
