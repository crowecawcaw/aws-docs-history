# Deciding which Savings Plans to purchase

Savings Plans Purchase Analyzer is a feature that allows you to model and evaluate potential Savings Plans
purchases. You can calculate these purchases by either using the recommended purchase amount
or inputting your own custom amount to see the impact on savings, coverage, and utilization.
You can fine-tune the results by adjusting the lookback period or removing expiring Savings Plans
from the analysis. Purchase Analyzer helps you in the decision-making process by enabling
you to evaluate key metrics, including estimated monthly savings and coverage
percentage.

Using Purchase Analyzer you can:

- Estimate the cost, coverage, and utilization impact of planned Savings Plans
  purchases.
- Model different purchase scenarios with customizable parameters.
- Compare savings and risk for different commitment amounts.
- Make more informed Savings Plan purchasing decisions.

###### To access Savings Plans Purchase Analyzer

1. Open the Billing and Cost Management console at [https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/](https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/ "https://console.aws.amazon.com/costmanagement/").
2. In the navigation pane, under **Savings Plans**, choose
   **Purchase Analyzer**.
