# Sharing a snapshot or removing snapshot permissions

To share a snapshot with another AWS account or remove an account's access to a
snapshot, perform the following procedure.

###### To share or remove access to a snapshot

1. On the Amazon Redshift Serverless console, choose **Data backup**.
2. Choose a snapshot to share.
3. Choose **Actions**, **Manage
   access**.
4. To share a snapshot with another account, enter an **AWS account
   ID**. To remove access from an account, choose
   **Remove**.
5. Choose **Save changes**.
