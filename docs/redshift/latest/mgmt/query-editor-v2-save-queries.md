# Saving and browsing for

queries

Before you can share your query with your team, save your query. You can view and
delete saved queries.

###### To save a query

1. Prepare your query and choose **Save**.
2. Enter a title for your query.
3. Choose **Save**.

###### To browse for saved queries

1. Choose **Queries** from the navigation pane.
2. You can view queries that are **My queries**,
   **Shared by me**, or **Shared to my
   team**. These queries can appear as individual queries or
   within folders you created.
