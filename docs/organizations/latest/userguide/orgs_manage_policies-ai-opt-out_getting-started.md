# Getting started with

AI services opt-out policies

Follow these steps to get started using Artificial Intelligence (AI) services opt-out policies.

1. [Learn about the permissions you
   must have to perform backup policy tasks](orgs_manage_policies_prereqs.md "orgs_manage_policies_prereqs.md").
2. [Enable
   AI services opt-out policies for your organization](enable-policy-type.md "enable-policy-type.md").
3. [Create an AI services opt-out
   policy](orgs_policies_create.md#create-ai-opt-out-policy-procedure "orgs_policies_create.md#create-ai-opt-out-policy-procedure").
4. [Attach the AI services opt-out policy to your organization's root, OU, or
   account](orgs_policies_attach.md "orgs_policies_attach.md").
5. [View the combined effective
   AI services opt-out policy that applies to an account](orgs_manage_policies_effective.md "orgs_manage_policies_effective.md").
   For all of these steps, you sign in as an AWS Identity and Access Management (IAM) user, assume an IAM
   role, or sign in as the root user ([not
   recommended](../../../IAM/latest/UserGuide/best-practices.md#lock-away-credentials "../../../IAM/latest/UserGuide/best-practices.md#lock-away-credentials")) in the organization's management account.
