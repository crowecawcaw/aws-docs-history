| [DescribeScheduledQuery](../../../timestream/latest/developerguide/API_DescribeScheduledQuery.md "../../../timestream/latest/developerguide/API_DescribeScheduledQuery.md")                | Describe a scheduled query in your account | Read         |

| [ListDatabases](../../../timestream/latest/developerguide/API_ListDatabases.md "../../../timestream/latest/developerguide/API_ListDatabases.md")                                           | List databases in your account             | List         |
