# Connecting to Adobe Analytics

Adobe Analytics is a robust data analysis platform that collects data from multi-channel
digital experiences that support the customer journey and provides tools for analyzing the
data. It’s a platform commonly used by marketers and business analysts for business
analytics purposes. If you're a Adobe Analytics user, you can connect AWS Glue to your Adobe
Analytics account. Then, you can use Adobe Analytics as a data source in your ETL Jobs. Run
these jobs to transfer data between Adobe Analytics and AWS services or other supported
applications.
