# Connecting to Pipedrive

Pipedrive is a sales pipeline CRM designed to help small businesses manage leads, track sales activities and close more deals.
Pipedrive enables sales teams in small businesses to: Streamline processes and consolidate sales data in one unified CRM sales tool.
If you're a Pipedrive user, you can connect AWS Glue to your Pipedrive account. Then, you can use Pipedrive as a data source in your ETL Jobs.
Run these jobs to transfer data between Pipedrive and AWS services or other supported applications.
