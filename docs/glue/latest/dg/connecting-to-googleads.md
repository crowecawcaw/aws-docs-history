# Connecting to Google Ads

The Google Ads API is the programmatic interface to Google Ads, used for managing large or complex Google Ads accounts and campaigns.
If you're a Google Ads user, you can connect AWS Glue to your Google Ads account. Then, you can use Google Ads as a
data source in your ETL jobs. Run these jobs to transfer data between Google Ads and AWS services or other supported
applications.
