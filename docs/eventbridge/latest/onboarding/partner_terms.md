# Partner terms

Amazon EventBridge unlocks new use cases for your customers by extending your application’s integration with AWS services. It typically takes about 5 days development time for Partners to integrate with EventBridge, and about two weeks to be added as an EventBridge SaaS integration partner, but can take longer for complex integrations. Contact the EventBridge Partner Operations team ([eventbridge-partners@amazon.com](mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com "mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com")) with any questions about the status of your integration.

To start the process, complete the following steps. 

1. Become an APN Partner. If you are not already an APN Partner, you can register for free [here](https://www.apn-portal.com/join "https://www.apn-portal.com/join").
2. Send an email to [eventbridge-partners@amazon.com](mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com "mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com") to request test access to the partner API. Complete and include all fields in the table listed in the section **Request for test access**. An AWS representative will provide an Onboarding Guide. Review the guide for a technical walkthrough of the Amazon EventBridge Partner APIs and how to develop your integration.
3. Complete and test your integration. You may not use customer data for testing.
4. Email a completed version of the **Partner integration questionnaire** to [eventbridge-partners@amazon.com](mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com "mailto:eventbridge-partners@amazon.com"). We will respond with a date and time on which we will validate your integration.
5. Validate your integration with an AWS representative. In your validation call, you will be expected to demonstrate a working version of your integration - following the process that a customer would go through to receive events from your service. Ensure you have downloaded [Amazon Chime](https://aws.amazon.com/chime/download-chime/ "https://aws.amazon.com/chime/download-chime/") and tested the screensharing functionality before joining your review call.
